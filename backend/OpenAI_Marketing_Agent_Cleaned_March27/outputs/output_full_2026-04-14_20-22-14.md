# 1) Detailed Marketing Plan

## Assumptions
- The business is an established Canadian furnishing design service with at least one showroom, consultation space, or partner dealer network.
- Pricing, delivery, and geographic service range are not provided, so the plan assumes a flexible service model with clear consultation-to-purchase conversion paths.
- The brand serves English-speaking customers primarily, with French localization optional for bilingual markets in Canada.
- The service is positioned as premium but approachable: elevated taste, expert curation, and simplified sourcing rather than luxury exclusivity.
- No unverified claims will be made about sustainability, materials, or product origin beyond what can be substantiated.

## Marketing objective
Attract homeowners, interior design clients, and trade-adjacent buyers who want stylish, cohesive furnishings without the stress of sourcing everything themselves.

## Core positioning
**Curated Canadian furnishing design service for cohesive, elevated interiors.**  
The brand should be presented as the trusted shortcut to a polished home: expert design judgment + access to well-curated furnishings + a simplified buying experience.

## Primary audience segments
- **Homeowners**: Want a refined home but feel overwhelmed by choice and coordination.
- **Interior design clients**: Need furnishings that align with a design vision and can be sourced efficiently.
- **Trade professionals**: May refer or collaborate when they need dependable furnishing support.

## Audience pain points to address
- Too many options, not enough clarity
- Fear of mismatched furniture and decor
- Time-consuming sourcing across multiple vendors
- Uncertainty about what will look cohesive in real life
- Desire for a polished result with less decision fatigue

## Key message pillars
1. **Curated expertise**
   - Every piece is selected with design intent.
   - The result feels cohesive, not collected at random.

2. **Effortless sourcing**
   - Clients save time by avoiding fragmented shopping.
   - One guided process replaces dozens of product searches.

3. **Trusted access**
   - Dealer relationships and furnishing access help streamline selection.
   - Clients benefit from professional judgment and availability awareness.

4. **Elevated but approachable**
   - The brand should feel refined without being intimidating.
   - Make the service feel welcoming, clear, and practical.

## Brand voice and messaging style
- **Refined**: elegant, visually driven, taste-led
- **Trustworthy**: calm, confident, specific
- **Design-forward**: aspirational without being overly editorial
- **Approachable**: simple language, clear next steps, no jargon-heavy copy

## Conversion strategy
### Primary conversion path
1. Discovery through website, social, or showroom
2. Strong homepage value proposition
3. Clear service explanation and visual proof
4. Consultation booking CTA
5. Follow-up with curated recommendations and proposal

### Supporting conversion tools
- Homepage hero with a single clear CTA: **Book a Consultation**
- Service overview page: explain process, outcomes, and who it’s for
- Portfolio/gallery: show cohesive room vignettes and before/after transformations if available
- FAQ page: address timing, sourcing, pricing structure, and geography
- Lead capture form: “Tell us about your project” to qualify prospects
- Social content that reinforces style, process, and results

## Content strategy
### Content themes
- Room transformations and styling inspiration
- “How to create a cohesive space” educational posts
- Behind-the-scenes curation and sourcing process
- Featured furnishings and vignette styling
- Client problem-solution stories
- Seasonal refresh ideas for Canadian homes
- Showroom highlights and consultation walkthroughs

### Content formats
- Website homepage and service pages
- Short-form social posts and reels
- Carousel posts with design tips
- Testimonials or client quotes
- Short promotional messaging for events, consultations, and showroom visits

## Website priorities
### Homepage structure
- Hero statement: what the service does and for whom
- Supporting subheadline: why it’s easier and more elevated
- CTA: Book Consultation / Visit Showroom
- Visual proof: curated room imagery
- Service summary: design guidance, curated furnishings, dealer access
- Social proof: testimonials, press, project highlights
- Process snapshot: consult → curate → source → style
- FAQ and contact options

### Recommended homepage copy direction
- “Cohesive interiors, curated with confidence.”
- “A simpler way to source beautiful furnishings for your home.”
- “Design-led furnishing selections tailored to your space, style, and goals.”

## Social strategy
### Platforms to prioritize
- **Instagram**: strongest for visual storytelling and brand discovery
- **Pinterest**: useful for furnishing inspiration and search-driven traffic
- **Facebook**: helpful for local reach, events, and older homeowner demographics
- **LinkedIn**: optional for trade partnerships and referrals

### Social content mix
- 40% inspirational imagery and styling
- 30% educational content
- 20% service/process content
- 10% promotional posts and events

## Local showroom and consultation strategy
- Promote in-person or virtual consultations as the easiest next step
- Use showroom visits to create tactile confidence and build trust
- Encourage appointment-based experiences to make the service feel personalized and premium
- Add signage and printed takeaway cards with QR codes to consultation booking

## Lead-generation tactics
- Website consultation form
- Social bio link to booking page
- “Get the look” or “Start your project” lead magnet
- Seasonal furnishing refresh checklist
- Newsletter signup for design tips and showroom updates
- Local partnerships with builders, agents, decorators, and allied trade professionals

## KPIs
- Website conversion rate to consultation
- Booking form completion rate
- Social engagement rate on visual posts
- Click-through rate from social to website
- Showroom appointment volume
- Lead quality and consultation-to-sale conversion
- Repeat engagement from trade/referral partners

## Recommended launch priorities
1. Tighten homepage message and CTA
2. Build a strong visual portfolio page
3. Create 6–9 evergreen social posts
4. Publish one consultation landing page
5. Add a simple email capture offer
6. Start local partnership outreach

# 2) Platform-Specific Playbooks

## Website playbook
### Goal
Convert interested visitors into consultation leads by clearly explaining the service and showing visual proof.

### Recommended page structure
- **Homepage**
  - Hero: curated furnishing design for cohesive interiors
  - One-line benefit: save time, reduce overwhelm, and source with confidence
  - CTA: Book a Consultation
- **Services page**
  - Who it’s for
  - What’s included
  - Process
  - Why choose us
- **Portfolio/gallery**
  - Styled room images
  - Project summaries
  - Short captions explaining the design intent
- **About page**
  - Brand philosophy
  - Why curated sourcing matters
  - Trust-building biography
- **FAQ**
  - Timing, process, availability, geography, pricing approach
- **Contact page**
  - Consultation form
  - Email/phone
  - Showroom location if applicable

### Copy tone
- Clear, polished, benefit-led
- Avoid jargon and overly artistic phrasing
- Emphasize confidence, ease, and curated results

### CTA examples
- Book a Consultation
- Visit the Showroom
- Start Your Project
- Explore Our Furnishing Services

## Instagram playbook
### Goal
Build awareness and desire through visually refined content that communicates style and process.

### Content types
- Carousel: “5 ways to make a room feel cohesive”
- Reel: styling a vignette or showroom walkthrough
- Single image: featured furnishing with a concise design note
- Story series: behind-the-scenes sourcing or client prep
- Testimonial graphic: short client quote with project photo

### Caption approach
- Lead with the outcome
- Add one practical takeaway
- End with a simple CTA

### Sample caption angle
“Designing a cohesive room starts with selecting pieces that speak to each other. Our curated furnishing approach helps simplify the process so your space feels intentional from the start. Ready to begin? Book a consultation.”

## Pinterest playbook
### Goal
Capture high-intent inspiration seekers and drive evergreen discovery.

### Content types
- Room inspiration boards
- “Before and after” pins
- Styling guides
- Product vignette images
- Seasonal refresh ideas

### Best practices
- Use descriptive pin titles
- Include terms like “cohesive living room,” “curated interior design,” “Canadian furnishing service”
- Link every pin to a relevant landing page or blog post

## Facebook playbook
### Goal
Reach local homeowners, community audiences, and event-driven prospects.

### Content types
- Event announcements
- Showroom visits
- Album posts of completed spaces
- Testimonials
- Local partnership announcements

### Best practices
- Keep copy straightforward and local
- Use event reminders and appointment booking prompts
- Repurpose strong Instagram content for broader reach

## LinkedIn playbook
### Goal
Support trade credibility and referral relationships.

### Content types
- Collaboration posts
- Design process updates
- Professional project highlights
- Thought leadership on sourcing efficiency and client experience

### Best practices
- Focus on professionalism and reliability
- Highlight how the service supports builders, agents, designers, and developers
- Keep posts concise and relationship-oriented

## Showroom / consultation playbook
### Goal
Turn interest into trust and bookings through a tactile, guided experience.

### Experience flow
1. Warm welcome
2. Brief project discovery
3. Style direction review
4. Guided look at curated options
5. Next-step summary
6. Follow-up with proposal or recommendations

### In-person marketing assets
- Branded consultation cards
- QR code to booking page
- Visual lookbook
- One-page service menu
- Sample boards or material displays if available

### Conversion tips
- Make booking easy and visible
- Offer a clear next step after the visit
- Capture contact details for follow-up
- Reinforce that the service saves time and improves cohesion

## Email playbook
### Goal
Nurture interested leads who are not ready to book immediately.

### Sequence ideas
- Welcome email with service overview
- “What to expect in a consultation”
- Style inspiration and recent project highlights
- FAQ and process clarity
- Booking reminder

### Tone
Helpful, polished, and low-pressure
