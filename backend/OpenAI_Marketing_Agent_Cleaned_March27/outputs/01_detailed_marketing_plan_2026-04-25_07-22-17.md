# 1) Detailed Marketing Plan

## Assumptions
- The brand is a Canadian boutique interior design studio with a shoppable décor store and a modest but credible service capacity.
- The website must work as both a lead-generation engine and a commerce destination.
- Inventory is curated rather than massive, so merchandising should emphasize edit, quality, and design authority over breadth.
- The most important conversion paths are:  
  1. book a discovery call / design consultation,  
  2. purchase shoppable décor/furniture online,  
  3. join email list for inspiration and promotions.
- The audience is primarily English-speaking Canadian homeowners, renovators, and design-conscious shoppers who value trust, convenience, and polished taste.

## Marketing Objective
Increase:
- qualified design-service leads,
- online product sales,
- bookings for virtual and full-service design.

## Positioning Strategy
Position the brand as:
**“Your trusted Canadian design partner for curated, shoppable interiors—bringing 60 years of expertise to modern homes through elevated guidance, designer-picked products, and seamless service.”**

### Core positioning pillars
- **Design authority:** 60 years of expertise signals trust and taste.
- **Curated convenience:** shoppable décor removes overwhelm.
- **Personalized outcomes:** spaces feel polished, not generic.
- **Canadian relevance:** local sensibility, pricing context, shipping, and service expectations.

## Messaging Framework

### Primary value proposition
Curated Canadian interior design and shoppable décor that helps homeowners create polished, personalized spaces without the stress of endless browsing.

### Supporting message themes
- **Save time:** designer-selected pieces reduce decision fatigue.
- **Look cohesive:** every item works together within a refined aesthetic.
- **Shop with confidence:** quality, fit, and style are pre-vetted.
- **Get expert help:** choose DIY inspiration, virtual guidance, or full-service design.

### Suggested brand voice
- Warm
- Elevated
- Editorial
- Trusted
- Clear
- Boutique and human

## Offer Architecture

### 1. Shop
For product-led visitors who want to buy now.
- Curated décor
- Accent furniture
- Statement pieces
- Seasonal edits
- “Shop the look” bundles

### 2. Virtual Design
For homeowners wanting expert support remotely.
- E-design packages
- Room refresh consultation
- Style direction and product sourcing
- A faster, lower-commitment entry point

### 3. Full-Service Design
For higher-value, high-intent clients.
- Renovation support
- Whole-home or room-by-room design
- Material and finish selections
- Procurement and implementation guidance

## Funnel Strategy

### Top of funnel: inspiration and discovery
Goal: attract design-conscious visitors and introduce the brand.
- Editorial blog posts
- Room inspiration pages
- Seasonal trend features
- Before/after storytelling
- “How to style” content
- Search-optimized collection pages

### Middle of funnel: consideration and trust
Goal: reduce uncertainty and show expertise.
- Service comparison pages
- Client process explainers
- Testimonials and case studies
- FAQ pages
- Product detail pages with styling notes
- Lead magnet: “Canadian Home Styling Guide” or “How to Design a Cohesive Room” checklist

### Bottom of funnel: conversion
Goal: make booking and purchase easy.
- Prominent consultation CTA
- Clear package pricing or starting prices
- Sticky mobile navigation
- Social proof near CTAs
- Free shipping/returns messaging where applicable
- Limited-time offers for first purchase or first consult

## Paid Media Strategy

### Best-fit channels
- Google Search for high-intent service terms
- Google Shopping for product sales
- Meta ads for inspiration and retargeting
- Pinterest for design discovery and product traffic

### Paid media approach
- Use product-focused ads for shop best sellers
- Use before/after or room reveal creatives for services
- Retarget site visitors with service booking and product reminders
- Promote lead magnets to build email list

## Email Strategy

### Core email flows
- Welcome series
- Browse abandonment
- Cart abandonment
- Post-purchase styling tips
- Consultation follow-up sequence
- Seasonal inspiration campaign

### Email cadence
- 1–2 newsletters per month
- Additional campaign emails around seasonal launches, new collections, and design moments

## Trust and Compliance Considerations
- Include clear Canadian shipping/returns language
- Show pricing in CAD
- Provide privacy policy and secure checkout cues
- Use transparent service expectations and timelines
- Highlight professional credentials, process, and client care
