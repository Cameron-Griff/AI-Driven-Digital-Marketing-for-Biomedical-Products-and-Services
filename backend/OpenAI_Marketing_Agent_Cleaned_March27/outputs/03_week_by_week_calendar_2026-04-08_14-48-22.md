Notes on execution
- LinkedIn organic: plan 3 posts/week throughout Weeks 1–24. Repurpose blog excerpts, video snippets, and case study highlights.
- Ads: ramp Paid from Week 10 after PH to preserve budget for post-launch momentum; continue retargeting through Weeks 17–24 focused on high-converting creatives.
- Webinars: record and make on-demand; use snippets as social proof and ad creative.
- Community posts: spaced to avoid spamming; always provide valuable content, not pure self-promo.
- Weekly review: every Monday, review KPIs and adjust next week's focus (content, ad spend, onboarding tweaks).
