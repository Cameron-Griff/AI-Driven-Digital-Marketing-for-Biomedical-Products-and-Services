# 1) Detailed Marketing Plan

## Objective
Drive adoption of an AI-powered triage experience that helps patients and caregivers choose the right care setting—emergency, clinic, telehealth, or home care—while reinforcing that the tool is safety-first, explainable, and not diagnostic.

## Assumptions
- The product is already clinically reviewed for intended use and deployed with appropriate oversight.
- The solution is integrated into a patient-facing app and/or clinic/telehealth workflow.
- Privacy, consent, and data-handling practices are compliant with HIPAA and PIPEDA.
- The primary commercial motion includes both patient adoption and B2B2C adoption through clinics, telehealth providers, and health systems.
- The brand can publish educational content without making diagnostic claims.

## Positioning
**Core promise:** “Get clear, safe guidance on where to go next—fast.”
**Differentiators:**
- Explainable recommendations, not black-box advice
- Safety-first escalation to emergency care when needed
- 24/7 availability for initial guidance
- Helps reduce unnecessary ER visits and administrative burden
- Supports clinicians rather than replacing them

**Positioning statement:**  
For patients and care teams who need quick, trustworthy guidance during health uncertainty, this AI triage solution provides explainable, safety-first care navigation that recommends emergency, clinic, telehealth, or home care—helping improve access, reduce anxiety, and ease congestion without diagnosing or replacing clinicians.

## Audience Strategy
### Primary audiences
1. **Patients and caregivers**
   - Need reassurance, speed, and clarity
   - Often unsure whether symptoms are urgent
   - Want a trustworthy first step before choosing care

2. **Clinic staff and telehealth providers**
   - Need to manage demand and route patients appropriately
   - Want fewer avoidable emergency escalations
   - Need tools that fit existing workflows

3. **Healthcare administrators**
   - Focused on patient flow, resource utilization, and operational efficiency
   - Want measurable impact on wait times and administrative burden

## Messaging Pillars
### 1. Safety-first guidance
- Emphasize that recommendations prioritize safety and escalation when appropriate.
- Avoid overpromising; the tool supports decision-making, it does not diagnose.

### 2. Explainability and trust
- Show users why a recommendation was made in plain language.
- Provide clear reasoning, symptom prompts, and urgent warning signs.

### 3. Access and convenience
- 24/7 availability for initial triage.
- Reduce the friction of figuring out where to go.

### 4. System efficiency
- Help redirect low-acuity cases away from emergency departments when appropriate.
- Support staff by reducing avoidable inbound questions and intake burden.

### 5. Privacy and compliance
- Make privacy handling visible and understandable.
- Reassure users that sensitive data is handled responsibly.

## Content Strategy
### Top-of-funnel educational content
- “When should I go to urgent care vs the ER?”
- “What to expect from an AI symptom checker”
- “How to tell if a symptom is an emergency”
- “How triage tools support, not replace, clinicians”

### Mid-funnel trust-building content
- Patient stories focused on clarity and reassurance
- FAQ pages explaining safety logic and disclaimers
- Short videos showing how explainable recommendations work
- Workflow explainers for clinics and telehealth teams

### Bottom-funnel conversion content
- Product demo pages
- Clinic integration pages
- Administrator ROI briefs
- Security, privacy, and compliance summaries
- Implementation checklists

## Channel Mix
### Patient-facing channels
- App onboarding screens
- In-app prompts and symptom triage entry points
- Search-optimized educational pages
- Email/SMS follow-ups after triage completion
- Social-friendly educational snippets if appropriate and compliant

### Provider and admin channels
- Website landing pages
- Webinars and demo sessions
- LinkedIn thought leadership
- Conference materials
- Sales enablement decks and one-pagers
- Partner co-marketing with clinics and telehealth providers

## Funnel Design
### Awareness
Goal: Build trust and educate on care navigation.
- Educational articles
- Short videos
- FAQ and glossary pages
- “Where should I go for care?” guides

### Consideration
Goal: Show value and safety.
- Product walkthroughs
- Explainability examples
- Safety and compliance content
- Provider workflow case studies

### Conversion
Goal: Drive trial, integration, or activation.
- Clear CTA to start triage
- Demo request for clinics
- Integration consultation for providers
- Patient activation prompts in app/portal

### Retention
Goal: Make the solution a habitual first step.
- Follow-up reminders
- Seasonal symptom guides
- Updated care navigation content
- Workflow optimization reports for partners

## KPIs
### Patient engagement
- Triage completion rate
- Repeat usage rate
- Click-through to recommended care pathway
- Reduction in abandoned sessions

### Clinical/operational
- Reduction in avoidable ER referrals
- Increase in appropriate clinic/telehealth routing
- Time saved for intake staff
- Patient wait-time improvements

### Business
- Demo request rate
- Partner conversion rate
- Integration adoption
- Retention among clinic and telehealth customers

## Compliance and Risk Messaging
Include clear disclaimers throughout:
- Not a medical diagnosis
- Not a substitute for emergency services
- Call emergency services if symptoms indicate urgent danger
- Protect privacy and explain sensitive data handling
- Encourage users to follow local care instructions and clinician guidance

## Campaign Themes

2. **“Clear guidance when you need it most”**
3. **“Safety-first triage for patients and care teams”**
