# 2) Platform-Specific Playbooks

## Website
### Role
The website should function as both storefront and portfolio.

### Best content types
- Homepage hero banners
- Shoppable collections
- Room-based category pages
- Lookbook/portfolio pages
- Service landing pages
- Testimonials and FAQs

### Messaging approach
- Editorial, polished, and clear
- Emphasize “curated for cohesive living”
- Reduce decision fatigue with guided shopping and simple service pathways

### CTA hierarchy
1. Shop collections
2. Book a consultation
3. View portfolio
4. Contact for design help

### Practical tips
- Use strong product photography and room context
- Add product pairing recommendations
- Keep service forms short and easy to submit
- Make shipping, returns, and service coverage easy to find

## Instagram
### Role
Build desire, trust, and brand recognition.

### Best content types
- Reels showing styled corners and transformations
- Carousels with room inspiration and product pairings
- Static posts for hero products and design quotes
- Stories for polls, launches, and behind-the-scenes
- Guides for room categories or seasonal styling

### Content themes
- “Shop this room”
- “How to style a console / sofa / bedside table”
- “Founder picks”
- “Before and after”
- “Design tip of the week”

### Caption style
- Warm, concise, tasteful
- Lead with a styling insight or benefit
- End with one clear action

### CTA examples
- Shop the collection
- Save this for later
- Tap to view the full room
- DM us about your space

## Facebook
### Role
Support trust, retargeting, and community visibility.

### Best content types
- Project reveals
- Service announcements
- Collection launches
- Blog or lookbook links
- Client testimonials
- Seasonal reminders

### Content approach
- Slightly more informational than Instagram
- Use clear headlines and direct links
- Prioritize posts that encourage clicks and inquiries

### CTA examples
- Learn more
- Book now
- Browse collections
- Send message

## Pinterest
### Role
Drive evergreen inspiration and qualified traffic.

### Best content types
- Room inspiration pins
- Product collage pins
- Mood boards
- Before/after pins
- Blog or lookbook feature pins

### SEO approach
Use searchable terms such as:
- Modern living room décor
- Warm neutral bedroom ideas
- Canadian interior design services
- Elegant entryway styling
- Curated home décor

### Best practices
- Create boards for room, style, and season
- Use vertical graphics
- Include clear titles and descriptions
- Link every pin to a relevant landing page

## Content governance
- Maintain a consistent visual style across all channels
- Reuse one hero photoshoot across multiple formats
- Keep copy aligned with the brand voice: warm, polished, editorial, approachable
- Ensure all claims about shipping, availability, and service area are accurate and updated
