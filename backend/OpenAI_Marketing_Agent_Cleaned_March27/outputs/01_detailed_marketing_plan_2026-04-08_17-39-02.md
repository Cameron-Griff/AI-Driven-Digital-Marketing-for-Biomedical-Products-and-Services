# 1) Detailed Marketing Plan

Assumptions (explicit)
- Campaign length: 12 weeks (quarterly push).  
- Budget: moderate growth budget ~ $30–50k/month for paid search & social plus creative production. Adjust as available.  
- Team: small internal team (1 growth, 1 content, 1 community manager), plus agency/freelancers for paid/social creative.  
- Baseline metrics unknown: assume current monthly site traffic 30–50k, conversion rate 1.5–2.5%, Average Order Value (AOV) $120. Targets below are relative improvements.  
- Languages: primary English; make top-level pages and paid ads localized to US, UK/EU, AU markets; currency & shipping rules enabled.  
- Compliance: GA4 + consent management; avoid server-side cookies without consent.

Objectives (SMART)
- Sales: Increase DTC revenue 20% over 12 weeks, with 30% growth for premium Titan sets.  
- Community: Grow community mailing list +30% and active community members (events/tournament signups) by 40%.  
- Events: Launch or support 12 new local tournament/PE programs across core regions in the quarter.  
- Efficiency: Maintain CAC such that ROAS >= 3.0 on paid campaigns overall.

Positioning & Messaging
- Core value prop: Durable, portable Roundnet gear — play anywhere; tiered sets for every skill.  
- Tiered messaging:  
  - Entry sets: “Start playing — lightweight, affordable, portable.” (families, weekend players, PE)  
  - Mid-tier: “Reliable play, community-ready — best for clubs and regular players.”  
  - Titan (premium): “Titan — built for intense play: pro-level durability, tournament-ready.” (competitive players, organizers)  
- Brand voice: Fun, inclusive, slightly edgy; nostalgic camaraderie with product credibility. Use playful calls-to-action (CTA) and bold visuals, but keep accessibility/inclusivity in copy.

Audience journeys & pain points (quick mapping)
- Unaware → Awareness: Short how-to reels, community spotlight, hero landing pages.  
- Researching: Product comparison pages, durability proof (real-world tests), reviews, clear tier guidance and “Which set is right for me?” quiz.  
- Ready to buy: Strong site UX, mobile-first checkout, bundled offers, free/fast shipping thresholds and clear warranty.  
- Post-purchase → Advocate: Onboarding (how-to), invite to local groups, event invites, referral incentives.

Top-funnel tactics (awareness & consideration)
- Short-form video (TikTok/IG Reels): play clips, user reactions, Titan durability tests, “first-time play” humor.  
- Paid social + search: Search shopping ads for intent; prospecting video ads focusing on social/PE audiences; dynamic product ads for retargeting.  
- Community partnerships: Local club sponsorships, PE program outreach, tournament partnerships and branded events.

Mid/low-funnel & conversion tactics
- Site enhancements: clear tier comparison matrix, interactive “Which set?” quiz, hero videos on product pages, reviews carousel, shipping & warranty badges. Mobile-first layout and <3s load target.  
- Email flows: welcome series, product education, cart-abandon, cross-sell (accessories), post-purchase onboarding and tournament invites.  
- Promotions: time-limited multi-pack discount for groups/PE, bundle offers (set + carry case), trade-up incentives for repeat buyers.

Community & tournament growth
- Ambassador & local organizer program: equip organizers with discounted event kits, promo codes, and co-branded materials.  
- PE program: curriculum PDF + teacher discount + classroom kit; micro-influencer outreach to PE/college sports influencers.  
- Events calendar & registration microsite with shareable links, leaderboards, and user-generated content (UGC) incentive.

Retail & partnerships
- Retail sell-in kit with tiered merchandising displays and product videos for in-store play demos. Prioritize big-box sports retailers and specialty outdoor stores in regions with strong demand.

SEO & content strategy
- Target keywords: roundnet set, best roundnet set, portable spikeball set, Titan roundnet, roundnet tournament kit, PE roundnet curriculum.  
- Create content pillars: how-tos, product comparisons, community stories, tournament recaps, PE curriculum. Optimize product pages with structured data (product, FAQ, reviews).

CRO & UX checklist
- Mobile-first product pages, currency auto-detect + manual switch, one-page checkout option, guest checkout, visible warranty/shipping & returns info, product videos above the fold, clear tier comparison table, fast imagery (webp), lazy-loading, 3rd-party scripts gated by consent banner.

Measurement & reporting
- KPI dashboard (GA4 + backend + ad platforms): Revenue, AOV, conversion rate, ROAS, CAC, email list growth, event signups, community active users. Weekly pulse + deeper monthly analysis.  
- Tracking: UTM naming convention for all campaigns, event tracking for product interactions, add-to-cart, checkout steps, and tournament signups. Ensure consent-based analytics.

Risk & mitigations
- Price sensitivity: emphasize financing, bundles, and value (warranty/durability).  
- Competitive copycats: highlight real-world testing & community proof and push product patent/unique design messaging.  
- Privacy/regulatory: ensure CMP coverage and region-specific refund/warranty terms visible.

Quick activation roadmap (first 30 days)
- Launch “Which Set?” landing page + hero video.  
- Start paid social prospecting + retargeting pools.  
- Email welcome flow live; cart-abandon setup.  
- Recruit 10 ambassador/organizer pilots for events in weeks 4–12.
