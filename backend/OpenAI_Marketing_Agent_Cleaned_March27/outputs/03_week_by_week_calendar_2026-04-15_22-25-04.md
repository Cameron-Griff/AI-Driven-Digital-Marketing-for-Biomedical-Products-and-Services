# 3) Week-by-Week Content Calendar

| Week | Audience | Content Asset | Key Message | CTA | Compliance / Safety Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Doctors, clinical leaders | Launch post + product overview | Safer triage and care routing with explainable AI support | Request demo | Include non-diagnostic disclaimer and emergency escalation language |
| 2 | Patients | Educational explainer: “When should I go to the ER?” | Understand care levels and avoid unnecessary uncertainty | Start guided triage | Plain-language privacy notice and emergency instructions |
| 3 | Clinic staff | Workflow brief on intake efficiency | Standardize symptom intake and reduce administrative burden | See workflow demo | State professional oversight assumption |
| 4 | Telehealth teams | Integration post | Pre-visit triage can improve appointment routing and reduce inappropriate consults | Book integration call | Note that urgent symptoms must bypass routine scheduling |
| 5 | Doctors | Short video demo of explainable recommendation summary | See why the system suggests home, clinic, or ED | Review sample case | Reinforce that it supports, not replaces, clinical judgment |
| 6 | Health system leaders | One-pager on ED overcrowding reduction | Better routing may reduce avoidable emergency visits | Download one-pager | Avoid efficacy claims without evidence; phrase as expected operational benefit |
| 7 | Patients | FAQ post | Answers to “Is this a diagnosis?” and “What if symptoms get worse?” | Try symptom check | Include immediate-care escalation guidance |
| 8 | Clinicians | Case study or pilot framework | Conservative routing with clinician oversight improves confidence | Join pilot program | Include privacy/compliance assumptions explicitly |
| 9 | Clinic administrators | Operations content | Reduce duplicate triage and improve front-desk flow | Schedule assessment | Mention local protocol alignment |
| 10 | Doctors | Clinical safety spotlight | Red-flag recognition and escalation safeguards | View safety overview | Safety messaging first; no diagnostic claims |
| 11 | Telehealth and clinics | Demo of workflow handoff | Share triage summary ahead of consult | Pilot integration | Include consent and data handling notes |
| 12 | All audiences | Wrap-up post + trust recap | Explainable, safety-first triage for better routing decisions | Contact sales / book demo | Reiterate emergency disclaimer, privacy, and non-diagnostic scope |

## Recommended weekly cadence
- **1 educational post**
- **1 short video or workflow visual**
- **1 trust/compliance asset**
- **1 conversion-oriented CTA**
- **1 reminder on emergency escalation and privacy**

## Content themes to rotate
- Safety and escalation
- Explainability
- ED overcrowding reduction
- Workflow efficiency
- Privacy and compliance
- Clinical oversight and trust

## Sample content prompts
- “What makes a triage recommendation trustworthy?”
- “How can AI reduce unnecessary emergency visits safely?”
- “Why explainability matters in healthcare routing”
- “From symptom intake to next-step recommendation”
- “How to improve care navigation without replacing clinicians”
