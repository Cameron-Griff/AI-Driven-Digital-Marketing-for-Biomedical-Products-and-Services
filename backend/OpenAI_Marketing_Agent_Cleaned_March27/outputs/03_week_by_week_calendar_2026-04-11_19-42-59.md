# 3) Week-by-Week Content Calendar

Assumptions for calendar: 12-week plan (quarterly), focused on clinician acquisition and clinic pilots in Canada with parallel trust-building activities. Owner roles: Mkt = Marketing, Sales = Sales/SDR, CS = Customer Success, PM = Product, Comms = Communications/Legal.

| Week | Channel(s) | Content type | Topic / Headline | CTA | Owner | KPI |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Website, Email, LinkedIn | Landing page + Email announce | Whitepaper launch: "AI-assisted Telehealth: Time-savings & Safety" | Download + Book demo | Mkt / Comms | Downloads 200; 25 demo requests |
| 2 | LinkedIn, Blog, Video | Short explainer video + blog | How integrated telehealth + labs reduces admin work | Watch + Book demo | Mkt / PM | Video views 1k; 15 demos |
| 3 | Email nurture, LinkedIn Ads | Nurture sequence (3 emails) | Whitepaper summary + case teaser | Webinar registration | Mkt | Email open 30%; webinar regs 150 |
| 4 | Webinar, LinkedIn, Email | Live webinar (45min) | "AI as a Clinical Assistant: Evidence & Demo" (+apply for CME) | Register / Attend / Book demo | Mkt / Clinical Lead | Registrations 250; live attendees 100; 30 demos |
| 5 | Website, Case Study | Publish case study #1 | Clinic pilot: "Saved 15 mins per consult, billing uplift 12%" | Read + Pilot request | Mkt / CS | Case views 300; 10 pilot inquiries |
| 6 | LinkedIn, Clinician Forums | AMA post + sponsored LinkedIn post | Panel Q&A with clinician champion about workflow change | Sign up for trial / demo | Mkt / Clinical Champion | Forum engagement 80; 20 demos |
| 7 | Email, App Store, Blog | App store update & patient explainer | Mobile-first telehealth — patient flow & privacy | Download app / demo | Mkt / PM | App impressions 1k; installs 200 |
| 8 | Paid Ads, SEO, Website | Targeted LinkedIn ad campaign | “Reduce admin time, improve referrals” (workflow creative) | Book demo | Mkt | CTR 1.5%; CPA target CAD 150 |
| 9 | Conference outreach, Email | Event invite + pre-scheduled demos | Meet us at [National Medical Conference] + exclusive demo slot | Book meeting | Sales / Mkt | Meetings booked 15 |
| 10 | Webinar (CME), LinkedIn, Email | CME webinar (co-branded with accreditor) | “Practical AI in Primary Care” (CME credit) | Register for CME + demo | Mkt / Clinical Lead | CME regs 120; 60 attendees; 25 demos |
| 11 | Case Study, Video, Website | Publish specialty case study | Specialist clinic: lab integration reduced result turnaround 35% | Read + Pilot request | Mkt / CS | Views 250; 8 pilot leads |
| 12 | Email, LinkedIn, Sales outreach | Pilot conversion push + ROI report | “Pilot wrap-up: ROI calculator + limited onboarding slots” | Start pilot / Request pricing | Sales / Mkt | Pilot conversions 10; demo-to-paid 30% |

Notes on cadence and follow-up
- Every demo request is followed within 24–48 hours by Sales for scheduling. Sales records lead source for channel ROI.
- Webinars: record and gate the recording behind a lead form; promote as on-demand content via email & LinkedIn after live date.
- Case studies: rotate between GP and specialist to address different clinician concerns.
- Ongoing: Monthly product updates + security/compliance newsletter to existing clinicians.

End of calendar.
