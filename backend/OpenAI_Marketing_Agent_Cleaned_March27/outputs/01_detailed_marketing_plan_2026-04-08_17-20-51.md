# 1) Detailed Marketing Plan

Assumptions (explicit)
- Manufacturing scaled and inventory managed, but seasonality affects supply and shipping windows.
- Starting marketing/funnel baselines (for planning only): monthly ecommerce traffic ~50k, conversion rate ~1.8%, AOV $160, email list 20k, IG 40k, TikTok 15k. Adjust metrics to your actual analytics.
- Monthly paid media budget assumed $40k (flexible). If actual budget is lower/higher, scale paid channels and event spend proportionally.
- Global storefronts in English-first markets; localization and ad approvals will add 2–4 weeks to campaign launch in Canada/EU.
- Product portfolio: core roundnet set + 4 accessory SKUs + tournament bundles + limited-edition seasonal sets.

Objectives (90-day and 12-month)
- 90-day: Increase direct sales by 20%, grow engaged community followers by 25%, sign 50 tournament hosts/partners.
- 12-month: Double repeat purchase rate, build an owned tournament calendar (100+ events worldwide), expand retail partner footprint in key markets.

Primary KPIs
- Revenue, ROAS, CAC, Conversion Rate, AOV, Repeat Purchase Rate, Email Open/Click Rates, Social Engagement Rate, Tournament registrations, Net Promoter Score (NPS).

Core strategy pillars
1. Product-led performance: Highlight durability, pro specs, quick setup and portability to overcome durability/transport skepticism.
2. Community-first growth: Use tournaments, local chapters, and ambassador programs to create social proof and repeated play occasions.
3. Trial & lower-friction acquisition: Offer trial incentives (demo events, campus pop-ups, rental programs) to remove learning/price barriers.
4. Content-driven conversion: Short how-tos, skill challenges, and irreverent brand stories (UGC-forward) to educate and entertain.
5. Retention via ecosystem: Accessories, bundles, tournament passes, and membership perks to increase LTV.

Target audience segments & messaging
- Families (parents): “Simple to set up, built to last — backyard play that gets everyone moving.” Emphasize safety, portability, and family bundles.
- College crews & casual weekenders: “Portable, loud, competitive — shows up wherever you do.” Emphasize social currency, quick setup, affordable bundles.
- Competitive players & clubs: “Pro-tested performance components and proprietary format.” Emphasize specs, tournament formats, official gear.
- Institutions (PE teachers, camps): “Durable, scalable sets and curriculum-ready resources.” Emphasize bulk pricing, lesson plans, safety testing.
- Community leaders/organizers: “Support to run events, ticketing, and branding.” Emphasize organizer toolkits, prize support.

Value props mapped to funnel stage
- Awareness: Bold lifestyle creatives showing social play, event highlights, and influencer matchups.
- Consideration: Specs, 30s setup demos, testimonials, and price-comparison bundles.
- Conversion: Free shipping thresholds, limited-time bundles, clear warranty/return policy, financing options.
- Retention: Membership or “Pro Club” with event discounts, early product drops, and accessories bundles.

Tactical plan (by channel & activation)
- Website / Ecommerce
  - Optimize PDPs: 15s setup video, specs, UGC carousel, tournament-ready badges, size/weight/packaging visuals, shipping ETA per region.
  - Bundles: Family, College, Tournament, Accessories Starter Pack.
  - Checkout: localized shipping, express/guest checkout, subscription or warranty upsell, one-click reorder.
  - CRO tests: pricing anchor, urgency messaging, 2-step checkout, social proof placement.
- Paid Social & Performance
  - Top-funnel: short narrative Reels/TikToks showing lifestyle + CTA to “Find a Local Game” or “Try a Demo.”
  - Mid-funnel: product demos, testimonials, comparison ads (vs low-quality competitors), dynamic retargeting.
  - Bottom-funnel: cart abandonment, urgency offers, bundle discounts.
  - Channel mix: 45% TikTok/Reels, 30% Meta (FB/IG), 15% SEM, 10% programmatic/YouTube.
- Organic Social & UGC
  - Creator program with seed packs and event exchange (creator hosts a local tournament).
  - Play-based challenges (skill move, setup race) with hashtag and monthly prize to drive UGC.
- Email & CRM
  - Welcome series, product education, tournament invites, post-purchase care, accessory cross-sell, reactivation flows.
  - Segment by buyer type (first-time, repeat, event attendee).
- Events & Tournaments
  - Tiered events: grassroots chapters (low friction), regional qualifiers, national invitational.
  - Event toolkits, co-branded assets, merch, staff support & mobile demo units.
  - Sponsor-run college circuits and summer camps.
- Partnerships & Retail
  - Retail demo days, point-of-sale displays, co-op ad funds for top partners, training videos for retail staff.
  - Wholesale bundles and drop-shipping support for global partners.
- PR & Earned Media
  - Feature plays: “How this backyard game exploded on college campuses,” community-first human stories, fitness/PE angles.
  - Seasonal product launches timed with spring/summer play windows.

Creative framework & tone
- Voice: playful, bold, irreverent, competition-ready.
- Visuals: high-energy outdoor shots, close-ups of performance components, quick how-to clips, candid UGC.
- Messaging hooks: “Set up in 60s,” “Built to take your best spike,” “Bring the chaos,” “Tournament-ready out of the box.”

Retention & monetization levers
- Subscription/club (annual): tournament priority booking, 10% accessory discount, member-only colorways.
- Consumables/accessories: nets, replacement parts, branded gear, carrying cases.
- Tournament passes & coaching packs with upsells.

Measurement & experimentation roadmap
- Month 1–3: baseline tracking (UTMs, GA4, FB pixel, conversion API), set benchmarks; run A/B tests on top 3 landing pages.
- Month 3–6: scale winning creatives, test membership pricing, pilot rental/demo program in 3 cities.
- Month 6–12: optimize CAC and LTV, expand tournaments to 10+ regions, open retail co-op program.

Risks & mitigations
- Seasonality: Shift budget to community events/campus launches ahead of peak seasons. OfferHoliday/indoor play bundles for off-season.
- Shipping complexity: Prominent product pages with delivery windows and regional FAQs; prioritize lighter bundles in distant markets.
- Ad policy/regulatory restrictions: Pre-clear ad creative for claims; avoid health claims; ensure localized privacy/cookie compliance.

Quick implementation roadmap (first 90 days)
- Week 0–2: Finalize hero creative bank (short Reels/TikToks, 30–60s PDP videos), freeze pricing & bundles, set analytics baseline.
- Week 3–6: Launch hero campaign (awareness + demo events), start paid social funnel, deploy welcome email series and cart flows.
- Week 7–12: Roll out local tournament sign-ups, creator seeding program, retail demo pilots, CRO experiments and retargeting push.

Resource needs
- Creative: 8–12 short-form videos, 20 UGC assets/month, 4 hero images, 3 product videos.
- Ops: Event team, community manager, performance marketer, partnerships lead.
- Tech: Tagging/analytics support, localized checkout integrations, ticketing system for events.

If any of these assumptions (budgets, baseline metrics, team size) differ, we'll re-prioritize channels and event cadence accordingly.
