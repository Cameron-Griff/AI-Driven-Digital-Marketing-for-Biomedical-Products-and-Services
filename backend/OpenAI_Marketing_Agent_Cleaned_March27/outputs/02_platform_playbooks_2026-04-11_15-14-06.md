# 2) Platform-Specific Playbooks

Website product pages
- Goal: Convert clinician visitors to demo requests and downloadable assets.
- Key pages: Role-based landing pages (admin, physician, staff), feature detail pages (EHR, AI diagnostics, labs, billing), pricing/tiers, pilot sign-up, security/compliance page, FAQ.
- Messaging: Hero statement that combines efficiency + trust (e.g., “Centralized telehealth and practice management built for Canadian clinics.”), role-specific benefits and a clear CTA: “Request a demo / Start a pilot.”
- Asset placements: Short explainer video (30–60s) in hero, downloadable one-pager behind a lightweight form, case study and ROI calculator on product page.
- SEO targets: keywords — “telehealth platform Canada”, “practice management EHR Canada”, “AI diagnostics telemedicine”, “lab integration EHR”.
- KPIs: Demo form conversion rate, downloads, time on page, bounce rate.
- Quick UX checklist: demo CTA visible in header, role selector to tailor content, mobile-responsive demo scheduling widget, compliance badge on footer.

Email campaigns (clinician-targeted)
- Streams: 1) Clinic administrators; 2) Physicians & clinical champions; 3) Clinic staff/allied clinicians; 4) Previous leads / trialers.
- Sequence examples:
  - Admins (6‑email nurture): Intro/pilot invite → ROI snippets & case study → compliance & integration details → live-demo invite → FAQ + pricing → final CTA (limited pilot spots).
  - Physicians (4 emails): Clinician benefits → short video + clinical workflow demo → testimonial/case study → schedule one-to-one demo.
- Creative brief: Professional tone; include 1–2 clinician quotes; short subject lines; preheader emphasizes time saved or pilot offer.
- Deliverability best practices: Use verified sending domain, small list hygiene, A/B subject testing, warmup for new IPs.
- Sample subject lines: “See how clinics cut admin time by 30% — pilot spots open”, “15‑minute demo: AI‑assisted diagnostics for your practice”.
- KPIs: Open rate, CTR, demo conversion rate, unsubscribe rate.

LinkedIn (organic + paid)
- Objectives: Build awareness among Canadian clinicians & admins; generate MQLs and demo bookings.
- Organic content types: Thought leadership (workflow efficiency posts), clinician testimonial videos (60–90s), product feature demos (30s clips), event announcements.
- Paid formats: Sponsored Content for lead gen, Message Ads to clinic admins, Text Ads for retargeting.
- Targeting: Canada, healthcare job titles (clinic manager, medical director, family physician), clinic size filters, interest in digital health.
- Creative recommendations: Use clinician imagery, short stats (e.g., “150+ doctors already using”), CTA to demo/pilot sign-up.
- Frequency: 3 organic posts/week; 2–3 paid campaigns rotating each month.
- KPIs: Leads, CTR, CPL (target CPL CAD 80–150), engagement rate.

Short explainer video & demo walkthrough
- Explainer (30–60s): Problem → solution overview → role-based benefits → pilot CTA. Tone: modern, supportive, trust-oriented. Include subtle privacy/compliance text: “Designed for Canadian clinics.”
- Demo walkthrough (6–8 minutes): Role-based scenarios (admin scheduling, physician consult + AI diagnostic assist, lab ordering and results), highlight integration points and audit trails.
- Production notes: On-screen captions, clinician narrator (real clinician if possible), show browser + mobile UI, include CTA overlay.
- KPIs: Video view-through rate, demo click attribution from video plays.

Case studies & one‑pagers
- One‑pager: Single-sheet focused on clinic admin audience: problem, solution, measurable outcomes, pilot CTA. Keep printable and email-friendly.
- Case study template: Challenge → approach → metrics (time saved, reduced claim errors, patient access improvement) → testimonial quote → pilot CTA.
- Use early adopter stats; ensure anonymization or signed approval for clinic names.

Medical conferences & events
- Objectives: Lead gen, demos, credibility building.
- Booth essentials: Live demo station, explainer video on loop, printed/digital one-pager, demo sign-up tablet, giveaway (clinical reference guides or branded USB drive with materials).
- Speaking slots: Submit abstracts focused on “workflow transformation in outpatient care” or “AI as clinical decision support in practice management.”
- Pre/post event: Pre-event email to registered clinicians; post-event nurture with recorded demo and pilot offer.
- KPIs: Leads per event, demo requests, follow-up conversion rate.

Partner clinic outreach & reseller/partner playbook
- Targets: Clinic networks, lab vendors, billing services, clinic-focused associations.
- Play: Co‑marketing pilot offers, joint webinars, integration highlight content, revenue-share or referral fees.
- Outreach sequence: Intro email → benefits + pilot case study → technical/implementation call → pilot onboarding.
- Assets for partners: Partner one-pager, integration checklists, co-branded webinar kit.
- KPIs: Number of partner pilots, referral leads, joint webinars executed.

Objection handling & FAQ content
- Common objections: Security/compliance; AI trust; integration complexity; cost/ROI; implementation time.
- Responses: Provide compliance one-pager, clinician testimonials, implementation timeline (typical 2–6 weeks), ROI calculator, free pilot.
- Create a public FAQ page and a deeper compliance/legal FAQ for procurement.

Sales enablement
- Materials: Demo script and playbook, objection cheat sheet, ROI calculator, pilot + pricing play.
- Demo cadence: 20–30 minute product demo focusing on pain points; include live Q&A with clinical champion.
- Follow-up assets: Tailored one-pager, recorded demo, pilot agreement, technical checklist.

Measurement & analytics playbook
- Funnel tracking: UTM tagging across campaigns, demo form → CRM lead stage mapping.
- Dashboard: Weekly dashboard with traffic → MQL → demo → pilot pipeline metrics.
- Experimentation: Run A/B tests on email CTAs, landing page hero messages, and LinkedIn creative.

Compliance & legal copy guidance
- Always reference Canadian privacy norms; avoid asserting HIPAA unless verified.
- Use language such as “aligned with Canadian privacy requirements (PIPEDA/PHIPA)”; make a clear “Security & Privacy” page detailing data residency, encryption, and audit logs.
- Coordinate all clinical claims with medical/legal prior to publication.
