# 3) Week-by-Week Content Calendar

Assumption for calendar: Campaign focused on an 8-week lead into peak season (e.g., spring→summer). Themes are staged: Awareness → Consideration → Conversion → Event Activation → Post-event Retention. Cadence assumes 3–6 social posts/week, 1–2 emails/week, paid ramping each week.

| Week | Channel(s) | Theme / Focus | Content & Format | Primary CTA | KPI |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | IG Reels, TikTok, Site, Email | Launch awareness: “Get Ready to Play” | Teaser reel + 15s TikTok showing setup in 5s, homepage banner, Welcome email (brand + 10% student code) | Visit product page / sign up for student code | Impressions, site visits, email sign-ups |
| Week 2 | TikTok (influencers), IG Stories, Paid Social | Challenge seed & UGC ask | Influencer short clips seeding #SpikeBounce challenge; Stories Q&A about rules; paid TopView push | Join challenge / view bundles | Challenge hashtag views, engagement, referral clicks |
| Week 3 | Site (bundle landing), Email, IG Feed | Product education & bundles | Bundle comparison carousel on site; email spotlight “Starter Pack” with video; IG carousel of unbox | Buy starter bundle | Bundle attach rate, email CTR, AOV |
| Week 4 | TikTok, IG Reels, Paid retargeting | How-to & social proof | 30s “Learn to Serve” tutorial, UGC montage from ambassadors; retarget ads to site visitors | Add to cart | Add-to-cart rate, ROAS (retargeting) |
| Week 5 | Email, Community Events, IG Live | Event sign-ups push | Event announcement email to segmented list; RSVP landing page; IG Live with local ambassador previewing event | RSVP / buy event bundle | RSVPs, event landing page conversions |
| Week 6 | Community Events, TikTok, IG Reels | Event weekend & recap | Local tournaments & pop-ups; live clips posted throughout weekend; TikTok recaps and stitch duets | Shop event-exclusive bundle / follow local rep | On-site sales, event attendance, UGC submissions |
| Week 7 | Email, IG Stories, Paid Social | Retarget & scarcity | Post-event email: recap + limited-time discount for attendees & followers; stories with countdown for discount | Purchase with limited discount | Conversion rate, revenue from promo |
| Week 8 | IG, TikTok, Site, Email | Retention & ambassador recruitment | Highlight best UGC, announce contest winners, recruit ambassadors for next season (application page) | Submit UGC / apply to amb program | UGC count, ambassador applications, repeat purchases |

Notes & execution details
- Format optimization: all ads and videos are mobile-first vertical (9:16) or 4:5 for IG feed; concise hooks (0–2s) followed by play/action.
- Localization: duplicate key assets for en-CA and other priority markets; local event pages show region-specific shipping/dates.
- Measurement cadence: weekly dashboard for impressions, clicks, purchases, RSVPs; deeper ROAS and LTV review every 2 weeks.
- UGC pipeline: permission request flow in post-purchase emails—collect rights to repurpose for ads (offer small discount).
- Contingency: if inventory constraints appear in Week 3–5, push pre-order messaging and prioritize bundle SKUs for events.
