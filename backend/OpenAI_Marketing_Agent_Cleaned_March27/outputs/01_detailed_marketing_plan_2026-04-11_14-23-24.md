# 1) Detailed Marketing Plan

Goal summary
- Primary goals: Acquire clinics and providers in Canada (initially Ottawa region), increase active telemedicine usage and convert pilots into paid subscriptions.
- Secondary goals: Build credibility (compliance/regulatory posture), establish referral partnerships (labs, billing services), increase clinician awareness via CME integration.

Core strategy (high level)
- Target high-value, solution-aware clinician audiences (physicians, clinic managers) with product-led demos and pilot offers; use content and proof (case studies, clinician testimonials, whitepapers) to overcome adoption/resistance and certification concerns.
- Combine outbound sales/PPC/LinkedIn with owned channels (website, email, app stores) and earned media (trade publications, partnerships) to drive pipeline.
- Emphasize privacy-first messaging, Canadian data residency preference, and secure workflows to reduce buyer friction.

Key value propositions to lead with (tailored by persona)
- Clinicians / Clinic managers: "Reduce admin burden and speed referrals/tests — one integrated platform with built-in AI diagnostics and billing."
- Health system admins / Labs: "Simplify interoperability and improve throughput with EHR+lab connectivity and measurable ROI."
- Patients: "Secure, convenient virtual care with continuity across tests and specialists."

Target segments & prioritization
1. Independent primary-care clinics and community health centres in Ottawa and surrounding Ontario regions.
2. Multi-clinic groups and walk-in chains across Canada for multi-site pilots.
3. Specialty clinics (mental health, dermatology) who benefit from AI diagnostics and lab integrations.
4. Secondary: labs, billing companies and provincial health networks for partnerships.

Assumptions (explicit — required because details were missing)
- Marketing budget: assume CAD 30k–50k/month for initial 3–6 month growth push. Adjust if you have a different budget.
- Sales team: assume 2–4 full-time sales/BD reps supported by 1 customer success manager and 1 solutions engineer for demos/onboarding.
- Pricing model: assume per-provider or per-clinic subscription (range CAD 150–400 per provider/month OR CAD 800–3,000 per clinic/month). If you have different pricing, adjust CAC/LTV projections.
- Certification timeline: assume limited visible certifications now; target formal provincial certification/attestations in 6–12 months.
- Product roadmap: assume stable core features (teleconsults, EHR, billing, lab integration, AI diagnostics) but ongoing enhancements.
- French support: assume English-first; French translations for core commercial/legal content to be prioritized within 8–12 weeks.
- Regulatory constraints: will avoid explicit claims of regulatory approval until achieved; focus on privacy, security practices (e.g., data residency) and clinical outcomes.

Go-to-market tactics (detailed)
- Demand generation
  - LinkedIn Ads & Sponsored Content targeted to physicians, clinic managers, practice administrators, and healthcare execs (job titles, company size, geography).
  - Sponsored content & thought leadership on healthcare trade sites (e.g., Canadian healthcare IT publications, provincial medical association newsletters).
  - Search & remarketing (Google Ads) for clinical software buyers and telemedicine keywords localized to Canada.
  - Account-Based Marketing (ABM) for multi-clinic groups: 1:1 outreach, tailored decks, executive webinars.
- Content & credibility
  - Short product demo videos (60–120s) and 6–8 minute deep-dive demos for sales.
  - Case studies and clinician testimonials from pilot sites; focus on time saved, referral speed, revenue recovery.
  - Whitepapers/analyst briefs on “Integrated telehealth ROI” and “AI-assisted diagnostics in primary care”.
  - CME-accredited webinars jointly run with clinician champions to drive adoption.
- Sales & conversion
  - Offer a 60–90 day pilot with clear success metrics (reduced admin time, increased consult volume, referral turnaround).
  - Bundle pilot with onboarding support and CME credits for clinicians if possible.
  - Lead scoring: prioritize clinics with >3 providers, prior telemedicine use, and expressed privacy/data residency concerns.
- Partnerships & channel
  - Integrate with labs and billing vendors via co-marketing; launch joint case studies.
  - Engage provincial medical associations and health system digital teams for pilot endorsements.
  - Explore reseller partners for rural/remote clinic networks.
- Product-led growth levers
  - In-app onboarding flows that encourage scheduling first teleconsult within 7 days.
  - Usage-based nudges (email/push) reminding clinicians about underused features (lab ordering, AI diagnostics).
  - Referral incentives for clinics that refer other clinics (discounts on subscription).

Messaging framework & compliance guardrails
- Core message pillars: efficiency (save time), continuity (integrated workflows), security (PHI-first, Canadian data residency), and clinical value (AI + CME).
- Tone: professional, supportive, secure, efficiency-focused, patient-empowering.
- Regulatory guardrails: Avoid claims implying regulatory certification until achieved. Make privacy and security statements factual (e.g., “data hosted in Canada; PHI access controls” — only if true). Include a short compliance checklist in all sales materials and require legal approval for public claims.

KPIs & targets (first 6 months — adjust to actuals)
- Leads: 100–200 MQLs/month (mix of inbound and ABM outreach).
- Sales qualified leads (SQLs): 20–50/month.
- Pilot starts: 10–20 clinics/month.
- Pilot-to-paid conversion: target 20–30% within 3 months post-pilot.
- CAC per clinic (initial): CAD 2,000–6,000 (depends on pilot incentives). Aim to reduce by 30% after 6 months.
- CLTV target: aim for >3x CAC within 24 months.
- Product usage: average teleconsults per clinic/week +20% within 3 months post-onboarding.

Budget allocation (illustrative split of assumed CAD 30k–50k/month)
- Paid media (LinkedIn, Google): 35–45%
- Content production (video, case studies, whitepapers): 20–25% (front-loaded)
- Events/webinars/CME partnerships: 10–15%
- Sales enablement & demos (solutions engineer time, pilot incentives): 15–20%
- Analytics/tools/CRM & translations (French): 5–10%

Risk mitigation & contingency
- Certification/regulatory delays: emphasize privacy/security and pilot results rather than compliance badges; accelerate partnerships with recognized clinical champions.
- PHI handling: ensure all demo environments are synthetic or consented; never use real PHI in marketing.
- Adoption resistance: offer concierge onboarding, CME incentives, and early success metrics to reduce perceived risk.

Required assets & immediate next steps (first 30 days)
1. Create 2 demo videos: 90s product overview + 6–8min feature demo (clinical workflow).
2. Produce one pilot offer package (terms, KPIs, onboarding plan) and pilot success template.
3. Build a landing page for clinics with localized messaging (English + placeholder for French).
4. Set up LinkedIn campaign targeting clinicians and clinic managers; prepare 3 ad creatives.
5. Plan and schedule first clinician webinar (CME opportunity) and outreach list for 50 high-priority clinics.
6. Legal/compliance review of all publicly facing claims and privacy language.
