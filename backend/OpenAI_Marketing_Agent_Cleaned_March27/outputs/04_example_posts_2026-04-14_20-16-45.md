## LinkedIn (2 posts)

### 1) Safety-first launch post
- **Goal:** Awareness
- **Caption:**  
  Not sure what care you need right now? A safety-first triage tool can help people understand their next step in minutes—while making emergency escalation clear when symptoms are urgent.  
  This tool helps guide care choices. It does not diagnose conditions. If you think this may be an emergency, call local emergency services now.
- **Media type:** Single image
- **Visual direction:**  
  Calm, clean graphic with 3-step flow:  
  1. Enter symptoms  
  2. Answer simple questions  
  3. See recommended care options  
  Include a visible emergency banner and a small “not a diagnosis tool” label.
- **CTA:** **Start symptom check**

- **Section 1:** Why care navigation matters

- **Section 3:** How explainable recommendations work

### Short intro paragraph
When people aren’t sure what kind of care they need, delays and unnecessary emergency visits can happen. A safety-first triage companion can help guide the next step with clear, plain-language recommendations, visible escalation prompts, and a focus on care navigation—not diagnosis. This tool helps guide care choices. It does not diagnose conditions. If you think this may be an emergency, call local emergency services now.
