# 2) Platform-Specific Playbooks

E-commerce product pages
- Hero experience: 3–5s autoplay mobile-friendly video showing setup and play; clear bundle selector with comparisons.
- Social proof: prominent UGC carousel, average ratings, and short testimonials (family, college club, tournament).
- Buy path: single-page checkout, bundle presets with add-ons (spare balls, carrying strap), “Buy for event” bulk CTA.
- Localization & consent: language selector, region shipping notice, cookie consent gating before personalized recommendations.
- CRO tests: A/B test hero creative (lifestyle vs. product demo), bundle messaging, urgency banners (limited stock/seasonal).
- KPIs: product page conversion rate, AOV, bundle attach rate.

Instagram (organic + paid)
- Content formats: Reels (primary), Carousels (bundle breakdowns), Stories (polls, UGC reshared), Guides (how-to & local events).
- Playbook:
  - Reels: 15–30s tutorials, challenges, event highlights. Post 3–5x/week.
  - Stories: daily during campaigns; use question stickers for UGC prompts, countdowns to events.
  - Feed: High-quality lifestyle shots, bundle promos, testimonials.
  - Lives: Monthly Q&A or live pickup game during campus activations.
- Influencer strategy: micro-influencers (10–200k) for campus reach; 1–2 macro pushes for national promos.
- Hashtags & UGC: primary #SpikeSocial, secondary #SpikeballChallenge, tag and reshare.
- Paid specs: mobile-first 9:16 reels, 4:5 vertical ads; UGC creative performs best.
- KPIs: reach, saves, shares, reel plays, link clicks.

TikTok
- Content formats: short, authentic, trend-aligned challenges and tutorials (10–30s).
- Playbook:
  - Launch a branded challenge (e.g., #SpikeBounce) that invites trick shots/creative plays with a prize for winners.
  - Seed with influencers and campus ambassadors for viral spread.
  - Post 4–7x/week during campaign peak; engage in duet/stitch with creators.
  - Use native sounds or create a branded sound to improve trend adoption.
- Paid: In-feed ads, TopView push on launch day, Spark ads to amplify creator content.
- KPIs: hashtag views, engagement rate, challenge participation, referral traffic to product pages.

Email marketing
- Lifecycle flows:
  - Welcome series (3 emails): brand story + starter bundle intro + 10% student discount with verification CTA.
  - Cart abandonment (3-step): friendly reminder + social proof + urgency (low stock).
  - Post-purchase (4-step): setup tips + invite to local event + request for review/UGC with incentive.
  - Re-engagement: exclusive event invites, community spotlights.
- Campaign cadence: 1–2 promotional emails/week during campaign; segmentation for students, past purchasers, event RSVPs.
- Personalization: recommended bundles based on browsing, geo-targeted event invites.
- KPIs: open rate, click-to-purchase rate, revenue per email.

Community events & on-ground activation
- Formats: campus demos, beach pop-ups, local tournaments, evening rooftop socials.
- Organizer toolkit: provide branded nets, flyers, social graphics, registration landing page, timing/format templates.
- Activation plan:
  - Seed 10–20 campus ambassadors per region to host weekly pickup games.
  - Run 1 signature city tournament per major market per month during season.
  - Sponsor local rec leagues and provide event kits (discount codes to attendees).
- Promotion: build event landing pages with RSVP, early-bird bundle offers, and post-event recap UGC.
- Measurement: RSVP rate, conversion to purchase, post-event social mentions.

Paid social & search
- Audiences: lookalikes from buyers, interest in outdoor sports/beach activities, college age demographics, event attendees.
- Funnel structure:
  - Top: awareness — short lifestyle reels and TikTok challenge.
  - Mid: consideration — UGC reviews, how-to content, bundle pages.
  - Bottom: retargeting — cart abandoners, viewed product pages, event RSVPs.
- Creative best practices: vertical, first 2 seconds show product in action, captions for sound-off, UGC-forward.
- Bidding & measurement: optimize for purchases (ROAS target) and event sign-ups as secondary conversion.

Influencers & ambassadors
- Tiering:
  - Micro (10k–100k): campus credibility, UGC creation, ambassador program.
  - Mid (100k–1M): trend seeding, challenge launches.
  - Local community leaders: event organization and recruiting.
- Brief: emphasize grassroots story, giveaways, discount code tracking, UGC rights for ads.
- Compensation: product + performance bonus on tracked sales and event sign-ups.

Customer support & post-purchase
- Rapid responses for shipping/parts; clear replacement parts shop and FAQs.
- Post-purchase engagement: community invite in order confirmation and instructional content to reduce returns.
- Warranty & durability messaging: highlight replacement ball program and spare parts.

SEO & content
- Pillars: “How to play Spikeball”, “Best Spikeball sets for X”, “Host a Spikeball tournament” landing pages.
- Local SEO: event landing pages with schema and localized keywords.
- Measurement: organic traffic to product & event pages, search rankings for “Spikeball set” + regional terms.

Compliance & ops
- Cookie consent gating for personalization, and sweeps/contest legal pages per region.
- Event waivers and age-specific rules for tournaments.
