Notes and execution guidance
- All patient-facing assets must carry clear safety disclaimers and privacy summaries (HIPAA/PIPEDA).
- Bilingual content: prioritize English first weeks 1–8, run French reviews in parallel and deploy EN/FR parity by Week 8.
- Measurement: ensure analytics pipeline is de-identified where required and pilot partners have data-sharing agreements in place before Week 1.
- Clinical safety: convene clinical advisory board weekly during initial 12 weeks and monthly thereafter.
- Flexibility: adjust weeks based on pilot onboarding speed and integration complexity; if integrations take longer, shift more emphasis to direct-to-patient acquisition and telehealth partnerships while awaiting EHR integration rollout.
