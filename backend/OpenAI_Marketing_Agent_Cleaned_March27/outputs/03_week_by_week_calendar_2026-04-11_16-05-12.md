# 3) Week-by-Week Content Calendar

Assumptions for calendar: 12-week launch sprint focused on pilot enrollment and producing two credibility assets (whitepaper, case study). Marketing team: 1 marketing lead, 1 content producer, 1 SDR, 2 sales reps, external agency support for video. All content in English.

| Week | Channel(s) | Content / Activity | Audience / Target | Goal / KPI | CTA / Owner |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Website, LinkedIn, Email | Publish "Security & Compliance" page + hero demo video (60s). Launch ROI calculator landing page. Send intro email to warm list announcing pilot program. | Clinic Admins, IT Leads | Publish assets; 40 website visits/day; 10 demo requests | Book demo / Marketing |
| 2 | LinkedIn, Email, SDR outreach | LinkedIn thought-lead article: "How clinics should evaluate AI diagnostics" (CEO + clinician). Start SDR outbound cadence to 100 target clinics. | Clinic Admins, Clinical Directors | 15 demos booked from outreach; organic LinkedIn engagement 200 views | Book pilot / Sales & SDR |
| 3 | Webinar promotion, Website | Announce webinar (Week 5): "Responsible AI & Faster Diagnostics in Clinics" + gated registration. Publish short explainer video (90s). | Clinicians, Admins | 150 webinar signups target; 20 demo requests | Register for webinar / Marketing |
| 4 | Email drip, LinkedIn posts | Send whitepaper teaser email (coming soon). Publish blog post: "Checklist for PIPEDA-compliant telemedicine platforms." Continue SDR follow-up. | IT Leads, Admins | Whitepaper pre-download interest; CTR >4% | Download pre-release / Content |
| 5 | Webinar, LinkedIn, Email | Host webinar (live) with clinician panel + demo. Record and publish on site. Post-event email to attendees with recording + pilot offer. | Clinicians, Admins | Webinar attendance 70+; 15 demo bookings; 8 pilot inquiries | Start pilot / Marketing + Sales |

| 12 | Review & scale, Paid ads | Campaign review: optimize top-performing creatives. Prepare scaled paid campaigns with refined targeting, and plan conference materials for upcoming events. Publish pilot summary landing page with testimonials. | Marketing & Sales execs | Increase MQLs month-over-month 25%; finalize SOC 2 roadmap comms | Book demo / Marketing |

Notes on cadence and follow-up
- SDR outreach is continuous; calendar highlights bursts tied to content releases.
- Every demo should trigger an automated demo follow-up sequence (video recap, ROI calculator, pilot SOW).
- For weeks with webinars or conference activity, allocate 48–72 hour rapid follow-ups to convert high-intent prospects.
- Owners noted per row; ensure content producer delivers assets at least 7–10 days before publish to allow legal/clinical review.

If any of the assumptions (budget, pricing, team size, certification priorities) differ, adjust the funnel metrics and weekly volumes accordingly.
