# 1) Detailed Marketing Plan

## Assumptions
- Inventory is curated and likely limited, so the strategy should emphasize high-intent products, seasonal capsules, and made-to-order or special-order options where applicable.
- Design services have finite capacity, so lead generation should prioritize qualification and booking conversion over broad volume.
- The business serves Canada-based customers primarily in English, with shipping, taxes, and service-area details clearly stated on-site.
- The brand can support a boutique, editorial look and feel across web and social, but content production resources are likely small-team, so the plan favors repeatable content pillars.

## Business objective
Drive revenue through two interconnected paths:
1. E-commerce sales of curated home décor and design-forward products
2. Qualified inquiries and bookings for virtual and full-service interior design support

## Positioning
Position the brand as the shortcut to a polished, cohesive home:
- Curated, not cluttered
- Expert-led, not overwhelming
- Shoppable, not aspirational-only
- Boutique and personal, not mass-market

## Core message
“Create a beautiful, cohesive home with thoughtfully curated décor and expert interior guidance.”

## Target audience segments
### 1. Style-conscious homeowners
- Want elevated rooms without hiring a full designer
- Need help selecting pieces that work together

### 2. Renovators and new-build clients
- Need support making finish, furniture, and styling decisions
- Benefit from room-by-room or whole-home coordination

### 3. Trade-up clients
- Already have a home but want to improve quality, flow, and polish
- Likely to buy premium décor and book design help

### 4. Solution-aware shoppers
- Already know they want curated décor or design guidance
- Need confidence, trust, and a clear path to purchase or inquiry

## Primary goals
- Increase product sales from website and social discovery
- Increase qualified design inquiries
- Build brand credibility as a trusted design-curation partner
- Improve conversion from inspiration to purchase/booking

## Value pillars
### Curated collections
Organize products into shoppable, editorially styled collections by room, season, or aesthetic.

### Founder-led taste
Show the founder’s point of view as a differentiator:
- Why each item is selected
- How it works in a space
- What it pairs well with

### Design made approachable
Make premium styling feel attainable through:
- Product pairings
- Room guides
- Before/after inspiration
- Clear service packages

### Flexible support
Offer a ladder of help:
- Shop only
- Virtual consult
- Full-service design
- Styling refresh

## Content strategy
### Content pillars
1. **Room inspiration**
   - Living room, bedroom, dining room, entryway, office, and seasonal styling
2. **Shoppable collections**
   - Curated bundles, mood boards, and product groupings
3. **Design education**
   - Tips on layering, scale, texture, and mixing finishes
4. **Proof and credibility**
   - Project highlights, testimonials, before/after transformations, founder insights
5. **Service-led content**
   - Explain how design services work, who they are for, and what results to expect

### Content formats
- Editorial lookbook pages
- Product/category page story blurbs
- Instagram carousel posts
- Reels showing room styling and product pairing
- Pinterest pins linking to collections and blog or lookbook pages
- Facebook posts for trust-building, service promotion, and local community reach

## Funnel strategy
### Top of funnel
Goal: discovery
- Pinterest inspiration boards
- Instagram reels and carousels
- Lifestyle photography
- Room mood boards
- “Shop the look” content

### Middle of funnel
Goal: consideration
- Collection pages
- Blog or editorial guides
- FAQ pages about design services
- Testimonials and project examples
- Comparison content such as “virtual vs full-service design”

### Bottom of funnel
Goal: conversion
- Strong product CTAs
- Clear booking buttons
- Consultation request forms
- Limited-time seasonal offers
- Featured bundles and bestsellers

## Website strategy
### Homepage
Include:
- Clear hero statement
- Shop collections CTA
- Book design consultation CTA
- Featured room categories
- Recent project or lookbook feature
- Testimonials and trust signals

### Product pages
Each product page should include:
- Styled photography
- Short editorial description
- Room use suggestions
- Pairing recommendations
- Shipping/return information relevant to Canada
- “Complete the look” cross-sells

### Category pages
Organize by:
- Room
- Style aesthetic
- Product type
- Seasonal collection

### Service pages
Clearly outline:
- Who the service is for
- What is included
- Process and timeline
- Deliverables
- Starting price or “request a quote” approach if pricing must remain custom
- CTA: book a call or submit an inquiry

### Lookbook / portfolio
Use as a conversion tool:
- Showcase completed rooms
- Include product callouts
- Explain the design problem solved
- End with a booking CTA

## Social strategy
### Instagram
Primary role: brand storytelling and engagement
- Reels: styling transformations, behind-the-scenes, product pairings
- Carousels: before/after, room guides, “3 ways to style”
- Stories: polls, Q&A, new arrivals, design tips, client wins
- Highlights: Shop, Services, Portfolio, FAQs, Reviews

### Facebook
Primary role: trust and conversion support
- Share project updates, blog posts, seasonal offers, and service announcements
- Use for local awareness and retargeting support
- Encourage inquiries through pinned posts and CTA buttons

### Pinterest
Primary role: discovery and evergreen traffic
- Create boards by room, style, and seasonal themes
- Pin lookbook pages, product collages, and styling tips
- Focus on searchable titles and consistent visual branding

## Offer and CTA strategy
### Commerce CTAs
- Shop the collection
- Complete the look
- View bestsellers
- Explore new arrivals

### Service CTAs
- Book a design consult
- Request a virtual design package
- Start your room refresh
- Inquire about full-service design

## Conversion tactics
- Bundle complementary products
- Offer “styled in this room” suggestions
- Add social proof near CTAs
- Use scarcity thoughtfully for limited inventory
- Provide a simple service inquiry flow
- Retarget site visitors with featured collections and service reminders

## Metrics to track
### E-commerce
- Product page views
- Add-to-cart rate
- Conversion rate
- Average order value
- Collection page click-through rate

### Services
- Consultation inquiries
- Booking conversion rate
- Form completion rate
- Lead quality by service type

### Content performance
- Pinterest saves and outbound clicks
- Instagram engagement rate
- Story tap-through rate
- Traffic from social to site
- Email signups if available
