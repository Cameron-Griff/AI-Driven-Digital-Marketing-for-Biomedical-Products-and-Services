# 1) Detailed Marketing Plan

## Positioning
A safety-first, explainable AI triage and care-navigation workflow that helps clinicians and patients choose the most appropriate care setting faster: home self-care, clinic/urgent care, telehealth, or emergency escalation when needed.

## Assumptions
- The product is **not diagnostic** and is used as a **decision-support and routing tool**.
- Clinical oversight exists for doctor and clinic deployments.
- Privacy and compliance programs are aligned with healthcare requirements such as **HIPAA** and **PIPEDA** where applicable.
- The core buying group includes healthcare organizations, telehealth providers, and clinic operations leaders; the end users include doctors, care teams, and patients.
- The product can be embedded in a patient-facing workflow, clinician workflow, or both.

## Core marketing objectives
1. **Reduce unnecessary emergency department visits** by improving first-point routing.
2. **Increase trust and adoption** through explainable recommendations and escalation safeguards.
3. **Shorten time-to-decision** for patients and doctors.
4. **Improve operational efficiency** for clinics, telehealth teams, and health systems.
5. **Position the product as compliant-minded and clinically conservative**, not as a diagnosis engine.

## Primary value pillars
- **Safety-first routing:** Clear escalation logic for red-flag symptoms and urgent situations.
- **Explainability:** Users see why a recommendation was made.
- **Operational efficiency:** Faster triage reduces administrative load and inappropriate escalation.
- **Patient reassurance:** Helps reduce anxiety by offering a clear next step.
- **Clinical oversight:** Designed to support, not replace, professional judgment.

## Target segments and messaging
### Doctors and clinical users
**Need:** Faster triage support, consistent decisions, reduced cognitive burden.  
**Message:** “Get a conservative, explainable triage summary that supports safer routing without replacing your judgment.”

### Patients
**Need:** Clarity about urgency, reassurance, and next steps.  
**Message:** “Understand where to go next—home care, clinic, telehealth, or emergency—based on a guided, safety-first symptom check.”

### Clinic staff and telehealth teams
**Need:** Better intake, fewer unnecessary escalations, less friction.  
**Message:** “Standardize symptom intake and route patients efficiently with built-in escalation safeguards.”

### Health system leaders
**Need:** ED load reduction, operational efficiency, compliance confidence.  
**Message:** “Improve care routing while maintaining conservative safeguards and privacy-aware workflows.”

## Messaging framework
### Main promise
“Safer care routing, faster—powered by explainable AI triage.”

### Supporting proof points
- Conservative recommendations with escalation triggers
- Non-diagnostic and professional-oversight positioning
- Clear rationale for each recommendation
- Workflow-friendly design for clinicians and patients
- Privacy-aware implementation assumptions

### Message guardrails
- Avoid claims of diagnosis, treatment, or replacing clinicians.
- Always include emergency messaging for red-flag symptoms.
- Avoid overstating accuracy unless validated with published clinical evidence.
- Frame AI as assistive and rule-based plus AI-assisted, not autonomous.

## Funnel strategy
### Awareness
Goal: Establish the need for safer triage and care navigation.  
Tactics:
- Thought leadership on ED overcrowding and triage inefficiency
- Educational content on symptom routing and escalation safety
- Webinars for clinicians and operations leaders

### Consideration
Goal: Build trust in explainability, privacy, and clinical safeguards.  
Tactics:
- Demo videos showing the triage workflow
- FAQ on non-diagnostic scope, privacy, and escalation logic
- Case studies on reduced unnecessary ED visits and improved routing

### Decision
Goal: Convert organizations into pilots or integrations.  
Tactics:
- Clinical review sessions
- Security/compliance documentation
- Pilot proposals with measurable KPIs
- Workflow integration demos for EHR, telehealth, or patient intake

## Conversion strategy
- Offer a **pilot-first motion** for clinics and health systems.
- Use a **clinical safety review package** to address trust barriers.
- Provide **implementation pathways** for app-only, embedded clinic intake, and telehealth integration.
- Include a **“trust bundle”**: disclaimers, escalation rules, privacy overview, and clinical governance model.

## Key KPIs
- Reduction in avoidable ED referrals
- Rate of completed triage workflows
- Time to recommendation
- Clinician acceptance rate of recommendations
- Patient completion and satisfaction
- Escalation sensitivity for urgent cases
- Integration adoption and workflow retention
- Conversion from demo to pilot to rollout
