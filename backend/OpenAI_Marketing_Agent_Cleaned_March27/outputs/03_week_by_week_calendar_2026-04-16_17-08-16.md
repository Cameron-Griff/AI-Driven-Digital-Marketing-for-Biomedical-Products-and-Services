## Weekly operating notes
- Keep imagery consistent: warm, polished, spacious, and editorial.
- Prioritize one clear CTA per page or post to avoid confusion.
- Repurpose each website asset into social, email, and Pinterest formats.
- Use Canadian spelling and references where appropriate.
- Review service inquiry quality weekly to refine messaging and qualification questions.
