# 2) Platform-Specific Playbooks

LinkedIn (primary B2B paid & organic)
- Objective: Generate SQLs and book demos with clinicians and clinic managers.
- Audience targeting: Job titles (Physician, Clinic Manager, Medical Director, Practice Administrator), company size (1–50 for clinics; 50+ for groups), location (Ottawa, Ontario, then Canada-wide).
- Content/creative:
  - Sponsored content: 90s demo clip + single-benefit CTA (book demo / start pilot).
  - Sponsored InMail: personalized pilot invite to decision-makers (include pilot KPIs).
  - Carousel ads: feature workflow (teleconsult → EHR → lab order → billing).
- CTA: Book a live demo / Start pilot.
- Cadence: 2–3 ad variations running continuously; rotate creative monthly.
- KPIs & benchmarks: CTR 0.4–0.8% (healthcare B2B), CPL CAD 80–300; demo booking rate 3–8%.
- Compliance tips: Do not include PHI screenshots; use anonymized/synthetic data in creative. Include privacy/data-residency bullet in landing page.

Healthcare trade sites & sponsored content
- Objective: Build credibility with clinical leadership; drive organic traffic and inquiries.
- Tactics:
  - Sponsored articles: “How integrated telehealth reduces admin burden in primary care” with real pilot stats.
  - Native ads linking to whitepaper download (lead capture).
  - Press releases for pilot launches and major partnerships.
- KPIs: Lead quality (higher), referral traffic, downloads.
- Compliance: All clinical claims vetted by medical/legal; anonymize clinical results.

Email marketing (nurture & ABM)
- Objective: Nurture leads through pilot lifecycle and convert to paid.
- Sequences:
  - Cold outreach (ABM): 6-touch sequence (email + LinkedIn + call) focused on pain points and pilot offer.
  - Post-demo nurture: 5-email sequence with ROI case study, pilot onboarding checklist, FAQ/legal sheet, clinician testimonial video, renewal/upgrade incentives.
  - In-app transactional emails: onboarding milestones, usage tips, CME invites.
- Cadence: ABM touches over 3–4 weeks; post-demo weekly.
- KPIs: Open 20–35%, Reply 5–12%, Demo-to-pilot conversion 15–30%.
- Compliance: Opt-in requirements; store consent records; include privacy/residency statements.

Website & SEO (owned content)
- Objective: Convert inbound buyers and educate patients/providers.
- Pages to prioritize:
  - Clinic landing page: benefits, pilot CTA, security/residency section, clinician testimonials.
  - Resources hub: demo videos, whitepapers, case studies, CME calendar.
  - Technical docs: integration specs for labs and billing partners (gated for partner leads).
- SEO focus: keywords like “telehealth platform Canada”, “virtual care EHR integration”, “telemedicine for clinics Ottawa”.
- KPIs: Organic traffic, MQLs from content, time-to-first-demo.
- Compliance: Legal footer, privacy page, data residency page.

Webinars & CME partnerships
- Objective: Build trust and generate qualified leads via clinical education.
- Structure:
  - Co-host with a well-known clinician; include CME credits where possible.
  - Agenda: clinical outcome evidence, demo of integrated workflow, Q&A + CTA for pilot.
- Promotion: LinkedIn, email to ABM targets, trade site promo.
- KPIs: Registrations (100+), attendees (40–60%), demo requests from attendees 10–20%.
- Compliance: CME accreditation process started early; ensure educational content separated from sales pitch.

Product demo & sales playbook
- Objective: Rapidly qualify and close clinics.
- Demo structure (30–45 minutes):
  1. 5 min: client pain validation (ask questions)
  2. 15 min: live product demo (patient flow + clinician workflow + billing/lab)
  3. 10 min: ROI & pilot terms
  4. 5–10 min: Q&A, next steps
- Sales follow-up: customized pilot proposal within 48 hours, technical readiness checklist, success metric targets.
- KPIs: Demo-to-pilot conversion, average sales cycle (target 6–12 weeks).
- Compliance: Demos on sandbox; confirm no real PHI used.

App Stores & ASO (patient channel + clinician mobile)
- Objective: Ensure discoverability and trust for clinician and patient apps.
- ASO elements:
  - Title: product name + “Telehealth & EHR”
  - Short description: security and Canadian hosting callout.
  - Screenshots: anonymized UI workflows; highlight booking & lab integration.
  - Ratings & reviews: encourage positive clinician reviews post-onboarding.
- KPIs: Downloads, active users, conversion to paid clinician accounts (if mobile used by clinics).
- Compliance: Privacy-first store descriptions; explicit user consent flows in-app.

Trade shows, conferences & local outreach
- Objective: Build relationships in Ontario health ecosystem and accelerate pilot sign-ups.
- Tactics:
  - Sponsor local physician association events, arrange speaking slots.
  - Host small roundtables for clinic managers to demo workflows.
- KPIs: Qualified leads per event, partnership meetings scheduled.

Paid Search (Google)
- Objective: Capture intent-driven search queries from decision-makers and clinic evaluators.
- Keywords: “telemedicine platform Canada”, “clinic EHR with telehealth”, “virtual care platform for clinics”.
- Landing pages: tailored per ad group (clinic vs health system vs lab).
- KPIs: Conversion rate for demo/pilot form 5–10%, cost per lead CAD 60–250.

Partnership & channel playbook (labs, billing vendors, CME providers)
- Objective: Drive co-sell and integration-led adoption.
- Tactics:
  - Reciprocal referral agreements; joint webinars and case studies.
  - Offer technical integration playbooks and marketing co-funded pilots.
- KPIs: Number of joint pilots, referral lead conversion rate.

Content & creative production checklist
- Always include: concise headline, benefit statement, clinician testimonial, security/data residency note.
- Use anonymized/synthetic screenshots for demos and marketing.
- Create translations (French) for high-impact assets (landing page, legal, pilot terms) within first 8–12 weeks.
