# 1) Detailed Marketing Plan

Summary objective
- Primary goal: Drive qualified clinician and clinic-admin adoption to increase clinician efficiency and patient access via a centralized telehealth + practice-management platform.
- Business goals (90 days): 1) Generate 150 qualified demo requests; 2) Convert 8–12 clinics (pilot agreements) representing 500–1,000 clinicians downstream pipeline; 3) Increase website product‑page conversion rate to 6% for clinician visitors.

Core value propositions (tailored)
- Physicians: Save time on charting and billing; faster AI‑assisted diagnostics; fewer administrative interruptions during clinic hours.
- Clinic administrators: One integrated system for scheduling, billing, lab integration and referrals; reduced vendor complexity.
- Clinic staff/allied clinicians: Streamlined workflows, fewer manual transfers between systems, role‑based dashboards.
- Patients/caregivers: Easier access to virtual consults, integrated labs, and centralized records.

Primary messaging pillars
1. Efficiency: “Save admin hours — one platform for clinical and practice workflows.”
2. Clinical confidence: “AI assists, clinicians decide — faster diagnostics with audit trails.”
3. Integration & continuity: “Lab, EHR, billing and referrals in one workflow.”
4. Trust & privacy: “Built for Canadian care settings — aligned to PIPEDA/PHIPA norms.” (assumption clarified below)

Target audiences and buying journeys
- Primary: Clinic administrators and practice managers (influencers & decision-makers). Goal: secure pilot + procurement.
- Secondary: Physicians (early adopters / champions). Goal: drive demos, testimonials and internal referrals.
- Tertiary: Clinic staff & allied clinicians. Goal: reduce friction at onboarding; secure operational buy‑in.
- Patient/caregiver messages used in clinician-facing materials to demonstrate patient access outcomes.

Channels & asset mix (aligned to publishing platform)
- Website: Product pages (features, role‑based pages), short explainer video hero, downloadable one‑pager and FAQ, demo scheduler, case study.
- Email: Clinician segmentation streams (admins vs physicians vs staff); nurture sequences with demo invites, ROI snippets, case studies.
- LinkedIn: Thought leadership, product highlights, clinician testimonials, targeted Sponsored Content for Canadian clinics.
- Conferences: Booth demo, short product walkthrough video, printed/digital one‑pager, 1–2 speaking abstracts (workflow efficiency).
- Partner outreach: Co‑branded outreach to clinic networks, lab partners and billing vendors; pilot program invitations.

Measurement & KPIs
- Acquisition: Website visitors to demo rate (target 6%), MQLs per month (target 200), demo requests (target 150/90 days).
- Engagement: Email open rate 25–35% (clinician lists), click-to-demo 10–15%, LinkedIn CTR >0.5% for sponsored content.
- Conversion: Demo → pilot conversion 15–20%; pilot → paid conversion 30–40% within 6 months.
- Operational: Time-to-first-demo < 3 business days; demo-to-decision cycle median 45–60 days.
- Support metrics: NPS for pilot clinics (target ≥ 40), reduction in admin tasks per clinician (customer success metric to capture).

Proof points & social proof
- Early adoption: 150+ doctors, 300+ patients (use verbatim).
- Measurable ROI: in pilots, capture admin-hours saved, reduction in claim rejections, time-to-diagnosis with AI assistance.
- Clinical validation: publish anonymized case studies and clinician testimonials; secure CME or peer endorsements where possible.

Pricing & offer strategy
- Pilot offer: 3‑month pilot for selected clinics with reduced fee + implementation support (includes integration to labs/ billing where feasible).
- Commercial: Tiered per-clinic or per‑user pricing with add‑ons (AI diagnostics credits, lab integration connectors, CME module).
- Contract levers: Volume discounts for clinic networks; success-based clauses tying fees to measured admin time savings.

Implementation timeline & resourcing (first 90 days)
- Week 0–2: Prepare core assets (explainer video, one‑pager, demo script, landing page, email templates, pilot agreement).
- Week 3–6: Launch website refresh, start segmented email nurture, deploy LinkedIn campaign, schedule conference presence.
- Week 7–12: Run pilot cohorts, capture case study material, iterate messaging based on feedback, scale outreach to partner clinics.

Assumptions (explicit)
- Regulatory compliance: Product aligns with Canadian privacy norms (PIPEDA/PHIPA) but may not be HIPAA‑certified. Messaging must avoid explicit HIPAA claims. If HIPAA compliance is required for non‑Canadian markets, assume additional compliance work and separate messaging.
- Resource availability: Assumes a small marketing team (1 product marketer, 1 content creator, 1 paid ads/tactical owner) + 1 dedicated sales engineer for demos. If staffing differs, timeline should be extended.
- Budget: Assumes modest paid budget (CAD 8–12k/month) for LinkedIn and conference sponsorships; if budget is lower, prioritize organic and partner outreach.
- Integrations: Assumes core lab and billing integrations exist but some connectors may be custom — pilot integrations may need 2–4 weeks technical work.
- Data/Analytics: Assumes analytics and CRM (or equivalent) are available to track MQL → demo → conversion funnel.
- Language and geography: Materials are English-first for Canada; translations for other English-speaking countries not required in initial 90‑day push.

Risks & mitigations
- Regulatory sensitivity: Avoid foreign privacy claims; emphasize Canadian privacy alignment and auditability. Mitigation: create compliance one‑pager and legal FAQ.
- AI trust: Clinicians may distrust AI diagnostics. Mitigation: emphasize clinician-in-the-loop, explainability, and provide case study metrics showing decision support benefits.
- Integration complexity: Per‑clinic technical variance can slow pilots. Mitigation: offer standard connectors and a clear pre‑pilot technical checklist.

Budget allocation (recommended split)
- Content & creative: 20%
- Paid acquisition (LinkedIn, retargeting): 35%
- Conferences & events: 20%
- Partnerships & pilot support: 15%
- Measurement & tools: 10%

Governance & reporting
- Weekly marketing standups; biweekly funnel review with sales; monthly executive KPI deck. Use CRM to capture pilot outcomes and convert to case studies.

# 2) Platform-Specific Playbooks

Website product pages
- Goal: Convert clinician visitors to demo requests and downloadable assets.
- Key pages: Role-based landing pages (admin, physician, staff), feature detail pages (EHR, AI diagnostics, labs, billing), pricing/tiers, pilot sign-up, security/compliance page, FAQ.
- Messaging: Hero statement that combines efficiency + trust (e.g., “Centralized telehealth and practice management built for Canadian clinics.”), role-specific benefits and a clear CTA: “Request a demo / Start a pilot.”
- Asset placements: Short explainer video (30–60s) in hero, downloadable one-pager behind a lightweight form, case study and ROI calculator on product page.
- SEO targets: keywords — “telehealth platform Canada”, “practice management EHR Canada”, “AI diagnostics telemedicine”, “lab integration EHR”.
- KPIs: Demo form conversion rate, downloads, time on page, bounce rate.
- Quick UX checklist: demo CTA visible in header, role selector to tailor content, mobile-responsive demo scheduling widget, compliance badge on footer.

Email campaigns (clinician-targeted)
- Streams: 1) Clinic administrators; 2) Physicians & clinical champions; 3) Clinic staff/allied clinicians; 4) Previous leads / trialers.
- Sequence examples:
  - Admins (6‑email nurture): Intro/pilot invite → ROI snippets & case study → compliance & integration details → live-demo invite → FAQ + pricing → final CTA (limited pilot spots).
  - Physicians (4 emails): Clinician benefits → short video + clinical workflow demo → testimonial/case study → schedule one-to-one demo.
- Creative brief: Professional tone; include 1–2 clinician quotes; short subject lines; preheader emphasizes time saved or pilot offer.
- Deliverability best practices: Use verified sending domain, small list hygiene, A/B subject testing, warmup for new IPs.
- Sample subject lines: “See how clinics cut admin time by 30% — pilot spots open”, “15‑minute demo: AI‑assisted diagnostics for your practice”.
- KPIs: Open rate, CTR, demo conversion rate, unsubscribe rate.

LinkedIn (organic + paid)
- Objectives: Build awareness among Canadian clinicians & admins; generate MQLs and demo bookings.
- Organic content types: Thought leadership (workflow efficiency posts), clinician testimonial videos (60–90s), product feature demos (30s clips), event announcements.
- Paid formats: Sponsored Content for lead gen, Message Ads to clinic admins, Text Ads for retargeting.
- Targeting: Canada, healthcare job titles (clinic manager, medical director, family physician), clinic size filters, interest in digital health.
- Creative recommendations: Use clinician imagery, short stats (e.g., “150+ doctors already using”), CTA to demo/pilot sign-up.
- Frequency: 3 organic posts/week; 2–3 paid campaigns rotating each month.
- KPIs: Leads, CTR, CPL (target CPL CAD 80–150), engagement rate.

Short explainer video & demo walkthrough
- Explainer (30–60s): Problem → solution overview → role-based benefits → pilot CTA. Tone: modern, supportive, trust-oriented. Include subtle privacy/compliance text: “Designed for Canadian clinics.”
- Demo walkthrough (6–8 minutes): Role-based scenarios (admin scheduling, physician consult + AI diagnostic assist, lab ordering and results), highlight integration points and audit trails.
- Production notes: On-screen captions, clinician narrator (real clinician if possible), show browser + mobile UI, include CTA overlay.
- KPIs: Video view-through rate, demo click attribution from video plays.

Case studies & one‑pagers
- One‑pager: Single-sheet focused on clinic admin audience: problem, solution, measurable outcomes, pilot CTA. Keep printable and email-friendly.
- Case study template: Challenge → approach → metrics (time saved, reduced claim errors, patient access improvement) → testimonial quote → pilot CTA.
- Use early adopter stats; ensure anonymization or signed approval for clinic names.

Medical conferences & events
- Objectives: Lead gen, demos, credibility building.
- Booth essentials: Live demo station, explainer video on loop, printed/digital one-pager, demo sign-up tablet, giveaway (clinical reference guides or branded USB drive with materials).
- Speaking slots: Submit abstracts focused on “workflow transformation in outpatient care” or “AI as clinical decision support in practice management.”
- Pre/post event: Pre-event email to registered clinicians; post-event nurture with recorded demo and pilot offer.
- KPIs: Leads per event, demo requests, follow-up conversion rate.

Partner clinic outreach & reseller/partner playbook
- Targets: Clinic networks, lab vendors, billing services, clinic-focused associations.
- Play: Co‑marketing pilot offers, joint webinars, integration highlight content, revenue-share or referral fees.
- Outreach sequence: Intro email → benefits + pilot case study → technical/implementation call → pilot onboarding.
- Assets for partners: Partner one-pager, integration checklists, co-branded webinar kit.
- KPIs: Number of partner pilots, referral leads, joint webinars executed.

Objection handling & FAQ content
- Common objections: Security/compliance; AI trust; integration complexity; cost/ROI; implementation time.
- Responses: Provide compliance one-pager, clinician testimonials, implementation timeline (typical 2–6 weeks), ROI calculator, free pilot.
- Create a public FAQ page and a deeper compliance/legal FAQ for procurement.

Sales enablement
- Materials: Demo script and playbook, objection cheat sheet, ROI calculator, pilot + pricing play.
- Demo cadence: 20–30 minute product demo focusing on pain points; include live Q&A with clinical champion.
- Follow-up assets: Tailored one-pager, recorded demo, pilot agreement, technical checklist.

Measurement & analytics playbook
- Funnel tracking: UTM tagging across campaigns, demo form → CRM lead stage mapping.
- Dashboard: Weekly dashboard with traffic → MQL → demo → pilot pipeline metrics.
- Experimentation: Run A/B tests on email CTAs, landing page hero messages, and LinkedIn creative.

Compliance & legal copy guidance
- Always reference Canadian privacy norms; avoid asserting HIPAA unless verified.
- Use language such as “aligned with Canadian privacy requirements (PIPEDA/PHIPA)”; make a clear “Security & Privacy” page detailing data residency, encryption, and audit logs.
- Coordinate all clinical claims with medical/legal prior to publication.

Notes and execution tips
- Weekly content cadence: at least 1 email and 2 LinkedIn posts per week; refresh creative every 2 weeks based on performance.

- Scarcity tactic: close pilot enrollment with limited pilot slots in W8 to drive urgency.
