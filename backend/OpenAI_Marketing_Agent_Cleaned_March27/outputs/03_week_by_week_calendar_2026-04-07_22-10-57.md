# 3) Week-by-Week Content Calendar

Assumptions for the calendar
- This is a 12-week launch-to-scale plan focused on an initial pilot cohort of health system and telehealth partners, plus consumer channels for pilot markets.
- Resources: small integrated team (growth, clinical affairs, product marketing). Budget assumed moderate for pilots (see Section 1).

| Week | Audience | Channel/Asset | Content/Goal | CTA / KPI |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Week 1 | Health system execs & clinical leads | Pilot kickoff pack (email + slide deck) | Introduce pilot scope, safety protocols, KPIs; secure sign-off | Signed pilot SOW; baseline metrics collected |
| Week 2 | Clinicians & front-desk | Training webinar + one-pager cheat-sheet | Walkthrough flows, escalation protocol, clinician handoff | Webinar attendance; clinician readiness checklist completed |
| Week 3 | Patients (pilot region) | In-portal banner + FAQ + privacy summary | Announce triage tool availability; explain safety-first non-diagnostic stance | Click-through rate; FAQ engagement |
| Week 4 | Product users & clinicians | Pilot telemetry dashboard (internal) | First-week operational metrics; iterate on flow friction points | Triage completion rate; latency < target |
| Week 5 | Press & partners | Press release + case study preview | Announce pilot launch and high-level goals (no PHI) | Media pickups; BD inbound leads |
| Week 6 | Telehealth clinicians | Case review webinar (pilot data) | Show early flows, explainability samples, clinician feedback loop | Clinician adoption rate; override rate |
| Week 7 | Patients (SEO/paid) | Symptom-query landing pages + paid search ads | Drive consumer triage use in pilot region; emphasize privacy & escalation | Organic traffic lift; conversion to triage start |
| Week 8 | Health system ops | Mid-pilot outcomes email & executive dashboard | Present early KPIs: triage volumes, recommended dispositions, escalations | Stakeholder satisfaction; decision to continue/expand |
| Week 9 | Clinicians & staff | Update training: microlearning videos | Address common pitfalls, show best-practice clinician handoffs | Training completion %; reduction in override reasons |
| Week 10 | Consumers | Short explainer video + app store updates | Clarify non-diagnostic role, show safety protocols and explainability | Video views; app installs/retention |
| Week 11 | All partners | Pilot outcomes webinar + anonymized success metrics | Share measured impact on ED diversion and operational gains | Pilot KPI attainment; LOI for scale |
| Week 12 | Sales & BD | Case study & playbook package | Publish pilot case study and integration playbook for new prospects | Number of new pilot signups; RFP responses initiated |

Notes on cadence and iterations
- Weekly telemetry reviews in weeks 1–8; shift to biweekly after stable.
- Collect clinician and patient qualitative feedback every 2 weeks; use for UX copy tuning.
- At week 11–12, prepare scalable assets: one-pager ROI calculator, clinical evidence brief, localized French/Spanish copy plan.

Quick follow-ups to prepare after Week 12
- Expand language localization priorities based on pilot demographics.
- Prepare pricing & contracting templates for accelerated rollout.
- Scale paid acquisition once enterprise pipeline shows conversion potential.
