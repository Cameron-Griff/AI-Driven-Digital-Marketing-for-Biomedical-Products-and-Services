# 2) Platform-Specific Playbooks

Website (ecommerce)
- Primary goals: Convert, educate, funnel event sign-ups.
- Key pages: Homepage hero (seasonal hero + tournament CTA), PDP (specs + 15s setup video + UGC carousel), Events page (searchable by city), Bundles page.
- Content & UX:
  - Above-the-fold: lifestyle hero showing play and CTA to “Buy,” “Find Game,” “Try Demo.”
  - PDP: 3–5 benefit bullets, “setup in 60s” clip, performance callouts, warranty, shipping ETA, cross-sell accessories.
  - Event signup widget integrated with ticket system (Offer pass discounts during checkout).
- CRO tests: Add urgency (limited seasonal color), test anchor prices, test buy-now vs. add-to-cart, reward for account creation.
- Measurement: GA4 ecommerce, purchase funnels, custom events for video plays and “Find a Game” clicks.

Instagram
- Objectives: Brand culture, product demo, drive Reels discovery.
- Format & frequency: 4–6 posts/week: 3 Reels, 1 carousel product post, 1 Story sequence/day during campaigns.
- Creative types:
  - Reels: 15–30s setup race, trick shot highlights, event recaps.
  - Stories: real-time event coverage, polls, swipe-up/product tags.
  - Shopping: tag products in posts/Stories; enable checkout where allowed.
- Community & UGC:
  - Repost UGC weekly; run a monthly “Play of the Month” contest.
  - Host live Q&A before big tourneys.
- Paid:
  - Use Reels ads for awareness; retarget viewers with product demos and offer codes.
- KPIs: Reels views, saves, website click-through rate, add-to-cart from IG.

TikTok
- Objectives: Awareness, trial aspiration, viral challenges.
- Format & frequency: 5 videos/week: 3 short (15–30s) trend/skill clips, 2 challenge/instructional videos.
- Creative playbook:
  - Hook in first 2–3 seconds (loud sound, challenge text).
  - Format examples: setup challenges (“60s Setup Race”), trick-shot series, before/after durability tests.
  - Use trending audio and stitch/duet to leverage creator network.
- UGC/Creator partnerships:
  - Seed sets to micro-creators (10–25k) + 5 macro partners for regional push.
  - Provide content briefs, brand kit, and challenge hashtag.
- Paid:
  - Top-funnel Spark Ads for creator content; In-Feed for mid-funnel product demos.
- KPIs: Views, engagement rate, hashtag use, traffic lift to product pages.

Facebook
- Objectives: Tournament organization, community groups, older demos, event ticketing.
- Content:
  - Event posts, long-form recaps, paid carousel showdowns, local chapter groups.
  - Weekly: 3 posts (event promo, community highlight, product reminder).
- Groups:
  - Launch regional groups for chapter organizers; provide moderator playbooks and event toolkits.
- Paid:
  - Use event-optimized campaigns to drive sign-ups; carousel ads for bundles for older demographics.
- KPIs: Event RSVPs, group growth, registrations from FB.

Email (CRM)
- Priority flows:
  - Welcome series (3 emails): brand story, benefits & how-to, first-purchase incentive.
  - Abandonment flows: cart (3 touches), browse abandonment.
  - Post-purchase series: setup/maintenance, request UGC, cross-sell accessories.
  - Event sequences: pre-event reminders, onsite check-in, post-event recap & CTA to next event.
  - Win-back & VIP membership offers.
- Frequency & segmentation:
  - Weekly newsletter during seasons; 1–2/month off-season.
  - Segment by buyer vs. non-buyer, event attendee, geographic proximity to events.
- KPI: Open, CTR, conversion, RPV (revenue per visitor).

Events & Tournaments
- Organizer playbook:
  - Tier 1 (grassroots): low/no fee, organizer toolkit, prize support for first 5 events.
  - Tier 2 (regional): co-marketing, paid staff support, signage and merch.
  - Tier 3 (national): sponsorship, prize pools, live streaming.
- Activation checklist: site layout, PA/music, merch booth, demo units, photographer, registration tech, sponsor signage.
- Promotion: local FB groups, campus channels, TikTok/IG Reels from previous events, email blast in radius.
- Measurement: registrations, on-site sales, social reach, new community members.

Retail partners & B2B (PE/Camps)
- In-store demo kit: floor display, quick-play zone guide, staff training videos.
- Retail marketing: joint email blasts, co-branded instore events, demo rental program.
- Institutional sales: downloadable curriculum, volume discounts, safety & durability certifications.
- KPI: sell-through rate, demo-to-sale conversion, reorder frequency.

Compliance & localization notes
- Ads: avoid unsupported health or performance claims; follow local advertising standards for contests and sweepstakes.
- Privacy: cookie banners and consent for EU; regional shipping disclaimers; data residency for CRM where required.
- Creative localization: translate core materials for top non-US markets and localize sizing/battery/shipping info.

Measurement & tagging
- Standard UTM taxonomy, conversion API for Meta, GA4 ecommerce with server-side where possible, event-based attribution for tournaments and demos.

Creative & asset specs (quick)
- TikTok/Reels: vertical 9:16, 15–60s max; 1080×1920, <500MB.
- Instagram feed: 1:1 or 4:5; Stories 9:16.
- Website hero: 1920×1080 and mobile 800×1200.
- Email visuals: optimized for quick load (<200KB per image).
