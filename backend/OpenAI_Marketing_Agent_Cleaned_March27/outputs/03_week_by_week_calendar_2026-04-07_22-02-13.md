Quick 2-week micro-content cadence (repeatable)
- Week A: Publish one symptom explainer + one short video (30–60s) for clinic staff.
- Week B: Publish one FAQ/update on safety/explainability + outreach email to 20 new prospects.

Sample short content snippets (ready to use)
- Social (consumer): “Not sure if you should go to the ER? Our explainable triage guides you to the right care — 24/7, clinician-reviewed. Start here: [link]”
- Clinic outreach subject line: “Reduce non-urgent ED visits — pilot our explainable triage at no cost”
- Landing hero: “Get safe, explainable guidance on where to get care — fast. Start triage.”

Measurement checkpoint cadence
- Weekly: funnel metrics (starts, completions, referral clicks), ad performance, pilot engagement.
- Monthly: clinical outcomes from pilots, NPS/CSAT, integration adoption.
- Quarterly: ROI and ED avoidance reported to partners and stakeholders.
