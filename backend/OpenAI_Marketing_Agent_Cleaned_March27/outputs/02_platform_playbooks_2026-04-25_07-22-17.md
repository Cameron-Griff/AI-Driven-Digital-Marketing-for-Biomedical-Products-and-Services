# 2) Platform-Specific Playbooks

## Website Playbook

### Primary role
Convert visitors into shoppers or leads by making the brand feel editorial, expert, and easy to navigate.

### Homepage structure
1. Hero with clear value proposition  
   Example: “Curated Canadian interiors, shoppable décor, and expert design support.”
2. Primary CTA: Shop the Collection
3. Secondary CTA: Book a Consultation
4. Featured services
5. Best-selling or seasonal products
6. Design portfolio highlights
7. Testimonials
8. Brand story / 60 years of expertise
9. Email signup

### Homepage copy priorities
- Spell out what the brand does within 5 seconds
- Make the shop and services equally visible
- Emphasize trust and ease

### Service page recommendations
For each service page include:
- Who it’s for
- What’s included
- Timeline
- Deliverables
- Starting price or “from” pricing if possible
- FAQs
- CTA to book

### Product page recommendations
- Strong product photography
- Styled-in-room imagery
- Short editorial description
- Dimensions, materials, care, shipping details
- “Why we selected it” note
- Cross-sells: “Pairs well with…”
- Prominent add-to-cart button

### UX priorities
- Sticky mobile CTA
- Search and filter by room, style, colour, price
- Easy access to shipping/returns
- Reduce page clutter
- Use scannable sections and accordions

## Online Shop Playbook

### Merchandising strategy
Curate by outcome, not just category:
- “Warm modern living”
- “Quiet luxury accents”
- “Entryway refresh”
- “Small-space solutions”
- “Entertaining essentials”

### Collection strategy
Create editorial collections that feel designed:
- Seasonal edits
- Room-based edits
- Style-based edits
- Gift guides
- New homeowner essentials

### High-converting commerce tactics
- Bundle products into “shop the look” sets
- Feature limited quantities honestly
- Add “designer pick” badges
- Use social proof where available
- Highlight Canadian shipping and expected delivery times

### Cross-sell and upsell ideas
- Rug + cushion + lamp combinations
- Dining table + centerpiece + lighting
- Bedside table + lamp + bedding accents

## Content Marketing Playbook

### Blog/editorial categories
- Decorating advice
- Room styling
- Renovation planning
- Material and finish guidance
- Product edits
- Design trends with longevity
- Client stories and transformations

### Content format mix
- Short how-to articles
- Long-form guides
- Inspiring visual stories
- Before/after case studies
- “Top 5” product roundups
- Seasonal trend pieces

### Editorial tone
- Expert but approachable
- Polished, not overly technical
- Aspirational, but practical
- Helpful for overwhelmed readers

### Best-performing content themes
- “How to make a room feel finished”
- “What to buy first when furnishing a home”
- “Designer tips for cohesive spaces”
- “How to style shelves and consoles”
- “Timeless décor investments”

## Service Sales Playbook

### For virtual design
Best for:
- Busy homeowners
- Budget-conscious clients
- Remote clients
- Smaller projects

### Conversion message
“Professional design direction from the comfort of home—ideal if you want a cohesive plan, curated product selections, and confidence before you buy.”

### For full-service design
Best for:
- Larger homes
- Renovation clients
- High-intent homeowners seeking end-to-end support

### Conversion message
“Hands-on design support for projects that deserve a thoughtful, polished result—from concept to completion.”

### Lead qualification questions
- Project scope
- Timeline
- Location
- Budget range
- Design goals
- Current pain points
