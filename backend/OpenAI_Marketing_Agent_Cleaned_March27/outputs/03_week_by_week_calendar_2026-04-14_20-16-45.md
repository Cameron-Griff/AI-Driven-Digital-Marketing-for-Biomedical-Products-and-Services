## Weekly publishing rhythm
- **2 patient-facing posts** per week
- **1 trust/compliance asset** per week
- **1 partner-facing B2B asset** per week
- **1 product demo or workflow asset** every two weeks

## Suggested content mix by audience
- **Patients:** symptom guidance, reassurance, urgency education
- **Caregivers:** “what to do if unsure” content
- **Clinic staff:** workflow and intake materials
- **Telehealth providers:** booking and handoff optimization
- **Administrators:** operational impact, compliance, and pilot ROI

## Execution notes
- Reuse the same core safety language across all channels for consistency.
- Localize only after legal and clinical review for the target region.
- Keep every asset aligned with triage-only scope and escalation pathways.
