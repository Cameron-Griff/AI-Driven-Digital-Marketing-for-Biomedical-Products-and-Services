  10. Next steps: request pilot, schedule demo, contact clinical affairs
- Short intro paragraph:
  Hospitals and clinics need a safer way to reduce avoidable emergency visits while preserving clinician oversight. Our explainable, non-diagnostic triage layer routes patients to the right next step—self-care, primary care, urgent care, or immediate emergency—while providing transparent rationale, clinician handoffs, and measurable pilot dashboards to track ED diversion and safety outcomes.
