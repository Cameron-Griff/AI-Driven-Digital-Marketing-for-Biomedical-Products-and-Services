### Outline
**Title:** When should I go to ER, clinic, or home care?

1. **Introduction**
   - Why care navigation is hard
   - Who this guide is for: patients and caregivers in Canada

2. **First rule: know the urgent symptoms**
   - When to seek emergency care immediately
   - Clear escalation language
   - Why urgent symptoms should not wait

3. **Clinic vs emergency care**
   - When a clinic or telehealth visit may be appropriate
   - When symptoms need faster in-person assessment
   - What to consider if symptoms are worsening

4. **Home care and self-care basics**
   - Mild issues that may be manageable at home
   - When to monitor and when to re-check
   - When to get help if things change

5. **How digital triage helps**
   - Guides next steps
   - Uses plain language
   - Supports safety-first routing without diagnosing

6. **Privacy, trust, and limitations**
   - Non-diagnostic clarification
   - Privacy-conscious design
   - Canada-first healthcare context

7. **Conclusion**
   - Reassurance and next-step framing
   - CTA to check symptoms or learn more

### Short intro paragraph
Not sure whether you should go to the ER, book a clinic visit, or manage symptoms at home? You’re not alone. This guide is designed to help patients and caregivers think through the next step safely, with clear escalation for urgent symptoms and simple, plain-language guidance. It does not diagnose or replace clinical care, but it can help you get to the right level of care faster.
