## Publishing rhythm
- **Weekly**
  - 1 blog post
  - 2 LinkedIn posts
  - 1 email
  - 1 short-form video or interactive demo update
- **Biweekly**
  - 1 healthcare buyer-facing thought leadership piece
  - 1 compliance/trust content asset
- **Monthly**
  - 1 downloadable guide or whitepaper
  - 1 pilot-oriented webinar or live demo

## Content guardrails for every week
- Avoid diagnosis language
- Use “may indicate,” “helps guide,” or “supports care navigation”
- Include immediate emergency instructions for red-flag symptoms
- Keep privacy language clear and compliant
- Ensure medically responsible review before publication
