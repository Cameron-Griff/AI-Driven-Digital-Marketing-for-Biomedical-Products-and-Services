## Website/Blog (1 outline + 1 short intro paragraph)

### Outline
**Title:** Explainable AI Triage: Safer Care Routing Without Replacing Clinical Judgment

1. **Intro**
   - Why care routing matters
   - The problem with delayed or inappropriate escalation

2. **What explainable AI triage is**
   - Decision-support and routing, not diagnosis
   - Home, clinic, telehealth, ED recommendation framework

3. **Why safety-first design matters**
   - Red-flag symptoms and escalation logic
   - Conservative recommendations and professional oversight

4. **How explainability builds trust**
   - Clear rationale for recommendations
   - Better adoption for clinicians, staff, and patients

5. **Workflow use cases**
   - Patient intake
   - Clinic triage
   - Telehealth pre-visit screening
   - Clinical summary and handoff

6. **Privacy and compliance considerations**
   - HIPAA/PIPEDA-aligned assumptions
   - Consent, privacy notice, and governance

7. **Operational benefits**
   - Faster time-to-decision
   - Fewer unnecessary ED referrals
   - Streamlined intake and routing

8. **FAQ**
   - Is this a diagnosis tool?
   - What happens if symptoms worsen?
   - How does escalation work?

9. **Call to action**
   - Demo, pilot, or workflow review

### Short intro paragraph
When patients aren’t sure where to go next, delays and uncertainty can lead to unnecessary emergency visits or missed escalation. Explainable AI triage offers a safety-first way to support care routing by helping clinicians, care teams, and patients choose the most appropriate next step—while remaining explicitly non-diagnostic and aligned with urgent-care escalation when needed.
