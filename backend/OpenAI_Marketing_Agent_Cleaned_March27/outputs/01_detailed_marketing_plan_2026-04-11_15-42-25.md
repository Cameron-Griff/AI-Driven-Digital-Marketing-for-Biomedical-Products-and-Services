# 1) Detailed Marketing Plan

Assumptions (explicit)
- Regulatory/certification status is incomplete: we assume provincial compliance (PHIPA/PIPEDA) is required and that the product will seek formal provincial approvals or third-party SOC/ISO audits within 6–12 months.
- Some key features require authenticated clinician login and lab integrations; assume demos and pilot access must satisfy identity verification (medical license + clinic domain/email).
- Pricing model: assume a freemium/paid SaaS for clinics (free basic account for signup + paid billing/integrations), plus referral-network commission or subscription for referral activation. If your pricing differs, adjust pilot/promotional tactics accordingly.
- Target launch window for the campaign: 12 weeks to initial traction with ongoing sustainment thereafter.
- Resources: small cross-functional team (1 growth marketer, 1 content producer, 1 account executive, 1 partnerships/BD rep), modest paid budget for LinkedIn and conference presence.

Primary Goals (90-day & 12-month)
- 90-day: Acquire X clinics/doctors (example target: 50 clinics / 200 clinicians signed up), generate 300 qualified demo requests, activate 10 lab partnerships pilot, and convert 20% of demoed clinics to paid billing trials.
- 12-month: Scale to 500 clinics, 30% paid-billing adoption among active clinics, and a live referral network with 200 active referral endpoints.
- KPIs: demo requests, clinician signups (authenticated), billing-module activations, referral links/acceptance rate, MQL→SQL conversion, CAC, LTV projection, churn.

Target Segments & Prioritization
- Tier 1 (high priority): Family physicians and small group clinics (easier to deploy, high volume referrals).
- Tier 2: Specialty clinics (cardiology, endocrinology) that benefit from lab integrations and ML diagnostics.
- Tier 3: Clinic administrators & billing staff (decision influencers).
- Secondary stakeholders: provincial health networks and lab partners for distribution/validation.

Positioning & Messaging Pillars (by persona)
- Family Physicians: "Streamline your clinical day — secure telemedicine, integrated labs, faster diagnostics, less admin." Emphasize time savings, billing efficiency, and PHIPA-compliant data residency.
- Specialists: "Accurate triage & faster results with ML-driven decision support + streamlined referrals." Emphasize clinical credibility and CME content.
- Clinic Admin / Billing Staff: "Reduce rejected claims, automate billing workflows, measurable ROI within 90 days." Emphasize integration and lower admin cost.
- Labs / Partners: "Plug into a clinician network that drives higher test volumes and seamless digital orders."
- Patients (supporting messaging): "Secure, timely care with coordinated lab results and easy messaging."

Value propositions to emphasize repeatedly
- Centralized telemedicine + EHR + billing + lab integration in one secure platform.
- AI/ML diagnostics to speed triage and improve decision-making.
- Referral network and CME content that drives clinician engagement and loyalty.
- Canadian data residency and privacy-forward operations (explicitly mention PHIPA/PIPEDA compliance efforts).

Acquisition Strategy — High-Level Mix
- Inbound Content & Thought Leadership (40%): Whitepapers, clinician case studies, CME webinars, explainer and demo videos to educate & build credibility.
- Outbound & Targeted Outreach (30%): Highly segmented email campaigns to clinicians, provincial health engagement, conference follow-ups, and targeted LinkedIn ads.
- Product-led & Sales Motion (20%): Gated clinician signup, verified demo scheduling, 30–90 day pilot programs for clinics with pricing incentives for early adopters.
- Partnerships & PR (10%): Lab integrations, provincial networks, industry press and conference speaking slots to validate product & open doors.

Funnels & Conversion Pathways
- Awareness → Interest: LinkedIn posts/ads, press pieces, conference presence, SEO for queries (telemedicine EHR Canada, integrated billing for clinics).
- Interest → Consideration: Whitepapers, clinic-focused ROI calculator, on-demand demo videos; gated clinician-only assets (CME credits) requiring verification.
- Consideration → Trial/Pilot: Live webinar with Q&A, 1:1 product demo, 6–12 week pilot with success metrics & onboarding support.
- Trial → Paid: Billing module activation discount within first 60 days, training + fee-for-service recovery guarantee (or SLA), case study co-op for pilot participants.
- Referral Activation: Provide referral tools in-platform + revenue share or credits for referring clinicians and labs.

Tactical Plays (actionable)
- Gated CME Webinar Series: Monthly accredited webinars that double as lead-generation funnels. Require clinician verification for CME credit; use to demonstrate AI diagnostics and billing workflows.
- Pilot Program with ROI Guarantee: 8–12 week pilot for early adopters measuring time-saved, claim recovery improvements, patient throughput; offer discounted onboarding and billing fee waiver for first 3 months.
- Case-Study Recruitment & Amplification: Convert pilots into documented case studies with measurable outcomes (time saved, revenue uplift). Promote via website, LinkedIn, and emails.
- Demo + Authentication Flow: Streamline demo scheduling — require medical license number and clinic verification step; offer sandbox clinician accounts showcasing full workflow with dummy patient data.
- Landing Pages Optimized for Clinicians: Clear CTA variants (Request Demo, Start Pilot, Download Whitepaper, Register for CME). Include security/compliance badge placeholders, lab integration list, and quick ROI snapshot.
- Targeted Email Cadence: 6-touch sequence per persona: intro → demo invite → whitepaper → webinar invite (CME) → pilot offer → close/feedback. Use personalization fields (specialty, province).
- LinkedIn Ads + Organic: Use Sponsored InMail for clinic admin outreach, lead gen forms for demo signups, carousel ads that highlight product features and CME.
- Conference Strategy: Secure speaking slots or panel participation about telemedicine efficiency and AI diagnostics; host a booth for demos and live signups with incentive (free 3-month billing trial).
- Provincial Health Network Outreach: Co-marketing with provincial organizations (e.g., digital health bodies) to run webinars and pilot cohorts; seek joint funding or grants where possible.
- PR & Analyst Outreach: Target healthcare IT trade press and Canadian industry outlets with product launch narratives emphasizing patient safety, ML validation approach, and partnership pilots.

Onboarding & Activation
- Fast-start checklist for clinics: 1) Verify clinician accounts; 2) Connect lab integrations (or select partner lab); 3) Configure billing settings; 4) Run a shadow week with support; 5) Activate live patients.
- Enablement assets: Quick-start video series, billing cheat-sheet, admin setup guide, step-by-step lab ordering walkthrough.
- Success measurement: Weekly activation dashboard for each pilot (appointments, labs ordered, billing submissions, claim acceptance rate, time saved per clinician).

Regulatory & Trust Building
- Immediate actions: Publish privacy and data residency page; create compliance roadmap publicly; initiate third-party security audit (SOC2 or ISO27001) and publish summary findings.
- Longer-term: Seek provincial approvals or endorsements and display partner logos (labs, provincial bodies) once contracts signed.
- Clinical credibility: Advisory board of clinicians and a published whitepaper on ML validation and clinical accuracy methodology.

Budget & Resource Allocation (example split)
- Content & webinars: 25%
- Paid LinkedIn ads & retargeting: 30%
- Conferences & travel: 20%
- Partnerships & BD (partner enablement): 15%
- Tools & third-party audits: 10%

Measurement & Iteration
- Weekly dashboard: demo requests, demo-to-trial conversion, trial-to-paid conversion, active clinician count, billing module adoption, referral activations.
- Monthly growth experiments: A/B test landing pages, email subject lines, webinar formats (live vs on-demand), and pilot incentive structures.
- Quarterly review: Adjust targeting, content themes, and budget allocation based on CAC and conversion velocity.

Risks & Mitigations
- Risk: Clinician trust and compliance concerns. Mitigation: Publish compliance roadmap, show third-party audit plans, and use clinician advisors for content & PR.
- Risk: Integration delays with labs/EHRs. Mitigation: Offer staged onboarding (start with telemedicine + EHR core; enable lab integrations as they come online) and promote partner labs first.
- Risk: Long sales cycles. Mitigation: Emphasize product-led onboarding for small clinics and create fast, measurable pilots to shorten decision time.
