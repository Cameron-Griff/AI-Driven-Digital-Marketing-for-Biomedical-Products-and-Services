# 2) Platform-Specific Playbooks

Canada .ca website (desktop & responsive)
- Role: Main conversion hub and canonical product information source.
- Key pages: Landing/Hero, Product detail pages (PDPs), comparison charts, trade-in estimator, AppleCare purchase page, support/FAQ, demo booking.
- Tactics:
  - Hero film & dynamic banner swapped by language and location (province-specific promotions).
  - PDP modular: feature row (benefit-led), specs, high-fidelity A/V, comparison matrix, trade-in & financing widget, AppleCare upsell module.
  - Add “Why privacy matters” expandable micro-site content (EN/FR) with third-party verification badges.
  - Implement one-click demo booking and calendar integration; show nearest stores with demo availability.
- Creative & copy:
  - Cinematic hero (60s) + micro-cut 15–30s assets.
  - Product-led headlines: “Seamless across devices. Designed for life in Canada.”
  - French copy must be native; maintain identical UX.
- Measurement:
  - Track funnel steps: view PDP → trade-in estimator → add-to-cart → AppleCare add → purchase.
  - Use conversion rate, time-on-page for hero, trade-in conversion, AOV.
- Tests:
  - PDP hero variant: lifestyle vs. feature-led.
  - Checkout flow: single upsell modal vs. inline AppleCare checkbox.

Apple Store app
- Role: Mobile-first checkout, push notifications, in-app demos, easy trade-in flows.
- Tactics:
  - Push campaign: targeted push for cart abandoners and trade-in offers.
  - In-app banners for curated subscription offers and limited-time AppleCare discounts.
  - Leveraged deep links from emails and social to direct to in-app checkout.
- Creative:
  - Short vertical videos (6–15s) optimized for mobile.
  - Clear microcopy for financing and trade-in acceptance.
- Measurement:
  - App install-to-purchase funnel, push open-to-purchase rates, AppleCare attach rate inside app.

Retail stores & in-store experience
- Role: High-touch conversion channel; demo-first to overcome hesitation and showcase interoperability and AV fidelity.
- Tactics:
  - In-store demos: scheduled and walk-in immersive demos with curated scenes (music, film, home/office workflows).
  - “Trade-in kiosks” and instant valuation tools with trained staff scripts (privacy for trade-in data).
  - AppleCare demo counter with cost-of-repair comparisons.
  - Localized hero displays and QR codes linking to PDP and demo booking.
  - In-store events: “Creative Night” or student-targeted demo events with partner universities and local creators.
- Staff training:
  - Standardized demo script, objection-handling playbook for cost and lock-in concerns, bilingual customer engagement.
- Metrics:
  - Demo-to-purchase conversion, attach rates for AppleCare, trade-in uptake, average sale size, NPS for in-store experience.

Email (CRM)
- Role: Personalisation & lifecycle nurturing — move high-intent prospects to purchase and convert purchasers to subscriptions/AppleCare.
- Segments & sequences:
  - Cart abandoners: timed reminders + trade-in estimate + 1-click purchase link.
  - Trade-in lapsed owners: valuation reminder + upgrade incentive.
  - New device purchasers: onboarding series with AppleCare upsell, subscription trial offers.
  - High-value prospects: limited-time bundle offers & book-a-demo invites.
- Creative & cadence:
  - Triggered flows: immediate abandon, 48-72h follow-up, 7-day final offer.
  - Bilingual templates, accessible HTML email patterns, and plain-text fallback.
- Tests:
  - Email subject lines (aspirational vs. functional), CTA placement for AppleCare.
- Measurement:
  - Open rate, click rate, conversion from email, lift in AppleCare attach.

Social channels (Organic + Paid)
- Platforms: Instagram, Facebook, X, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn (for SMB/enterprise).
- Role: Awareness & consideration; drive to PDP, demo booking and in-store events.
- Tactics:
  - Hero campaign on YouTube + OLV: 60s film + 15/30s cuts.
  - Short-form social: 6–15s verticals for reels/stories/TikTok showing interoperability moments.
  - Product feature breakdowns (carousel) and comparison posts.
  - Creator partnerships: Canadian creators (EN/FR) showing real workflows and privacy storytelling.
  - Paid targeting: retarget site visitors, lookalikes of purchasers, student audiences, creative professionals.
  - Geotargeting: ads earmarked for provinces/stores with inventory availability.
- Creative specs:
  - Vertical video 9:16 for Reels/TikTok; 1:1 and 16:9 for feed and YouTube.
  - Copy: aspirational lead + 1–2 feature bullets + strong CTA.
- Measurement:
  - View-through rate, click-through, micro-conversions (demo booking), purchase ROAS.

Support & content (post-purchase and research stage)
- Role: Reduce barriers (repair cost fear, lock-in concerns), support conversions for AppleCare & subscriptions.
- Tactics:
  - Create comparison charts showing cost-savings with AppleCare, trade-in environmental benefits.
  - Publish localized privacy & interoperability whitepapers.
  - FAQ pages and video tutorials with captions (EN/FR).
- Measurement:
  - Reduced support tickets about compatibility & repair costs, increased AppleCare uptake.

Accessibility & bilingual compliance
- All assets: Translated by humans, tested for readability; accessible HTML and video captions (EN/FR), keyboard navigation for web, voiceover demos for accessibility users.
- Legal & privacy: Review all creative/measurement with privacy/legal teams to ensure compliance with Canadian regulations.

Testing & optimization cadence
- Weekly: Channel performance reviews, creative-level CTR and spend shifts.
- Biweekly: A/B test outcomes, swap in winning creatives.
- Monthly: Cohort analysis, ROAS and LTV estimation, reallocation of budget based on top-performing segments.
