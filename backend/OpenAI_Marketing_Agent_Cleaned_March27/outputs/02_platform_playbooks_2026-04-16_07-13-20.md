# 2) Platform-Specific Playbooks

## Website playbook
### Homepage
- Lead with the all-in-one care platform promise
- Use a provider-first headline and a short patient-benefit subheadline
- Include CTA options: “Book a demo” and “See workflows”
- Add trust signals above the fold: security, privacy, countries served, provider categories

### Product pages
Create separate pages for:
- Telemedicine
- Records and documentation
- Messaging
- Scheduling
- Diagnostics
- AI-guided care

Each page should include:
- What it does
- How it helps clinicians
- Workflow examples
- Security/privacy notes
- CTA to demo

### Comparison page
- Compare against single-purpose tools
- Highlight consolidation benefits
- Include feature matrix
- Avoid naming competitors unless legally reviewed

## Doctor portal playbook
### Goals
- Improve activation
- Reduce time-to-first-value
- Encourage daily use
- Increase feature adoption across the care team

### In-product messaging
- Tooltips for key workflows
- Welcome checklist for new clinicians
- “Next best action” prompts
- Contextual reminders for follow-ups and documentation

### Activation sequence
1. Complete profile setup
2. Invite team members
3. Add patient records or connect existing workflow
4. Schedule first visit
5. Use messaging and follow-up tools
6. Review analytics or care summaries

## Patient-facing playbook
### Goals
- Reinforce convenience and trust
- Reduce friction in scheduling and virtual care
- Build confidence in privacy and care coordination

### Messaging focus
- Easy access to care from home
- Secure communication
- Faster responses and better continuity
- Clear next steps after visits

## LinkedIn playbook
### Best for
- Doctors
- clinic operators
- practice administrators
- healthcare founders

### Content formats
- Carousel explaining fragmented workflow problems
- Short demo clips
- Founder POV posts on digital care
- Security and compliance reassurance posts
- Case study summaries

### CTA
- “See the clinic workflow demo”
- “Download the provider brief”
- “Book a consultation”

## Email nurture playbook
### Sequence
- Email 1: Problem framing and platform overview
- Email 2: Provider workflow benefits
- Email 3: Security and privacy reassurance
- Email 4: AI-guided care explained responsibly
- Email 5: Demo invitation and case example

### Segmentation
- Physicians
- Clinic managers
- Telemedicine evaluators
- Existing leads with abandoned demo requests

## Search playbook
### Target keywords
- telemedicine platform for clinics
- digital healthcare workflow software
- secure patient messaging platform
- virtual care platform Canada
- scheduling and records platform for doctors
- AI-assisted healthcare workflow

### Landing page principles
- Match the search intent immediately
- Show feature summary and proof points
- Include clear security language
- Keep the primary CTA visible
