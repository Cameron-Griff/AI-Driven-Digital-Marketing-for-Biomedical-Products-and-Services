# 1) Detailed Marketing Plan

Goal summary
- Primary marketing goal: Rapidly deploy and scale clinically validated, safety-first AI triage to reduce inappropriate ED visits and increase patient confidence in care decisions.
- Business objectives (90 days / 12 months): enroll 3–5 pilot health systems and 10–50 clinics in a first 90-day pilot program; reduce non-urgent ED referrals from pilot sites by 10–20% within 6 months; gather clinical and patient experience data to support broader rollout and reimbursement conversations.

Core positioning and messaging
- Positioning statement: "Explainable, safety-first triage that guides patients to the right level of care quickly — reducing unnecessary ED visits while improving clinician handoffs."
- Primary value props to emphasize:
  - Safety-first and explainable recommendations (not a diagnosis).
  - Clinically validated and pilot-ready.
  - Reduces non-urgent ED visits and system burden.
  - Seamless clinician handoff and EHR integration.
- Tone: reassuring, clear, empathetic, professional, concise, safety-focused.
- Top-level messaging pillars:
  1. Safety & clinical governance (explainability, clinician oversight).
  2. Access & speed (24/7 low-latency digital triage).
  3. System impact (fewer inappropriate ED visits, smoother clinic workflows).
  4. Privacy & compliance (HIPAA / PIPEDA, secure integrations).

Target audiences & tailored value
- Patients/caregivers: quick, reassuring guidance that reduces anxiety and unnecessary ED trips.
- Primary care clinics & telehealth providers: reduced inappropriate in-person/ED referrals, better triage throughput.
- Emergency departments & health system admins: ED overcrowding mitigation, throughput improvement.
- Health IT/EHR teams: API-first, standards-compliant integration with audit trails and handoff summaries.

Assumptions (explicit)
- Pricing and commercial model: assume pilot is offered under a paid pilot subscription or outcomes-based pilot (assume budget for subsidized pilots); final pricing model TBD with stakeholders.
- Budget and resources: assume a modest marketing launch budget (paid media + clinical outreach) and availability of a small sales team and clinical champions to run pilots.
- Integration readiness: assume EHR APIs and clinic portal integration points are technically available for pilots; full integration timelines may vary by system.
- Regulatory: assume product is permissibly deployed as a triage/support tool under current HIPAA/PIPEDA frameworks and that no new national regulatory clearance beyond privacy/compliance is required for pilot use; local clinical governance approvals will be needed.
- Metrics access: assume pilot partners will share de-identified metrics (ED referrals, visits avoided, patient satisfaction) for evaluation.
- Languages: assume English and French localization resources are available for primary markets (US & Canada). Future localization will be phased.

Go-to-market strategy (high level)
1. Pilot-first outreach:
   - Identify and recruit clinical champions at health systems and primary care networks.
   - Offer 8–12 week co-designed pilots with clinical governance oversight and data-sharing agreements.
2. Clinical evidence & trust-building:
   - Publish pilot protocols, validation briefs, patient safety documentation, and explainability examples.
   - Create clinician-facing explainers and CME-style webinars.
3. Patient acquisition & activation:
   - Push through clinic portals, telehealth callbacks, and EHR inbox nudges to reach patients at point-of-need.
   - Use SEO (symptom search intent), app store optimization (ASO), and targeted social ads for direct-to-patient reach.
4. Partnerships:
   - Integrate with telehealth vendors and EHR vendors; seek reseller/implementation partnerships.
   - Engage payers and health system administrators for outcomes-based pilots.
5. Measurement & iteration:
   - Core KPIs: % reduction in non-urgent ED visits, patient satisfaction (NPS), time-to-recommendation, clinician adoption rate, integration success rate.
   - Run rapid A/B tests on messaging, question flow, and recommendation card formats to optimize safety and conversion.

Acquisition channels & tactics
- Clinic/health system outreach: sales + medical affairs-led pilot recruitment; clinician champion playbooks; targeted conferences and regional health authority briefings.
- Digital patient channels: symptom SEO, paid search targeting acute symptom queries, targeted social for caregiver audiences, app & web landing pages with quick symptom checkers.
- In-clinic activation: portal notifications, waitroom QR codes, SMS/phone triage links from clinics and telehealth follow-ups.
- Referral & virality: “share this guidance with your caregiver/clinic” referral prompts; clinician referrals to the app.
- PR & thought leadership: case studies, whitepapers, clinical validation summaries, and interviews focusing on safety-first explainability.

Retention & engagement
- Activated patients: follow-up nudges, symptom tracking, care navigation reminders, and simple confidence-check surveys.
- Clinicians: clinician dashboards, handoff summary templates, performance reports showing system-level impact and safety metrics.
- Health systems: regular review meetings, joint outcomes reporting, and optimization roadmaps.

Risk mitigation and legal/ethical guardrails
- Always present clear disclaimers that the product is not a diagnostic tool and does not replace clinician judgment.
- Display clinical governance information, data handling and privacy statements prominently (HIPAA/PIPEDA).
- Maintain auditable explainability logs per recommendation for medicolegal review.
- Escalation flows and 24/7 emergency messages must be conservative and clinically reviewed.
- Establish a clinical review board and legal counsel sign-off for all external-facing content and pilot contracts.

Success metrics (examples)
- Short-term (pilot): number of pilot sites onboarded; % of eligible patients using the triage tool when prompted; patient satisfaction score > 80; zero safety incidents from tool recommendations.
- Medium-term (6–12 months): 10–20% reduction in non-urgent ED visits at pilot sites; clinician adoption rate > 40% of triage cases; validated reductions in unnecessary telehealth/clinic visits.
- Long-term: paid contracts with health systems; integrations into EHRs and telehealth platforms; improved system-level throughput and cost savings data to support scaling.

Deliverables & timeline (summary)
- Weeks 1–4: pilot recruitment materials, clinical governance docs, patient-facing MVP content (EN/FR), integration playbooks.
- Weeks 5–8: onboard 1–3 pilot sites, run clinician webinars, deploy patient activation campaigns.
- Weeks 9–12: collect initial outcomes, iterate on content and flows, prepare case study/whitepaper.

# 2) Platform-Specific Playbooks

Patient Web App
- Goal: rapid self-triage, reduce ED anxiety, convert to appropriate care pathway.
- Key UX elements: one-click symptom start, progressive disclosure questionnaire, visual risk meter, explainable recommendation card (why recommendation made), clear escalation CTA (call 911 / go to ED / contact clinic), option to share summary with clinician.
- Messaging: reassuring, concise, safety-first; plain-language explanations and clear disclaimers; bilingual EN/FR toggle.
- Content & assets: symptom landing pages, short explainer videos, trust badges (clinically validated, HIPAA/PIPEDA), patient FAQs, privacy summary.
- Conversion points: clinic booking link, telehealth appointment link, printable/emailed handoff summary.
- Privacy/security: cookies minimized, encrypted session, explicit consent screens for sharing info with clinicians.
- Success metrics: completion rate of triage flows, time-to-recommendation, conversion to recommended action, user-reported confidence.

Mobile App (iOS/Android)
- Goal: on-demand, 24/7 accessible triage with push-based follow-ups.
- App-specific features: offline-friendly UI, push notification triage reminders, symptom history, caregiver mode (manage multiple profiles), ASO-optimized metadata.
- Onboarding: quick privacy & safety overview, permission gating (notifications), guided first-triage walkthrough.
- Engagement: contextual nudges (e.g., seasonal symptom pushes), in-app educational micro-content, appointment integration with calendar.
- Security: biometric sign-in option, device-level encryption, session timeouts.
- Success metrics: daily/weekly active users (DAU/WAU), retention at 7/30/90 days, push opt-in rates, referral activation.

Clinic Portals & In-Clinic Activation
- Goal: integrate at point-of-care to influence patient behavior before ED visits and improve clinic workflows.
- Implementation: lightweight widget or link embedded in patient portal, waitroom tablets/QR codes, SMS links post-teletriage.
- Clinician workflow: one-button send of patient's triage summary to clinician inbox; standardized clinician handoff summaries that map to clinic intake fields.
- Messaging to clinics: emphasize decreased non-urgent ED visits, reduced phone triage burden, faster patient routing.
- Training assets: quick-reference cards, 15–30 minute clinician demo, cheat sheets for interpreting recommendation cards.
- Success metrics: uptake of portal widget, number of patient-initiated triages from portal, clinician use of handoff summaries.

Telehealth Integrations
- Goal: reduce phone burden and support remote triage ahead of clinician assessment.
- Integration points: embed triage link in telehealth intake, pre-visit symptom capture, and clinician dashboard within telehealth platform.
- UX: pre-visit triage prompts sent via SMS/email; telehealth provider sees structured summary and confidence/urgency signals.
- Safety features: real-time escalation flags for high-acuity symptoms with recommended immediate action.
- Success metrics: % telehealth appointments with triage summary available, reduction in no-shows for appropriate care, reduced unnecessary scheduled urgent visits.

EHR Integrations
- Goal: seamless clinician handoff, audit trails, and system reporting.
- Technical approach: FHIR-based APIs, HL7 where required, OAuth2 for auth, SMART on FHIR app capabilities for embedding.
- Data to exchange: structured symptom data, recommendation card, timestamps, patient consent metadata, clinician notes (if any).
- Compliance: ensure audit logging, encrypted transit/storage, role-based access lists.
- Implementation playbook: developer sandbox, mapping templates, sample handoff payloads, integration checklist for security review.
- Success metrics: number of integrated clinics, time-to-live integration, number of handoffs per week.

Content & Creative Playbook (cross-platform)
- Tone: empathetic, clear, concise, authoritative.
- Primary assets:
  - Interactive symptom questionnaires tailored to common urgent and non-urgent conditions.
  - Explainable recommendation cards with “Why this recommendation?” toggles showing reasoning and next-steps.
  - Escalation templates: scripts for “call 911” and “seek urgent care” that meet local regulatory language.
  - Clinician handoff summaries designed to pre-populate EHR intake fields.
  - Patient education microcontent: short text + icon-based explainers and 30–60s videos for common triage outcomes.
- Localization: professional translation and clinical review in French (Canada), adapt wording to local idioms and regulatory phrasing.
- A/B testing: start with variations of reassurance language and escalation wording to measure impact on compliance with recommendations and patient confidence.

Trust & Risk-Communication Playbook
- Always include: clinical validation highlights, clear disclaimer “not a diagnosis,” data privacy summary, clinician oversight details.
- Visual trust signals: clinical partner logos (with permission), certification badges, clinician quotes and short video testimonials from pilots.
- Crisis messaging: pre-approved emergency wording templates that default to conservative escalation when red-flag symptoms are detected.

Sales & Partnership Playbook
- Target partners: integrated health systems, primary care networks, telehealth vendors, EHR vendors, regional health authorities, payers.
- Value-based pilots: offer outcomes-based pilots (e.g., shared savings on avoided ED visits), subject to measurement agreements.
- Sales collateral: pilot one-pagers, ROI calculator (based on ED visit cost savings), clinical validation brief, technical integration deck.
- Implementation support: clinical onboarding, technical onboarding, privacy/legal checklist, and a pilot success dashboard.

Measurement & Optimization Playbook
- Required analytic events: triage started/completed, recommendation type, time-to-recommendation, escalation invoked, patient action taken, share-to-clinic used.
- Safety monitoring: incident reporting workflow, weekly safety reviews, and rapid rollback capability for any change flagged by clinicians.
- Iteration cadence: bi-weekly product/content sprints informed by pilot data, monthly clinician advisory board calls.
