# 1) Detailed Marketing Plan

Executive summary
- Goal: Rapidly acquire and engage clinician users (GPs and specialists), convert clinics, and increase patient retention while preparing for geographic expansion (Canada → US → other English-speaking markets).
- Core message: “An integrated telehealth + EHR platform that centralizes care with AI diagnostics, lab connectivity, billing and CME—helping clinicians work faster, safer, and more confidently.”
- Priority outcomes (90 days): +30% clinician signups (active), 10 clinic conversions (multi-user), 20% increase in clinician weekly active usage, one published case study, one CME-accredited webinar series kickoff.

Key target audiences & value propositions
- Independent clinicians (GPs/specialists): Save time on documentation, improve diagnostic confidence via AI, simplify billing and lab orders. Primary CTA: schedule a live product demo / start trial.
- Clinic administrators: Reduce administrative overhead, streamline lab/lab billing integration, improve patient throughput. CTA: request clinic pilot.
- Patients (secondary audience): Easier access, integrated labs, smoother referrals. CTA: download app / book telehealth visit.
- Health system IT & lab partners: Integration and compliance-first partner. CTA: technical discovery call.

Positioning & messaging pillars
- Tech-forward clinician ally: “AI-assisted diagnostics that support — not replace — clinician decision-making.”
- Workflow-first integration: “One platform for telehealth, EHR, labs, billing and referrals.”
- Trust & privacy: “Canadian-resident data, privacy-first design; compliance-minded approach to PIPEDA/PHIPA and cross-border rules.”
- Evidence & education: “Clinician-focused CME and outcomes-focused case studies.”

Assumptions (explicit)
- Certification status: No public certifications (e.g., SOC2, ISO27001) yet. We assume these are pending.
- Budget: Not provided; plan assumes a modest early-stage marketing budget (~CAD 150–300k over 6–12 months) for digital, events, and content. If budget is <CAD 100k, scale paid channels down and prioritize organic/partnerships.
- Mobile apps: Assume future native apps exist or will be launched soon; current focus is responsive web & app store listings.
- CME accreditation: Assume the product team is seeking accreditation but not yet approved. Plan includes provisional CME content strategy contingent on accreditation.
- Geographic expansion: Assume legal/regulatory support will be available for US expansion; cross-border data residency is a gating consideration.
- Sales capacity: Assume small direct sales / customer success team (2–4 reps) for targeted outreach and clinic onboarding.

Acquisition strategy (funnel & tactics)
- Awareness: Thought leadership (whitepapers on AI in diagnostics, privacy), LinkedIn content, targeted display to clinician audiences, conference presence, app store optimization.
- Consideration: Live demos, on-demand product videos, case studies showing time saved / billing recovery, clinician testimonials, CME webinars.
- Conversion: 14–30 day pilot programs for clinics, free trial for individual clinicians (limited features), sales outreach with ROI calculators and lab integration proof points.
- Activation & onboarding: New clinician welcome flow with product tours, 1:1 onboarding calls for pilots, early-user playbook, clinician champions program.
- Retention & expansion: CME content + learning credits, monthly product updates focused on time-savings, referral incentives, integration enhancements (labs/referrals), analytics dashboard for clinics.
- Partnerships & channels: Lab networks, referral networks, EMR/EHR integrators, clinician associations, medical schools/residency programs for early adoption.

Growth & viral loops
- Clinician referral program: credits for colleagues who sign up; clinics get admin-credit when referring clinicians.
- Patient-to-clinic retention loop: Patients book telehealth → integrated lab results → follow-up with same platform → clinic sees value and adopts full EHR.
- Lab partnership co-marketing: Labs promote integrated ordering for participating clinicians.

Pricing & commercial strategy (high level)
- Freemium / trial: Free trial for individual clinicians with premium features gated (AI diagnostics, lab integrations, billing).
- Clinic pricing: Subscription per clinician seat + per-clinic admin fee + setup/integration fee for lab/EHR connectors.
- Enterprise/hospital: Custom pricing with SSO, SAML, SLA, data residency options.
- Value anchors for sales: Time saved per visit, increased billable codes captured via documentation templates, lab revenue facilitation.

Measurement & KPIs
- Acquisition: MQLs by channel, demo requests, CPA (LinkedIn/SEM), downloads (if apps), conference leads.
- Activation: % of new signups completing onboarding flow, time-to-first-telehealth-visit.
- Engagement: Weekly Active Users (WAU) for clinicians, number of patients per clinician, AI diagnostic feature usage.
- Retention/Revenue: 30/90-day retention, churn rate, ARR (clinics), expansion revenue from add-ons.
- Trust: Number of security certifications completed, NPS among clinicians.

Compliance & trust roadmap
- Short-term (30–90d): Publish privacy policy, data residency statements, HIPAA/PIPEDA communications, transparent data flow diagrams, clinician-facing security FAQ.
- Mid-term: Pursue SOC2 Type I, plan for SOC2 Type II/ISO27001, and a Canadian certification where relevant; publish whitepaper on AI safety and governance.
- Legal/Regulatory: Create a cross-border data checklist for expansion; finalize terms addressing clinician licensing across jurisdictions.

Go-to-market resourcing & timeline (90-day priorities)
- Content & Demand Gen: Whitepaper + landing page, 4 clinician-focused case study interviews, 6 LinkedIn posts, email nurture sequences.
- Events & Outreach: Book speaking slot/demo at 1 national Canadian medical conference; schedule 3 webinars (including 1 CME-applicable pending accreditation).
- Sales Enablement: Build ROI calculator, clinician demo scripts, one-pager for clinic admins.
- Technical/Compliance: Publish privacy/data residency page; begin SOC2 engagement.

Budgeting notes (assumed)
- Content production (whitepaper, videos, case study): CAD 20–40k.
- Digital ads (LinkedIn, search): CAD 5–15k/month.
- Conferences & travel: CAD 10–30k per event (booth/speaking).
- Sales & onboarding (headcount): dependent; assume 2 FTEs initially.

Risks and mitigation
- Regulatory obstacles in cross-border expansion — mitigate by delaying onboarding of US patients until legal checks complete and offering Canada-only deployments.
- Trust gaps due to missing certifications — mitigate with transparency, strong testimonials, and early SOC2 investment.
- Clinician change resistance — mitigate with CME content, peer champions, minimal-friction pilot.

Deliverables & next steps (first 30 days)
1. Publish privacy & compliance landing page and clinician FAQ.
2. Create whitepaper: “Measuring time saved and diagnostic concordance from AI-augmented telehealth” and promotional landing page.
3. Launch LinkedIn ad test targeting GPs in Ontario; A/B test messaging on workflow vs. AI diagnostics.
4. Schedule first webinar and outreach to an accrediting partner for CME.
5. Build demo & ROI materials for sales outreach to 20 targeted clinics in Ottawa.
