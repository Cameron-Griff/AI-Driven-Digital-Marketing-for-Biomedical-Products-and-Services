# 2) Platform-Specific Playbooks

## 1. Clinic dashboard playbook
**Goal:** Drive adoption inside existing clinician workflows.

### What to show
- Brief patient summary
- Symptom-based triage inputs
- Recommendation: emergency, clinic, or home care
- Explainability panel: top reasons, confidence context, escalation flags
- Clinician override and documentation notes

### Best content format
- Embedded workflow cards
- Short tooltips
- Inline safety prompts
- “Why this recommendation?” expanders

### Messaging
- “Support faster triage without losing oversight.”
- “See the reasoning behind the recommendation.”
- “Escalate urgent cases immediately.”

### CTA
- “Review a sample triage workflow”
- “See how it fits your clinic dashboard”

## 2. Telehealth workflow playbook
**Goal:** Improve pre-visit triage and routing in virtual care.

### What to show
- Intake screen before clinician review
- Red-flag symptom detection
- Route-to-care recommendations
- Safe escalation prompts for urgent cases
- Privacy and consent reminders

### Best content format
- Short workflow videos
- Interactive demo with branching scenarios
- Provider-facing decision support cards

### Messaging
- “Triage patients faster before the visit begins.”
- “Standardize routing across high-volume telehealth encounters.”
- “Identify urgent cases early.”

### CTA
- “Watch the telehealth triage demo”
- “Request a virtual care pilot”

**Goal:** Position the product as a capacity and flow improvement tool.

### What to show

- Governance and compliance controls

### Best content format
- Executive summary brief
- ROI model
- Flow improvement infographic
- Implementation checklist

### Messaging

- “Improve routing consistency across teams.”
- “Support safer patient flow at scale.”

### CTA
- “Download the operations brief”
- “See the ROI model”
