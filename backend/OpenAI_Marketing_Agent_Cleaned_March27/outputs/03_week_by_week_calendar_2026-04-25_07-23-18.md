### Suggested posting cadence
- **Patients:** 2 educational touchpoints per week via app, email, or website
- **Providers/admins:** 1 thought-leadership or proof-point asset per week
- **Live sessions:** 1 webinar or demo every 2–3 weeks
- **Evergreen assets:** Keep the red-flag, privacy, and “how it works” pages continuously visible

### Content governance
- Review all patient-facing content with clinical and compliance stakeholders before publishing.
- Maintain version control for all triage-related educational content.
- Update emergency guidance and legal disclaimers to reflect current regional requirements.
- Ensure localization is adapted carefully for future regions and languages.
