# 3) Week-by-Week Content Calendar

Notes: This 12-week calendar assumes you start with 0–2 pilots and the goal is to recruit pilots, publish a whitepaper, run two webinars and secure conference presence prep. All assets should have English + French variants prepared where applicable.

| Week | Primary Focus / Theme | Content & Activities | Channels | KPI / CTA |
|------|-----------------------|----------------------|----------|-----------|
| 1 | Pilot recruitment + core asset production | Create demo video (3min) + 30s clips; build pilot offer landing page; produce one-pager feature sheets (EN/FR) | Website, LinkedIn organic, direct outreach | Pilot landing page live; 10 pilot inquiries |
| 2 | Awareness ramp + whitepaper initial draft | Launch LinkedIn awareness posts; start paid LinkedIn Sponsored Content; draft whitepaper outline and data collection from internal team | LinkedIn, Sales outreach, Email teasers | 1–2 demo bookings; 1000 impressions |
| 3 | Webinar promotion + email nurture start | Open webinar registration (Intro session), send first email in nurture sequence, run InMail to targeted pharma office managers | Email, LinkedIn InMail | 50 webinar signups; 5 demo requests |
| 4 | Whitepaper release + retargeting | Publish whitepaper (gated); retarget video viewers with lead gen form; publish short demo clip on LinkedIn | Website (gated), LinkedIn, Email | 200 downloads/lead captures; 15 new MQLs |
| 5 | Webinar #1 (Intro) + follow-up | Host Intro webinar (record + Q&A); follow-up emails + sales calls for attendees; post webinar clip as short social videos | Webinar, Email, LinkedIn | 100 registrants, 35% attendance, 10 demo requests |
| 6 | Pilot onboarding + case study planning | Onboard 2–3 pilots; begin structured data capture for case study metrics; publish blog about compliance roadmap | Sales, Website, Email | 2 pilot activations; data capture plan in place |
| 7 | Thought leadership & trade pubs outreach | Submit whitepaper excerpts/byline to trade publications; post CEO/founder thought leadership on LinkedIn | Trade pubs, LinkedIn | 2 placements in pipeline; referral traffic |
| 8 | Deep-dive content + webinar #2 promotion | Produce deep-dive demo focused on trial/inventory management; open registrations for deep-dive webinar | LinkedIn, Email, Trade pubs | 75–100 registrants; 8–12 demo follow-ups |
| 9 | Webinar #2 (Deep-dive) + PR | Host deep-dive with pilot clinic speaker; issue press release with pilot highlights (with permissions) | Webinar, Trade pubs, Email | 40% attendance; 12 high-quality leads |
| 10 | Conference outreach + sales push | Announce conference presence; book 1:1 demo slots at event; distribute revised case study one-pager | Email, LinkedIn, Direct outreach | 15 booked event meetings; 5 enterprise leads |
| 11 | Scale outreach + French localization push | Launch francophone campaign in Quebec: translated assets, targeted LinkedIn posts and emails | LinkedIn (FR), Email (FR) | 50 FR leads; 5 demo requests |
| 12 | Convert pilots to paid + publish case studies | Publish 1–2 case studies from pilots; run conversion push: limited-time pilot-to-paid offers; present onboarding references | Website, Email, Sales outreach | Convert 10–20% of pilots to paid; 20–30 demos scheduled |

Execution notes and owners
- Owners: Marketing lead (campaigns & content), Sales lead (outreach + pilot conversion), Product/CS (pilot onboarding & data capture), Legal/Compliance (asset reviews).
- Localization: Translate and QC all public assets to French by Week 11; webinars to provide French subtitles or a bilingual session option.
- Follow-up: Sales to call all webinar attendees/high-engagement leads within 48 hours.
- Reporting cadence: Weekly sprint reviews (marketing + sales), monthly KPI review against targets.

Additional tactical notes
- Prioritize producing measurable pilot outcomes (time saved, errors reduced, trial enrollment speed) to power case studies.
- Keep messaging conservative on clinical efficacy; emphasize workflow benefits and AI-assisted decision support.
- If regulatory badges are obtained mid-campaign, update all assets immediately and run a press/PR amplification.

End of plan — ready to adapt to budget, final pricing, and product roadmap confirmations.
