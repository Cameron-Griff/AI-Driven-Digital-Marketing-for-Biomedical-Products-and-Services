# 2) Platform-Specific Playbooks

Company website & authenticated landing pages
- Primary goals: convert visitors into authenticated signups and demo requests.
- Key pages: Patient landing, Clinician landing, Security & Privacy, Pilot results/case studies, FAQ.
- Messaging focus: Hero + 3 proof points (privacy, integrated labs, faster diagnostics), clinician endorsement quote, quick demo video.
- CTA hierarchy: Book a consult (main), Request clinician demo, Download case study.
- SEO/Content: Target keywords: virtual care Canada, telemedicine labs integration, secure digital health records Canada.
- Conversion tactics: Social proof (pilot stats), trust badges (PHIPA/PIPEDA), exit-intent modal for demo requests, micro‑form to reduce friction (email + phone).
- KPIs: Conversion rate (visit→auth signup), demo requests/week, bounce rate, time on page.
- Tracking: GA4 + funnel events, authenticated user attribution.

Mobile apps & app store
- Goals: increase app installs, authenticated activations, and appointment bookings.
- Assets: Short promo video (30s), 3–5 screenshots showing workflow (book → consult → lab order → results), privacy/security statement in app description.
- App Store Optimization (ASO): Title + subtitle emphasize "Secure virtual care + lab integration", include Ottawa/Canada for local relevance.
- CTA inside app: “Create secure account” with simplified multi‑role signup (patient or clinician).
- Onboarding: Progressive disclosure for permissions, explain consent for labs and AI insights.
- KPIs: Install to activation conversion, retention Day1/7/30, bookings per install.

LinkedIn (primary paid/organic for clinicians & B2B)
- Goals: recruit clinician champions, clinician demo signups, clinic partnerships.
- Organic content mix: Thought leadership posts (AI safety, TAT improvements), clinician testimonials, pilot metrics.
- Paid campaigns: Sponsored Content & InMail targeting Canadian clinicians, clinic managers, lab managers. Use exact job titles and clinical specialties in pilot.
- Ad creative: 15–30s video clips of clinician testimonial + static ad with ROI stat. CTA: Request demo / Join pilot.
- Frequency: 3–4 posts/week organic; paid: 1–2 active campaigns with rotating creatives.
- KPIs: Cost per lead (CPL), demo booking rate, LinkedIn engagement.

Targeted email campaigns (patients & clinicians)
- Segments: New visitor (unauthed), authenticated patient (activated), inactive patients, clinician prospects, clinician pilots.
- Patient flows: Welcome → How it works → Privacy & billing → Book now → Reengagement. 5‑email nurture over 3 weeks.
- Clinician flows: Intro → ROI & workflow → Demo invite → Onboarding resources → Referral invite.
- Personalization: Use role, region (Ottawa), specialty (for clinicians).
- CTA: Book consult, Request demo, Join webinar.
- KPIs: Open rates (25–35% target), CTR (5–10%), conversion to demo/book.

Clinician outreach & sales enablement
- Tactics: Peer webinars, live demos, ROI calculator, clinical champion ambassador program, CME‑style sessions if possible.
- Collateral: One‑pager ROI, workflow comparison (time saved), integration tech sheet, liability & data governance brief.
- Outreach cadence: 6‑touch sequence (LinkedIn message → email → call → webinar invite → demo → follow up).
- KPIs: Demos scheduled, pilot signups, active clinician %.

Healthcare conferences & events
- Goals: brand credibility, clinician leads, lab partner meetings.
- Pre-event: Targeted invitations to book demos at booth, publish speakership abstracts.
- At-event: Live demos, QR codes to instant sign-up/book consult, handouts (case study one‑pager).
- Post-event: Follow-up emails with recorded demo, special pilot enrollment offer.
- KPIs: Leads per event, demo appointments scheduled from event, partnership meetings.

Paid social & search
- Patient targeted channels: Google Search (local), Facebook/Meta (privacy sensitivity—use careful targeting), programmatic for local geo-targeting.
- Messaging tests: Convenience vs security vs speed of labs.
- Landing pages: Tailored to ad creative promise (e.g., "Same‑day lab ordering from home" ad goes to page showing lab workflow).
- KPIs: CPA for activation, CTR, conversion rate.

Content & creative guidance (format & tone)
- Format mix: Short explainer videos, demo walkthroughs, clinician testimonial clips, patient stories, 1‑page case studies, infographics on TAT improvements.
- Tone: Modern, empathetic, clinically credible. Avoid hype around AI—use "AI assistance" language and explainability snippets.
- Accessibility: All content mobile responsive, captions on video, plain language for patient audiences.

Measurement & analytics playbook
- Events to track: Landing view, auth signup, first appointment booked, lab order placed, lab result viewed, clinician demo booked, clinician active session.
- Tools: GA4, product analytics (Mixpanel/Amplitude), CRM for lead tracking, ad platform pixels, call tracking for phone leads.
- Attribution: UTM standards, first/last touch for channel performance, cohort analysis for retention.

Compliance & legal playbook
- Include privacy statements on every landing page and in app onboarding.
- All testimonials must have signed consent; anonymize clinical data in case studies.
- Medical/legal sign‑off on any clinical claims and AI performance statements.
