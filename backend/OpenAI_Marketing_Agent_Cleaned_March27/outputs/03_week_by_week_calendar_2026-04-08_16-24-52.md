# 3) Week-by-Week Content Calendar

Assumptions for calendar: 8-week campaign starting pre-season ramp to peak. Local event weekends planned Weeks 3, 5, 7 in three major markets. Paid creative rotates every 2 weeks; influencer content seeded throughout.

| Week | Theme / Focus | Key Social Content (Instagram / TikTok / YouTube Shorts) | Email & Site Actions | Event / Retail |
|------|---------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|----------------------|----------------|
| 1 | Launch: Announce seasonal push & bundles | Launch Reel (30s hero + setup), TikTok challenge intro, YouTube Short teaser | Launch email (72h discount), update PDPs with bundle options & CAD pricing | Retail: Ship demo units; retail staff training email |
| 2 | How-to & barriers: setup + beginner tips | 3x short vids: 15s setup demo, 30s “how to serve”, UGC compilation push | Welcome series & cart recovery flows live; blog: “How to play in 5 mins” | Local influencer seeding begins; RSVP page live for Week 3 event |
| 3 | Local event push (City A) | Live Stories from event, Reel recap (45s), TikTok duet with attendees | Event reminder email + exclusive event bundle offer; post-event recap email | Event activation (City A) — demos + QR shop codes |
| 4 | Community & UGC showcase | UGC montage Reel, influencer match highlight, Shorts tutorial | Flash bundle drop email (24hr); add UGC gallery on product page | Retail demo weekend (support signs & staff incentives) |
| 5 | Competition & skill play (City B event) | Highlight Reel of trick shots, TikTok challenge entrants, long-form 3–5min tutorial on YouTube | Event invite + follow-up to attendees; targeted local promo for City B | Event activation (City B) + retail cross-promo |
| 6 | Social proof & retention | Customer testimonial Reel, “what’s in the bag” influencer posts, Shorts tips | Post-purchase flow reminder (setup guide + referral code); promo for returning buyers | Stock check & replenish retail demo kits |
| 7 | Big community weekend (City C) & UGC contest finale | Hero recap Reel from City C, contest highlight Reel, TikTok results video | UGC contest final reminder + winner announcement email; last-chance bundle offer | Event activation (City C); in-store pop-up at partner locations |

Notes and execution heuristics
- Posting times: Peak evenings (6–10pm local) for Instagram/TikTok; test first 2 weeks and lock best-performing time.
- UGC contest mechanics: Users post clip with campaign hashtag and tag brand; top 10 finalists selected by engagement + judge panel; legal rules provided in email/landing page.
- Creative rotations: Swap primary creative variations every 2 weeks based on CTR/ROAS.
- KPI targets (example): Site conversion 2.5–4%, AOV +15% with bundles, CPA target depends on budget — refine live.
- Weekly check-ins: Review top 3 wins/risks and creative winners; iterate quickly on underperforming formats.
