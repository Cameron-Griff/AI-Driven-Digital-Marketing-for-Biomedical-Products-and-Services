# 1) Detailed Marketing Plan

Executive summary
- Goal: Rapidly acquire and engage clinician users (GPs and specialists), convert clinics, and increase patient retention while preparing for geographic expansion (Canada → US → other English-speaking markets).
- Core message: “An integrated telehealth + EHR platform that centralizes care with AI diagnostics, lab connectivity, billing and CME—helping clinicians work faster, safer, and more confidently.”
- Priority outcomes (90 days): +30% clinician signups (active), 10 clinic conversions (multi-user), 20% increase in clinician weekly active usage, one published case study, one CME-accredited webinar series kickoff.

Key target audiences & value propositions
- Independent clinicians (GPs/specialists): Save time on documentation, improve diagnostic confidence via AI, simplify billing and lab orders. Primary CTA: schedule a live product demo / start trial.
- Clinic administrators: Reduce administrative overhead, streamline lab/lab billing integration, improve patient throughput. CTA: request clinic pilot.
- Patients (secondary audience): Easier access, integrated labs, smoother referrals. CTA: download app / book telehealth visit.
- Health system IT & lab partners: Integration and compliance-first partner. CTA: technical discovery call.

Positioning & messaging pillars
- Tech-forward clinician ally: “AI-assisted diagnostics that support — not replace — clinician decision-making.”
- Workflow-first integration: “One platform for telehealth, EHR, labs, billing and referrals.”
- Trust & privacy: “Canadian-resident data, privacy-first design; compliance-minded approach to PIPEDA/PHIPA and cross-border rules.”
- Evidence & education: “Clinician-focused CME and outcomes-focused case studies.”

Assumptions (explicit)
- Certification status: No public certifications (e.g., SOC2, ISO27001) yet. We assume these are pending.
- Budget: Not provided; plan assumes a modest early-stage marketing budget (~CAD 150–300k over 6–12 months) for digital, events, and content. If budget is <CAD 100k, scale paid channels down and prioritize organic/partnerships.
- Mobile apps: Assume future native apps exist or will be launched soon; current focus is responsive web & app store listings.
- CME accreditation: Assume the product team is seeking accreditation but not yet approved. Plan includes provisional CME content strategy contingent on accreditation.
- Geographic expansion: Assume legal/regulatory support will be available for US expansion; cross-border data residency is a gating consideration.
- Sales capacity: Assume small direct sales / customer success team (2–4 reps) for targeted outreach and clinic onboarding.

Acquisition strategy (funnel & tactics)
- Awareness: Thought leadership (whitepapers on AI in diagnostics, privacy), LinkedIn content, targeted display to clinician audiences, conference presence, app store optimization.
- Consideration: Live demos, on-demand product videos, case studies showing time saved / billing recovery, clinician testimonials, CME webinars.
- Conversion: 14–30 day pilot programs for clinics, free trial for individual clinicians (limited features), sales outreach with ROI calculators and lab integration proof points.
- Activation & onboarding: New clinician welcome flow with product tours, 1:1 onboarding calls for pilots, early-user playbook, clinician champions program.
- Retention & expansion: CME content + learning credits, monthly product updates focused on time-savings, referral incentives, integration enhancements (labs/referrals), analytics dashboard for clinics.
- Partnerships & channels: Lab networks, referral networks, EMR/EHR integrators, clinician associations, medical schools/residency programs for early adoption.

Growth & viral loops
- Clinician referral program: credits for colleagues who sign up; clinics get admin-credit when referring clinicians.
- Patient-to-clinic retention loop: Patients book telehealth → integrated lab results → follow-up with same platform → clinic sees value and adopts full EHR.
- Lab partnership co-marketing: Labs promote integrated ordering for participating clinicians.

Pricing & commercial strategy (high level)
- Freemium / trial: Free trial for individual clinicians with premium features gated (AI diagnostics, lab integrations, billing).
- Clinic pricing: Subscription per clinician seat + per-clinic admin fee + setup/integration fee for lab/EHR connectors.
- Enterprise/hospital: Custom pricing with SSO, SAML, SLA, data residency options.
- Value anchors for sales: Time saved per visit, increased billable codes captured via documentation templates, lab revenue facilitation.

Measurement & KPIs
- Acquisition: MQLs by channel, demo requests, CPA (LinkedIn/SEM), downloads (if apps), conference leads.
- Activation: % of new signups completing onboarding flow, time-to-first-telehealth-visit.
- Engagement: Weekly Active Users (WAU) for clinicians, number of patients per clinician, AI diagnostic feature usage.
- Retention/Revenue: 30/90-day retention, churn rate, ARR (clinics), expansion revenue from add-ons.
- Trust: Number of security certifications completed, NPS among clinicians.

Compliance & trust roadmap
- Short-term (30–90d): Publish privacy policy, data residency statements, HIPAA/PIPEDA communications, transparent data flow diagrams, clinician-facing security FAQ.
- Mid-term: Pursue SOC2 Type I, plan for SOC2 Type II/ISO27001, and a Canadian certification where relevant; publish whitepaper on AI safety and governance.
- Legal/Regulatory: Create a cross-border data checklist for expansion; finalize terms addressing clinician licensing across jurisdictions.

Go-to-market resourcing & timeline (90-day priorities)
- Content & Demand Gen: Whitepaper + landing page, 4 clinician-focused case study interviews, 6 LinkedIn posts, email nurture sequences.
- Events & Outreach: Book speaking slot/demo at 1 national Canadian medical conference; schedule 3 webinars (including 1 CME-applicable pending accreditation).
- Sales Enablement: Build ROI calculator, clinician demo scripts, one-pager for clinic admins.
- Technical/Compliance: Publish privacy/data residency page; begin SOC2 engagement.

Budgeting notes (assumed)
- Content production (whitepaper, videos, case study): CAD 20–40k.
- Digital ads (LinkedIn, search): CAD 5–15k/month.
- Conferences & travel: CAD 10–30k per event (booth/speaking).
- Sales & onboarding (headcount): dependent; assume 2 FTEs initially.

Risks and mitigation
- Regulatory obstacles in cross-border expansion — mitigate by delaying onboarding of US patients until legal checks complete and offering Canada-only deployments.
- Trust gaps due to missing certifications — mitigate with transparency, strong testimonials, and early SOC2 investment.
- Clinician change resistance — mitigate with CME content, peer champions, minimal-friction pilot.

Deliverables & next steps (first 30 days)
1. Publish privacy & compliance landing page and clinician FAQ.
2. Create whitepaper: “Measuring time saved and diagnostic concordance from AI-augmented telehealth” and promotional landing page.
3. Launch LinkedIn ad test targeting GPs in Ontario; A/B test messaging on workflow vs. AI diagnostics.
4. Schedule first webinar and outreach to an accrediting partner for CME.
5. Build demo & ROI materials for sales outreach to 20 targeted clinics in Ottawa.

# 2) Platform-Specific Playbooks

Website
- Objective: Convert clinician visitors into demo requests, trials, and clinic pilot inquiries.
- Audience focus: Clinicians (GPs/specialists), clinic admins, IT decision-makers.
- Core pages & content: Homepage (value props), Product pages (telehealth, EHR, AI diagnostics, labs, billing), Case studies, Security & privacy center, Pricing (indicative), Demo sign-up, Resources (whitepapers, webinars, CME).
- Messaging: Short hero lines focused on time-savings + trust (e.g., “Centralize care. Spend more time with patients. Less admin.”). Below-the-fold: evidence points, testimonial quote, key metrics (time saved, billing recovered), CTA.
- CTAs & flows: Primary CTA = Book demo (calendar integration + qualifying questions); Secondary = Download whitepaper / Request a clinic pilot; Tertiary = Sign up for webinar.
- SEO & pages: Target keywords: “telehealth EHR Canada”, “AI diagnostic platform for clinicians”, “lab integrated EHR Canada”.
- Measurement: Demo conversion rate, time on page, heatmaps, bounce rate; goal: 10–15% demo conversion on product pages.
- Compliance checklist: Privacy link in footer, data residency & consent language on relevant pages, avoid clinical claims that imply FDA-equivalent approvals.

LinkedIn (organic + paid)
- Objective: Thought leadership, drive demo signups and webinar registrations.
- Organic strategy: Company page + employee advocacy (encourage clinicians on payroll to repost). Weekly cadence: 3 posts/week: 1 evidence-based post (whitepaper excerpt), 1 clinician testimonial/case highlight, 1 product explainer or demo clip.
- Paid strategy: Sponsored content targeted to MDs, DOs, nurse practitioners, clinic admins in Canada & select US states. Ad creative variants: workflow-first vs. AI-as-assistant. Landing pages: webinar signup or demo booking.
- Messaging pillars: “Save 10–20 mins/visit”, “integrated lab order + results”, “AI supports diagnostic confidence”.
- CTAs: Register for webinar / Book demo / Download whitepaper.
- Measurement: CTR, CPL, demo conversion, cost per demo (benchmark CAD 60–200 depending on audience).

Email newsletters & automated nurture
- Objective: Nurture leads to demo and trial; onboard new users; drive webinar attendance and CME sign-ups.
- Flow examples:
  - MQL nurture (7–10 emails over 30 days): value + social proof → product features → case study → webinar invite → demo CTA.
  - New clinician onboarding drip: welcome → product tour video → quick wins (templates, billing setup) → invite to community forum/webinar.
  - Clinic pilot nurture: setup checklist → training schedule → pilot evaluation survey.
- Content: Short, evidence-minded subject lines; link to resources; include one explicit CTA per email.
- Measurement: Open rate (>25% target), CTR (>5%), demo bookings from email.

Medical conferences & events
- Objective: Brand trust, demo leads, partnerships (labs/hospitals).
- Pre-event: Targeted outreach to attendees, pre-book demos, share exclusive invite to CME session.
- At-event: Live demos, clinician testimonial video loop, handouts (one-pager with ROI), schedule for follow-up pilot offers.
- Post-event: Email nurture for leads, LinkedIn follow-ups, publish event summary blog and short video.
- Measurement: Leads per event, demo-to-pilot conversion within 60 days.
- Tactical tip: Apply to speak on panels about AI governance, telehealth integration to position as evidence-minded.

Clinician forums & communities (Sermo, Doximity-equivalent, specialty forums)
- Objective: Engagement with clinicians, gather feedback, recruit champions.
- Tactics: Host AMA (ask-me-anything) sessions with product clinicians or co-authored thought pieces; target specialty subgroups (e.g., family medicine).
- Rules: Maintain academic tone, avoid overt sales messaging; focus on clinical value and safety.
- Measurement: Number of quality interactions, referral signups from forum links.

Targeted hospital/clinic outreach (direct sales)
- Objective: Close clinic pilots and multi-user deployments.
- Playbook: Identify clinic targets via lists (specialty, size), SDR outreach (email + LinkedIn + phone), offer pilot terms with low friction (30–60 day pilot, onboarding included), present ROI and integration plan.
- Sales assets: ROI calculator, integration checklist (labs/EHR), pilot SOW, clinician success stories.
- Decision-maker mapping: Clinic admin/medical director → IT lead → billing manager → clinician champion.
- Measurement: Demo-to-pilot conversion rate, pilot-to-paid conversion rate, sales cycle length.

App Store & mobile presence
- Objective: Support patient and clinician discovery; improve trust with screenshots and updated release notes.
- ASO (App Store Optimization): Use keywords: “telehealth EHR”, “Canadian telemedicine”, provide localized screenshots (Canada), highlight security and clinician features.
- Assets: Short demo video, clinician testimonial, privacy & data residency note in description.
- Measurement: Impressions → installs → signups; app rating (aim >=4.5).

Webinars & demos (including CME track)
- Objective: Drive high-intent leads, provide education and activation.
- Formats: 45–60min sessions: 25–30min demo/edu session + 20–30min case panel + live Q&A. Offer CME credits when possible.
- Topics: “AI-assisted diagnosis in primary care: Evidence and practical use”, “Streamlining lab orders and billing in one workflow”.
- Promotion: LinkedIn ads, email list, partner co-promotion with lab partners or clinician associations.
- Measurement: Registrations, live-attendance rate (>40% target), demo signups following webinar.

Explainer videos & product demos
- Objective: Shorten sales cycle, clarify complex integrations.
- Formats: 60–90s hero video for homepage, 3–5 min feature demos, 1–2 min patient-facing explainer.
- Distribution: Website, LinkedIn, YouTube (unlisted/controlled), sales collateral.
- Measurement: Views → watch-through rate → demo requests.

Case studies & clinician testimonials
- Objective: Prove outcomes (time saved, billing improvements, patient satisfaction).
- Format: 800–1,200 word case studies with metrics, clinician quotes, visuals/screenshots (obfuscate PHI).
- Tactical: Offer honorarium or CME credits to clinicians who participate.
- Measurement: Page conversions, sales-sourced leads citing case study.

CME content & academic partnerships
- Objective: Use CME to attract clinicians and reduce friction to trial/engagement.
- Tactics: Partner with accredited CME providers; develop modules showing clinical utility and safety of AI support; promote as limited-time credit for pilot participants.
- Measurement: CME registrations → demo signups → retention among attendees.

Paid media experiments
- Channels: LinkedIn (primary), Google Search (targeted keywords), programmatic display on clinician outlets.
- Tests: Workflow-first vs. AI-first messaging; short video vs. static imagery; geographic split (Ontario vs. rest of Canada).
- Measurement: CPA, demo conversion, CAC by channel.

Trust & compliance comms
- Publish a security & privacy hub on site with:
  - Data residency and encryption details
  - Third-party partner list (labs, integrations)
  - Roadmap for certifications (SOC2/ISO)
  - Clinical governance: AI explainability whitepaper
- Use customer-facing contractual language (BAA equivalents) for clinics and partners.

Community & retention tactics
- Clinician champions program: early adopters used as case study authors & webinar panelists; offer incentives (discounts, CME credits).
- Product updates newsletter: monthly technical changelog + clinical implications.
- In-app tips and micro-CME: Quick learning modules for new features.

Measurement & reporting cadence
- Weekly: Channel-level leads, demo bookings, LinkedIn ad performance.
- Bi-weekly: Onboarding and activation metrics, pilot progress.
- Monthly: CAC, LTV projections, retention metrics, regulatory milestones.
