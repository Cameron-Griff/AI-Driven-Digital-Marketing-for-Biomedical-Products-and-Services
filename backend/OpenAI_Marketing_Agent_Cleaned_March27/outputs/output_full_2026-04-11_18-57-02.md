# 1) Detailed Marketing Plan

Assumptions (explicit)
- Pricing: assume tiered per-provider SaaS + transaction fee for billing/teleconsult capture; pilot discounts available. If different, adjust acquisition offers.
- Budget & team: assume a modest growth marketing budget and a small internal team (1 product marketer, 1 content creator, 1 growth marketer, outsourced design/video + a channel partnerships lead).
- Sales capacity: assume a lean sales/dev-ops team running demos and 10–20 integration pilots concurrently.
- Timing: 12-week initial campaign to drive measurable adoption; ongoing cadence thereafter.
- Regulatory/Legal: assume product meets PIPEDA/HIPAA-equivalent technical controls; marketing must avoid clinical claims beyond supported evidence.

Goal summary (primary)
- Drive doctor adoption, increase teleconsult volume, and simplify billing/revenue capture by convincing practicing physicians and clinic managers to try and adopt the platform.

Success metrics (KPIs)
- Top-level: number of new clinician signups (target: +150–300 clinicians in 12 months), number of teleconsult sessions (target +X% quarter-over-quarter), monthly recurring revenue (MRR) growth.
- Funnel metrics: demo requests, pilot conversions (pilot → paid), activation rate (login + first teleconsult within 14 days), retention (30/90-day active clinician%), billing capture rate (percent of billable consults captured).
- Content metrics: email open/click rates, demo video view-through, webinar registrations/attendees, conference lead conversion.

Positioning & messaging pillars
- Core position: “A secure, clinician-first orchestration layer that centralizes EHR, teleconsults, AI-assisted diagnostics, labs and billing—so clinicians spend less time administrating and more time treating.”
- Pillar 1 — Workflow efficiency: fewer clicks, fewer systems, faster visit throughput.
- Pillar 2 — Clinical confidence: embedded AI supports diagnostics — evidence-based, clinician-validated, auditable.
- Pillar 3 — Revenue integrity: integrated billing and lab orchestration reduces leakage and accelerates capture.
- Pillar 4 — Privacy & governance: enterprise-grade encryption, regional data residency, clinical governance and audit trails.
- Tone: modern, tech-forward, authoritative and patient-centric; prioritize clinician credibility and privacy assurances.

Target segments & value propositions
- Practicing physicians (primary): “Reduce administrative burden and get faster diagnostic support so you can see more patients with better outcomes.”
- Clinic managers / practice owners: “Centralize workflows, simplify billing and improve practice revenue capture.”
- Labs & referral partners: “Seamless orchestration and faster turnaround, enabling referral networks and fewer manual steps.”
- Allied clinicians & billing staff: “Simpler scheduling, one source of truth, and automated billing handoffs.”

Acquisition strategy
- Demand gen mix: inbound content (SEO & website), targeted email sequences, LinkedIn outreach (thought leadership + sponsored posts), webinars with CME credits, presence at medical conferences, partnerships with clinics and labs for pilot programs.
- Lead magnets: clinician case studies, whitepapers on AI-assist clinical validation, CME webinar registration (lead capture), pilot program signup form and one-pagers for practice managers.
- Paid tactics (targeted): LinkedIn Sponsored Content to physicians and clinic managers in Ontario/Canadian provinces; programmatic banners on clinician portals/newsletters; conference sponsorships.

Sales & conversion funnel
- Awareness → Consideration → Trial/Pilot → Activation → Expansion.
- Entry points: content download, webinar signup, LinkedIn ad, conference contact, partner referral.
- Conversion tactics: live demo (sales engineer + clinical champion), 30–60 day pilot with success criteria (reduced admin time, billing capture increase, teleconsult adoption), onboarding playbook and dedicated onboarding manager.
- Incentives: limited-time pilot discount, first 3 months waived for early clinic partners, CME webinar attendance qualifies as a lead nurture priority.

Content mix & formats
- Product demos (short explainer + longer demo for sales).
- Clinician case studies (video + one-pager).
- Whitepapers & technical briefs (data residency, security, AI validation).
- Quick explainer videos (30–90s) for social & website.
- CME-accredited webinars (driven by clinical advisory board).
- One-pagers tailored to practice managers and lab partners.
- Newsletter snippets targeted to clinician audiences.

Retention & expansion
- Activation: onboarding checklist, milestone emails, in-product tips, quick-start playbook for clinic managers.
- Ongoing engagement: monthly clinical updates, feature highlights, new AI model validation reports, referral incentives.
- Upsell: advanced analytics, lab orchestration premium, network referrals; present ROI reports to clinic managers.
- Support: dedicated clinician success manager for pilots and early adopters; clinician advisory board to steer product trust.

Regulatory & compliance considerations (marketing)
- Avoid unsubstantiated clinical claims; use clinician testimonials and case data with consent.
- Prominently state PIPEDA/HIPAA-equivalent compliance, data residency options, encryption standards, and auditability.
- Collaborate with legal for any content referencing diagnostic accuracy of AI; include disclaimers and clinical governance notes in whitepapers and demos.

Operational checklist (immediate next steps)
- Finalize pilot offer & success metrics; create onboarding playbook.
- Produce cornerstone assets: one demo video, two clinician case studies, a whitepaper on AI validation, CME webinar landing page.
- Build targeted email nurture sequences and LinkedIn ad creatives.
- Prepare compliance-approved messaging docs and legal signoffs.

# 2) Platform-Specific Playbooks

Corporate website
- Goal: convert clinician & practice-manager visitors to demo requests, pilot signups, or gated content downloads.
- Priority pages: homepage hero (value prop + CTA), Solutions for Physicians, Practice Manager one-pager, Security & Compliance page, Resources (case studies, whitepapers, webinars), Demo/Pilot signup.
- Messaging: hero emphasizes workflow orchestration + embedded AI + security; use clinician quotes and measurable benefits.
- Content pieces: explainer video (30–60s), product tour (3–5 min), downloadable one-pagers, case studies, security brief.
- CTAs: Request Demo, Start Pilot, Download Whitepaper, Register for CME.
- SEO: target keywords: “teleconsult platform for doctors Canada”, “EHR integration telemedicine”, “AI diagnostics clinical workflow”.
- Success metrics: demo requests/page, resource downloads, time-on-page, conversion rate on CTA.
- Compliance notes: host security/privacy statements, HIPAA/PIPEDA badges, data residency FAQ; ensure demo registration collects only permitted info.

Targeted email
- Goal: nurture clinicians from awareness → demo → pilot.
- Segments: new leads (conference or download), webinar attendees, pilot prospects, existing users for upsell.
- Sequence examples:
  - Lead welcome: 1) intro + 30s demo, 2) clinician case study, 3) invite to CME webinar, 4) pilot offer.
  - Post-webinar: thank you + on-demand recording + demo invitation.
- Cadence: initial lead sequence over 3 weeks (3–5 touches), webinar sequences 2–3 touches, pilot nurture weekly until decision.
- Content: short subject lines, clinician-centric benefits, clear CTA to schedule a demo or sign pilot.
- Metrics: open rate, click-through rate, demo scheduling rate, unsubscribe rate.
- Compliance: opt-in only, clear privacy policy links, data retention per PIPEDA.

LinkedIn (organic + paid)
- Goal: build clinician credibility, generate targeted demo requests and pilot leads.
- Organic tactics:
  - Weekly posts: short clinical tips, product feature highlight, clinician quote, link to case study.
  - Monthly long-form post or LinkedIn article on workflow efficiency and AI validation.
  - Employee advocacy: equip clinical co-founders and engineers with approved posts.
- Paid tactics:
  - Sponsored Content targeting physicians, clinic managers, and allied clinicians by specialty and geography.
  - Lead Gen Forms for webinar signups and demo requests.
- Creative: clinical visuals, short demo clips, quote cards with measurable outcomes.
- Cadence: 3–4 organic posts/week, 1–2 sponsored campaigns/month.
- Metrics: leads from LinkedIn, CPL, demo conversion rate, post engagement.
- Compliance: do not make unverified diagnostic claims; include links to technical validation or disclaimers.

Medical conferences & conferences playbook
- Goal: generate qualified leads, run pilot recruitment, build brand trust in clinical community.
- Pre-conference:
  - Announce sessions, schedule demos, promote CME webinar to attendees.
  - Secure speaking slot or panel with clinical champion to present workflow/AI validation.
  - Create a conference-specific offer: free 30-day pilot for attendees who book a demo.
- At-conference:
  - Live demo station with clinician-facing demo; short explainer videos on loop.
  - Printed one-pagers for practice managers + signed clinician case study cards.
  - Collect lead data in CRM and trigger immediate follow-up email within 24 hours.
- Post-conference:
  - Follow-up email sequence, personalized demo scheduling, set up pilot conversations.
- Metrics: leads collected, demos scheduled, pilot signups, cost per lead.
- Compliance: ensure any talks/presentations have appropriate peer-reviewed citations and legal review.

Clinician newsletters & professional publications
- Goal: reach problem-aware clinicians with credible thought leadership and product placement.
- Tactics:
  - Sponsored content/ads in high-open-rate clinician newsletters.
  - Contributed articles from clinical advisors about workflow transformation and AI governance.
- Content: teaser that links to a long-form whitepaper or webinar sign-up.
- Metrics: CTR, landing page conversions, demo requests.
- Compliance: editorial reviews; clinical authorship credentials included.

Partnerships with clinics & labs
- Goal: drive pilots, co-marketing, and integration referrals.
- Partnership types:
  - Pilot partnerships with clinics: co-developed success metrics, case study rights, pilot discounts.
  - Lab integrations: co-branded workflows to speed lab orders and result delivery; revenue-share on orchestration if applicable.
  - Referral partnerships: incentives for clinic networks and billing providers who refer paying clinics.
- Co-marketing assets: joint webinars, co-authored case studies, cross-promoted email to clinic networks.
- Onboarding: integration playbook, SLAs for data flow and security.
- Metrics: pilots initiated, partner-sourced MRR, lab integrations completed.
- Compliance: data-sharing agreements, MOUs; ensure partner marketing reviewed by legal.

Video & demo playbook
- Short explainer (30–60s) for home page and social: focus on clinician time saved and secure AI assistance.
- Product tour (3–5 min): show workflow orchestration, teleconsult flow, billing capture.
- Case-study videos (2–4 min): clinician testimonial + metrics.
- Distribution: website, LinkedIn, email, conference booth.
- Metrics: view-through rate, demo request after view, watch-to-demo conversion.

CME webinars / clinical validation events
- Goal: high-quality leads and credibility; attract physician audience and create content assets.
- Format: 60–90 min webinar with clinical moderator, product demo (15–20 min), Q&A, and survey.
- Incentive: CME credit or certificate, limited pilot slots.
- Follow-up: send recording + demo invite + survey; prioritize high engagement leads.
- Metrics: registrations, attendance rate, demo requests, pilot conversions.

Creative & compliance governance
- All assets must be pre-cleared by legal/compliance: privacy statements, AI performance claims, patient data usage.
- Clinical review: any content that references outcomes or diagnostics must be reviewed by clinical advisory board.
- Brand guidelines: modern, clinical-credible visual language; use clinician photography, avoid stock-y generic patient images where possible.
