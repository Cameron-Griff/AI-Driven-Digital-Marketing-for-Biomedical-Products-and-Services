# 2) Platform-Specific Playbooks

## Direct-to-patient app playbook
**Goal:** Drive first-time use and repeat engagement.

**Primary message**
- “Not sure what care you need? Get clear, safety-first guidance in minutes.”

**Best content**
- App store screenshots showing the triage flow
- Short onboarding video
- Symptom-specific entry points
- Push notifications for saved follow-ups
- Trust page with privacy and emergency disclaimers

**Creative guidance**
- Use calm colors, simple icons, and large readable text.
- Show the user journey: symptom → questions → explanation → recommended next step.
- Avoid overly “AI” language; emphasize safety and clarity.

**CTA examples**
- Start symptom check
- Check symptoms now
- See recommended care options

**Conversion tips**
- Keep the first screen frictionless.
- Make emergency escalation visible early.
- Show “why this recommendation” immediately after triage.
- Offer a clear next-action button to urgent care, primary care, telehealth, or emergency services depending on result.

## Clinic integration playbook
**Goal:** Reduce front-desk burden and improve patient routing.

**Primary message**
- “Help patients arrive at the right level of care before they call, book, or walk in.”

**Best content**
- Front-desk workflow sheet
- Patient intake QR code poster
- Staff training deck
- Compliance and oversight summary
- Implementation one-pager for administrators

**Placement ideas**
- Pre-visit patient portal
- QR code on reception signage
- SMS link in appointment reminders
- Embedded intake step for new patients

**Operational considerations**
- Clarify that staff can review outputs but should not treat the tool as diagnostic.
- Add escalation protocols for concerning symptom patterns.
- Provide a handoff summary that is easy to scan and document.

**CTA examples**
- Add triage to intake
- Request a pilot
- See clinic workflow demo

## Telehealth integration playbook
**Goal:** Improve booking quality and prioritize urgent cases.

**Primary message**
- “Route patients to the right virtual or in-person care faster, with safety-first triage.”

**Best content**
- Provider intake summary example
- Booking flow mockup
- Escalation workflow diagram
- Partner ROI snapshot
- Safety and governance brief

**Placement ideas**
- Before scheduling
- During pre-visit symptom collection
- In post-visit follow-up recommendations
- On telehealth provider landing pages

**Operational considerations**
- Trigger immediate escalation when red-flag symptoms appear.
- Summarize triage context for clinicians in a concise format.
- Ensure the system supports oversight, auditability, and privacy controls.

**CTA examples**
- Add triage to booking
- View provider workflow
- Start a pilot

## Healthcare system administrator playbook
**Goal:** Establish strategic pilots and scale adoption.

**Primary message**
- “Reduce avoidable ED congestion while improving patient navigation and safety.”

**Best content**
- Executive brief
- ROI and capacity planning summary
- Safety/compliance overview
- Pilot success metrics
- Stakeholder map

**Selling points**
- Better routing to the right care setting
- Reduced unnecessary ED volume
- Standardized, explainable triage pathway
- Governance-ready deployment model

## Trust and safety playbook
**Goal:** Prevent misunderstanding and reinforce legitimacy.

**Must-have elements**
- Plain-language disclaimer: not a diagnosis tool
- Emergency warning banner
- Privacy and data-use summary
- Clinical oversight statement
- Region-specific compliance references where required

**Recommended copy pattern**
- “This tool helps guide care choices. It does not diagnose conditions. If you think this may be an emergency, call local emergency services now.”
