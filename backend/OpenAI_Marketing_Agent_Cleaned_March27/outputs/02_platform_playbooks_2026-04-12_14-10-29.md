# 2) Platform-Specific Playbooks

## 1. Website / Landing Page
### Goal
Convert awareness into demo requests, pilots, or embedded-use inquiries.

### Page structure
- Hero section: clear value proposition + safety-first disclaimer
- Interactive symptom-check preview
- Use cases by audience segment
- Compliance and privacy section
- FAQ: limitations, escalation, data handling
- CTA: request demo / start pilot / talk to solutions team

### Sample headline
“Help patients choose the right care level quickly, safely, and with confidence.”

### CTA ideas
- “See the triage flow”
- “Request a pilot”
- “Talk to our team”

### Best practices
- Add prominent emergency warning language
- Avoid any diagnosis phrasing
- Use plain-language explanations
- Include HIPAA/PIPEDA-friendly trust messaging

## 2. LinkedIn
### Goal
Reach healthcare administrators, clinic leaders, telehealth operators, and provider decision-makers.

### Content types
- Founder or product thought leadership
- Operational insight posts on ED overcrowding
- Short videos showing explainable triage logic
- Carousel posts: “5 ways to reduce avoidable emergency visits”
- Client pilot outcomes and process lessons

### Tone
Professional, evidence-minded, trust-oriented, outcome-driven

### CTA ideas
- “Book a demo”
- “See how triage routing works”
- “Explore patient flow use cases”

## 3. Email nurturing
### Goal
Convert interested stakeholders through trust-building education.

### Segmentation
- Patients/caregivers
- Clinic and telehealth operators
- Administrators and procurement stakeholders

### Sequence examples
#### For healthcare buyers
1. Problem overview: care navigation and ED overload
2. Solution overview: explainable, non-diagnostic triage
3. Safety/compliance deep dive
4. Use case and workflow integration
5. Pilot invitation

#### For patients
1. “When should you seek urgent care?”
2. “How the triage tool works”
3. “What the tool can and cannot do”
4. “When to seek emergency help immediately”

### Best practices
- Keep claims conservative
- Use reassurance without overpromising
- Repeat privacy and emergency guidance where appropriate

## 4. Short-form video
### Goal
Demonstrate ease of use and build confidence.

### Video concepts
- “What happens when symptoms feel unclear?”
- “How digital triage helps route care safely”
- “When to choose urgent care vs the ED”
- “What explainable triage means”

### Format
- 30–60 seconds
- Clear captions
- Simple interface mockups
- End with safety disclaimer and CTA

## 5. Blog / SEO
### Goal
Capture problem-aware search intent.

### Target topics
- “When should I go to the emergency room?”
- “Urgent care vs emergency room: how to decide”
- “How to reduce emergency department overcrowding”
- “What is symptom triage?”
- “How digital triage supports safer care navigation”

### SEO guardrails
- No diagnostic claims
- Medical review recommended
- Update content regularly for accuracy
- Include emergency escalation guidance on each page

## 6. Partnerships / B2B healthcare outreach
### Goal
Secure pilots with clinics, telehealth providers, and health systems.

### Partner message
“Embed a safe, explainable triage layer into patient intake to improve routing, reduce congestion, and support responsible escalation.”

### Outreach angles
- Operational efficiency
- Patient satisfaction
- Access expansion
- Compliance-conscious design
- Integration flexibility
