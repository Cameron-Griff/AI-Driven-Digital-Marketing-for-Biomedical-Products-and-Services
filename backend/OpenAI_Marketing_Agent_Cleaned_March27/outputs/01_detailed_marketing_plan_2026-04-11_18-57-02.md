# 1) Detailed Marketing Plan

Assumptions (explicit)
- Pricing: assume tiered per-provider SaaS + transaction fee for billing/teleconsult capture; pilot discounts available. If different, adjust acquisition offers.
- Budget & team: assume a modest growth marketing budget and a small internal team (1 product marketer, 1 content creator, 1 growth marketer, outsourced design/video + a channel partnerships lead).
- Sales capacity: assume a lean sales/dev-ops team running demos and 10–20 integration pilots concurrently.
- Timing: 12-week initial campaign to drive measurable adoption; ongoing cadence thereafter.
- Regulatory/Legal: assume product meets PIPEDA/HIPAA-equivalent technical controls; marketing must avoid clinical claims beyond supported evidence.

Goal summary (primary)
- Drive doctor adoption, increase teleconsult volume, and simplify billing/revenue capture by convincing practicing physicians and clinic managers to try and adopt the platform.

Success metrics (KPIs)
- Top-level: number of new clinician signups (target: +150–300 clinicians in 12 months), number of teleconsult sessions (target +X% quarter-over-quarter), monthly recurring revenue (MRR) growth.
- Funnel metrics: demo requests, pilot conversions (pilot → paid), activation rate (login + first teleconsult within 14 days), retention (30/90-day active clinician%), billing capture rate (percent of billable consults captured).
- Content metrics: email open/click rates, demo video view-through, webinar registrations/attendees, conference lead conversion.

Positioning & messaging pillars
- Core position: “A secure, clinician-first orchestration layer that centralizes EHR, teleconsults, AI-assisted diagnostics, labs and billing—so clinicians spend less time administrating and more time treating.”
- Pillar 1 — Workflow efficiency: fewer clicks, fewer systems, faster visit throughput.
- Pillar 2 — Clinical confidence: embedded AI supports diagnostics — evidence-based, clinician-validated, auditable.
- Pillar 3 — Revenue integrity: integrated billing and lab orchestration reduces leakage and accelerates capture.
- Pillar 4 — Privacy & governance: enterprise-grade encryption, regional data residency, clinical governance and audit trails.
- Tone: modern, tech-forward, authoritative and patient-centric; prioritize clinician credibility and privacy assurances.

Target segments & value propositions
- Practicing physicians (primary): “Reduce administrative burden and get faster diagnostic support so you can see more patients with better outcomes.”
- Clinic managers / practice owners: “Centralize workflows, simplify billing and improve practice revenue capture.”
- Labs & referral partners: “Seamless orchestration and faster turnaround, enabling referral networks and fewer manual steps.”
- Allied clinicians & billing staff: “Simpler scheduling, one source of truth, and automated billing handoffs.”

Acquisition strategy
- Demand gen mix: inbound content (SEO & website), targeted email sequences, LinkedIn outreach (thought leadership + sponsored posts), webinars with CME credits, presence at medical conferences, partnerships with clinics and labs for pilot programs.
- Lead magnets: clinician case studies, whitepapers on AI-assist clinical validation, CME webinar registration (lead capture), pilot program signup form and one-pagers for practice managers.
- Paid tactics (targeted): LinkedIn Sponsored Content to physicians and clinic managers in Ontario/Canadian provinces; programmatic banners on clinician portals/newsletters; conference sponsorships.

Sales & conversion funnel
- Awareness → Consideration → Trial/Pilot → Activation → Expansion.
- Entry points: content download, webinar signup, LinkedIn ad, conference contact, partner referral.
- Conversion tactics: live demo (sales engineer + clinical champion), 30–60 day pilot with success criteria (reduced admin time, billing capture increase, teleconsult adoption), onboarding playbook and dedicated onboarding manager.
- Incentives: limited-time pilot discount, first 3 months waived for early clinic partners, CME webinar attendance qualifies as a lead nurture priority.

Content mix & formats
- Product demos (short explainer + longer demo for sales).
- Clinician case studies (video + one-pager).
- Whitepapers & technical briefs (data residency, security, AI validation).
- Quick explainer videos (30–90s) for social & website.
- CME-accredited webinars (driven by clinical advisory board).
- One-pagers tailored to practice managers and lab partners.
- Newsletter snippets targeted to clinician audiences.

Retention & expansion
- Activation: onboarding checklist, milestone emails, in-product tips, quick-start playbook for clinic managers.
- Ongoing engagement: monthly clinical updates, feature highlights, new AI model validation reports, referral incentives.
- Upsell: advanced analytics, lab orchestration premium, network referrals; present ROI reports to clinic managers.
- Support: dedicated clinician success manager for pilots and early adopters; clinician advisory board to steer product trust.

Regulatory & compliance considerations (marketing)
- Avoid unsubstantiated clinical claims; use clinician testimonials and case data with consent.
- Prominently state PIPEDA/HIPAA-equivalent compliance, data residency options, encryption standards, and auditability.
- Collaborate with legal for any content referencing diagnostic accuracy of AI; include disclaimers and clinical governance notes in whitepapers and demos.

Operational checklist (immediate next steps)
- Finalize pilot offer & success metrics; create onboarding playbook.
- Produce cornerstone assets: one demo video, two clinician case studies, a whitepaper on AI validation, CME webinar landing page.
- Build targeted email nurture sequences and LinkedIn ad creatives.
- Prepare compliance-approved messaging docs and legal signoffs.
