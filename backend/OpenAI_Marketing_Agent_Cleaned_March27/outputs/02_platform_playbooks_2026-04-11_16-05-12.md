# 2) Platform-Specific Playbooks

Website landing pages
- Objective: Convert clinic admins to demo requests and downloads (whitepaper/ROI calculator).
- Pages to create: Homepage hero (AI + workflows), Security & Compliance page, Solutions by role (Admin / Clinician / IT), Pilot program landing page, Case studies page, Pricing outline.
- Messaging hooks (examples): "Unify care delivery and admin operations — faster diagnostics with model-level transparency." "Piloted in clinics across 3 countries — see measurable admin time savings."
- CTAs: Request demo, Download whitepaper, Start pilot, Book security review.
- Conversion elements: short demo video (hero), social proof (150+ doctors), clinical advisory board badge, model-evaluation sample PDF, ROI calculator.
- SEO & content: Pages optimized for keywords: "telemedicine EHR Canada", "clinic EHR with AI diagnosis", "PIPEDA-compliant telemedicine." Publish 2–4 SEO blog posts/month about AI governance, telehealth workflows, and case study highlights.
- KPIs: Landing conversion rate ≥6% for demo; whitepaper downloads; bounce rate <60%; demo video playthrough rate.

LinkedIn & healthcare professional networks (detailed)
- Objective: Establish clinical credibility, drive demo requests, generate outbound leads.
- Tactics:
  - Company page: Weekly posts — 1 thought leadership article/month (CEO + clinician co-authored), 2–3 short posts (product features, security claims).
  - Sponsored content: Promote whitepaper + webinar to clinic admin and healthcare managers (seniority targeting).
  - Thought leadership: CEO + Clinical Advisor multi-post series: "Safe AI in clinic workflows" (3 posts + infographic).
  - Employee advocacy: Equip sales and leadership with post templates and a calendar.
  - Healthcare networks (Doximity-like, local medical association platforms): Sponsored educational posts and event listings.
- Ad targeting: Job titles (Clinic Manager, Administrator, Medical Director, IT Lead), industry: Healthcare, company size filters (1–50 clinicians and 50–200), geolocation: Canada + target international markets.
- KPIs: CTR >0.45% for sponsored posts, Cost per demo <$200 (goal), leads from LinkedIn → % demos.

Email campaigns
- Objective: Nurture MQLs to demo and pilot.
- Sequences:
  - Cold outbound (3-touch): Intro (value + social proof + pilot offer) → Case study share → Call to action to book a short 20-min demo.
  - Demo follow-up (4-touch): Thanks + short video recap → ROI calculator → Pilot proposal → Final reminder.
  - Pilot nurture (ongoing): Weekly touchpoints with pilot KPIs, success checklist, clinician tips, and technical onboarding items.
  - Drip for leads (educational): Weekly or biweekly distribution on AI governance, security, and clinician testimonials.
- Example subject lines: "Reduce admin time by 30% — pilot offer for clinics", "How our ML model reports improve clinical confidence", "Quick demo? Secure, integrated EHR + telemedicine".
- Compliance: All outbound must include opt-out and follow CASL/PIPEDA email rules for Canada.
- KPIs: Open rate >25% (cold), CTR >3% (nurture), meetings booked per sequence 2–6%.

Medical conferences & events
- Objective: Generate high-quality leads, speak to clinical audiences, secure pilot partners.
- Tactics:
  - Targeted conferences in Canada and regionals with clinical track. Apply to speak on "Implementing responsible AI in outpatient clinics" (panel + case study).
  - Booth playbook: Demo station (clinician + admin workflows), printed one-pagers (compliance brief), tablet signup for demos, scheduled on-site demo times.
  - Pre-event outreach: Email invitations to target list offering 15-minute live demos and pilot discounts.
  - Post-event follow-up: 72-hour sequence with recorded webinar + case study download + CTA to book pilot.
- Conference collateral: 5-min product demo video, clinician testimonial poster, compliance one-pager, sample model evaluation reports.
- KPIs: Qualified leads per event (target 20–50), demo bookings post-event, pilot starts resulting from event.

Targeted clinic outreach (outbound & SDR play)
- Objective: Secure pilot agreements and paid deployments.
- Play components:
  - Build ICP list: clinics with 2–10 providers, clinics using legacy EHRs, clinics in regions with telemedicine growth.
  - Outbound cadence (for clinic admins): LinkedIn connect → 3-email sequence over 10 days → call + voicemails → product demo.
  - Pilot offer framework: 8–12 week pilot with KPI targets (reduction in admin time, consult throughput, satisfaction). Offer discounted pilot with SOW and success criteria.
  - Objection handling:
    - "AI isn’t trustworthy": Show model-evaluation reports, clinician co-authored whitepaper, human-in-loop workflow and audit logs.
    - "Regulatory concerns": Show security page, PIPEDA/HIPAA alignment, and roadmap to SOC 2.
    - "Integration complexity": Offer sandbox demo and integration checklist; highlight fast browser-only deployment.
  - Playbook assets: Pitch deck for admins (cost/time savings), demo scripts for clinicians, technical integration checklist for IT leads.
- KPIs: Calls per SDR/day (target 30–40), demos per month per SDR (6–8), pilot conversion rate (20–40% from demos based on outreach quality).

Content formats & creative guidance
- Product demo: 5–7 minutes, role-based views; include AI model-report walkthrough and auditability features.
- Explainer videos: 60–90s for landing pages and ads focusing on clinician and admin benefits.
- Whitepapers: Clinical/technical whitepaper with method, dataset size, evaluation metrics for the ML models; co-author with clinician.
- Case studies: 1–2-page one-pagers + full case study PDF showing measurable savings and clinical outcomes.
- Webinars: 30–45 min with clinician panel, demo, and Q&A. Use webinars as primary lead-gen drivers.

Ad and copy best practices
- Headlines: Lead with pain point and benefit: "Unify telemedicine + records — speed diagnostics with transparent AI."
- Ad copy variants to A/B test:
  - Feature-led: "Integrated ML model library with evaluation reports — deployed in clinics now."
  - Outcome-led: "Cut admin time by X% and improve diagnostic turnaround — book a demo."
  - Trust-led: "PIPEDA/HIPAA-aligned. Clinical advisory board oversight."
- Landing page variants: long-form (for whitepaper landing) vs. short-form (for demo signup). Test social-proof placement.

Analytics & testing plan
- A/B test landing pages (video vs. static hero), CTA wording, and email subject lines.
- Track downstream impact of content on pilot conversions (which whitepaper/webinar led to pilot?).
- Weekly review of ad performance and monthly content performance review.

Regulatory & legal playbook (quick checklist)
- Mandatory legal review of all claims around diagnostic accuracy and model performance.
- Include disclaimers: ML outputs are decision-support, not definitive diagnoses; clinicians retain final judgment.
- Create a one-page compliance brief for prospect IT/legal teams (data flow, storage, encryption, incident response).

Sales + marketing handoff
- Standardized lead scoring for MQL → SQL with required fields (clinic size, decision timeline, budget, compliance concerns).
- Demo recording templates and meeting notes stored in CRM; pilot SOW templates pre-approved by legal.
