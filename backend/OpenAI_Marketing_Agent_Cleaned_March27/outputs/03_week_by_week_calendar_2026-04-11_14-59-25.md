# 3) Week-by-Week Content Calendar

Assumptions for calendar
- Start date: Week 1 = campaign kick‑off week.
- Duration: 12 weeks sprint.
- Team: marketing lead (M), content producer (C), product/engineering (P), clinical liaison (CL), growth/ads specialist (G), external video agency (V).
- Budget: CAD 50k–100k over 12 weeks (as previously assumed).

| Week | Focus / Objective | Content & Deliverables | Channel(s) | Owner | KPI (target) |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Core setup & messaging | Finalize hero messaging, privacy copy, analytics events, draft landing page wireframes, kickoff explainer video | Internal, website | M / P / C | Analytics events defined; landing wireframes ready (100%) |
| 2 | Asset production | Produce explainer video storyboard; design patient + clinician landing pages; draft FAQ (privacy & AI) | Website, video agency | C / V / P | Video storyboard approved; 2 pages ready for dev |
| 3 | Build & QA | Implement landing pages, embed privacy/legal, set up tracking; app store promo assets start | Website, Analytics, App Stores | P / M | Pages live (staging→prod); tracking QA complete |
| 4 | Soft launch — patient acquisition | Publish patient landing, launch 2 organic LinkedIn posts, deploy initial patient email welcome flow (5 emails) | Website, Email, LinkedIn | M / G / C | 500 visits; 50 auth signups; email open rate 25% |
| 5 | Clinician outreach start | Publish clinician landing and ROI one‑pager; host first clinician webinar demo; LinkedIn sponsored content to clinicians | Website, LinkedIn, Webinar | CL / M / G | 50 webinar registrations; 10 demos booked |
| 6 | App & ASO | Publish app to stores; push app promo video and screenshots; announce via email to pilot prospects | App Stores, Email | P / C | 200 installs; install→activation 30% |
| 7 | Case study production | Draft anonymized pilot case study (early metrics) and clinician testimonial video shoot | Website, Video | CL / V / C | Case study draft complete; 1 testimonial video produced |
| 8 | Scale paid & retargeting | Launch LinkedIn paid to clinician segments; launch search/generic patient ads with retargeting set; launch re-engagement emails to unactivated users | LinkedIn, Search, Email, Retargeting | G / M | CPL (clinician) target CAD 150; patient CPA < CAD 60 |
| 9 | Publish case study & PR | Publish case study landing + press release; share results on LinkedIn and email to existing contacts | Website, LinkedIn, Email, PR | M / CL | 1 PR pickup; increase demo requests by 20% |
| 10 | Conference prep & outreach | Finalize conference collateral, schedule meetings with lab partners, send conference invites; run clinician testimonial posts | Events, LinkedIn, Email | M / CL | 30 pre‑booked meetings; 100 leads targeted |
| 11 | Optimize & iterate | Analyze ad performance, A/B test landing hero (security vs convenience), update creatives based on performance | Website, Ads, Email | G / M / C | Lift landing conversion by 10% on winning variant |
| 12 | Scale & report | Push top performing channels; compile 12‑week performance report, publish follow‑up patient success story video, plan next quarter | Website, Email, LinkedIn, Report | M / P / CL | Summary report delivered; week 12 activations +20% vs baseline |

Notes on weekly execution
- Weekly Standups: 30‑minute sync every Monday to track owners and blockers.
- Tracking: Each CTA must include UTM; CRM lead source enforced.
- Rapid feedback loop: Weekly ad & landing performance review to reallocate budget to best ROI.
- Consent & compliance checks: All patient stories and demos must have legal/clinical sign‑off before publishing.

Primary CTAs across calendar
- Patients: Create secure account → Book a consult → Order lab (post‑auth).
- Clinicians: Request demo → Join pilot → Book onboarding session.

End of calendar: deliver a measurement pack (dashboard + topline deck) and a prioritized backlog of next‑quarter experiments (e.g., expand to additional provinces, patient referral program, multilingual landing pages).
