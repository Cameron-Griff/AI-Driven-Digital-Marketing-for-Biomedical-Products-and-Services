# 1) Detailed Marketing Plan

Executive summary
- Positioning: A cloud eHealth platform that unifies telemedicine, EHR, admin workflows and an integrated ML-model library to speed diagnostics — sold to clinics and clinic administrators who need secure, workflow-focused, AI-augmented clinical systems.
- Primary objective: Acquire clinic and admin users and convert to paid SaaS deployments by demonstrating operational ROI, clinical credibility, and secure governance of AI diagnostics.
- Time horizon: 6–12 month initial growth push focused on proof points, pilot conversions, and building trust signals for wider adoption.

Core value proposition (single-sentence)
- "A secure, workflow-first eHealth platform that consolidates telemedicine and records while accelerating diagnostic confidence with integrated ML models and evaluation reporting — so clinics run safer, faster and with measurable clinical oversight."

Messaging pillars (audience-focused)
- For Clinic Administrators: Reduce admin overhead and appointment friction — centralized records, role-based workflows, simplified billing and faster throughput. Emphasize cost-savings and reduced no-shows.
- For Physicians / Clinical Users: Improve diagnostic workflow — integrated ML suggestions with explainability and model-evaluation reports; time saved during consults with unified patient data.
- For IT / Security Leads: Modern, browser-based SaaS with secure hosting, role-based auth, encryption and PIPEDA/HIPAA-aligned practices.
- For Patients (support collateral): Convenient virtual visits and consolidated records; data security and clinician oversight.

Primary differentiators to highlight
- Integrated ML model library + model-evaluation reporting (clinical transparency and auditability).
- Workflow-first design: telemedicine + EHR + admin in a single UI.
- Modern, cloud SaaS that is secure and role-based (fast deploy for clinics without heavy IT).

Target audiences & segments
- Primary: Clinic Administrators and Clinic Managers at small-to-medium outpatient clinics (multi-provider clinics, specialty clinics).
- Secondary: Physicians (family physicians, specialists), IT leads for healthcare organizations, procurement leads for multi-clinic groups.
- Tertiary: Early-adopter patients (for pilots) and clinical champions/research partners.

High-level GTM motions
- Inbound demand gen: Website landing pages, whitepapers, webinars, LinkedIn thought leadership and healthcare networks.
- Outbound sales: Targeted outreach (email + LinkedIn + phone) to clinic administrators and managers; account-based pilots for groups.
- Events & PR: Presence and speaking at regional medical conferences; publish clinical whitepapers with partner clinics; secure one or two clinical champions for case studies.
- Channel/partnership: Integrations with common billing/IMS systems, partner with clinical associations and telemedicine vendors for endorsements.

Sales model & funnel
- Lead capture → MQL via demos/whitepaper downloads → Nurture (email + LinkedIn) → Pilot (6–12 week paid/paid-with-discount) → Deployment (SaaS subscription, onboarding + success).
- Standard sales cycle assumption: 8–12 weeks for pilot-based deals; enterprise/multi-clinic 12–24 weeks.
- Offer: 30-day trial not recommended for clinics; prefer pilot programs with clear KPIs and onboarding to prove ROI.

KPI targets (initial 6 months) — assumptions listed below
- MQLs per month: 120
- Demo requests per month: 30 (25% conversion from MQL)
- Pilot starts per month: 6 (20% conversion from demos)
- Pilot-to-paid conversion: 50% within 3 months post-pilot
- CAC (marketing + sales) target: <$6k per clinic (assumption)
- Target ARR from new deployments in 12 months: $350k (assumption)

Pricing & commercial approach (recommended)
- Tiered subscription per clinic:
  - Starter (single provider clinic): Core EHR + telemedicine + basic admin
  - Clinic (multi-provider): Role-based workflows, reporting, integrations
  - Clinical AI bundle (add-on): Model library + evaluation reporting + audit logs
- Offer pilot pricing (discounted full-platform pilot with defined KPI metrics) to overcome skepticism.
- Per-provider or per-clinic seat pricing; include implementation fee and optional premium onboarding.

Trust, compliance & clinical governance plan
- Immediately publish a “Security & Compliance” landing page that highlights encryption, role-based auth, data residency options, PIPEDA/HIPAA alignment, SOC 2 roadmap, and a clinical governance framework for ML models (model evaluation reports, versioning, human-in-loop workflows).
- Launch clinical advisory board (3–5 clinician leaders) and publish members to lend credibility.
- Clinical validation plan: pair with 1–2 pilot clinics to co-author a whitepaper/case study demonstrating model performance and workflow impact.
- Legal/regulatory: All claims about ML diagnostic accuracy and compliance must be reviewed by clinical and legal teams. Avoid regulatory guarantees until certifications are in place.

Content & demand generation plan
- Hero assets: Technical whitepaper on ML model evaluation + clinician-authored case study + product demo video (5 min).
- Lead magnets: ROI calculator for clinic admins, checklist for safe AI adoption in clinics, PIPEDA/HIPAA compliance checklist for SaaS vendors.
- Engagement: Monthly webinars featuring clinical champions, 1-2 short explainer videos per quarter, and targeted email nurture sequences.
- Thought leadership: LinkedIn articles from CEO/CMO and clinician advisors about safe AI adoption and integration into workflows.

Sales enablement & operations
- Sales playbooks for inbound demo, pilot kickoff, pilot KPIs, objection handling (especially AI skepticism/regs), and pricing negotiation.
- Demo environment with role-based flows (admin view, clinician view, patient view) and simulated AI model reports.
- Onboarding templates and a standard SOW for pilots.

Measurement & analytics
- Track funnel conversion rates, CAC, LTV, pilot KPIs (reduction in admin time, consult time saved), time-to-first-value, churn, NPS among clinicians.
- Instrument UTM, content heatmaps, and conversion funnels on landing pages. Weekly SDR and marketing syncs to optimize messaging.

Assumptions (explicit)
- Pricing: assumed average contract value (ACV) per clinic = CAD 18,000/year (≈USD 13–14k). This is a placeholder; adjust to finance inputs.
- Budget: assumed monthly marketing budget = CAD 25k (ads, content production, events, agency); sales team = 2 field reps + 1 SDR. If budget or team differs, funnel targets should be re-projected.
- Sales/Conversion assumptions: MQL → demo 25%; demo → pilot 20%; pilot → paid 50%; average sales cycle 8–12 weeks. These are conservative startup-stage numbers.
- Compliance/certification status: No SOC 2 / formal certifications yet. Plan assumes certification roadmap: SOC 2 within 6–9 months; PIPEDA/HIPAA compliance statements without certification.
- Clinical evidence: Only modest adoption exists (3+ countries, 150+ doctors). This will be used as early credibility but broader validation (case studies/whitepapers) will be required.
- Language & region: English-first targeting Canada and international markets; all content produced in English initially.

Resourcing & timelines (high level)
- Month 0–2: Build security/compliance page, produce demo video, create ROI calculator, launch LinkedIn thought leadership and targeted paid campaigns; recruit clinical advisory board; begin pilot outreach.
- Month 3–6: Run pilots, publish 1–2 case studies and a clinical whitepaper, host first webinar, attend 1–2 regional medical conferences.
- Month 6–12: SOC 2 + more formalized certifications (if prioritized), scale inbound demand gen, refine pricing and enterprise sales plays.

Risks & mitigations
- Risk: Clinician skepticism about AI diagnostics. Mitigation: Publish model-evaluation reports, human-in-loop workflows, and clinician-authored validation content.
- Risk: Regulatory/compliance concerns slow purchasing. Mitigation: Clear security page, PIPEDA/HIPAA alignment statements, third-party security audits on roadmap.
- Risk: Limited adoption numbers may impede credibility. Mitigation: Use pilot case studies and promote measurable operational ROI.

Deliverables to build first 90 days
- Sales demo kit + demo environment
- Security & compliance page and downloadable compliance brief
- ROI calculator landing page
- 1 clinician co-authored case study and 1 ML evaluation whitepaper
- 90-day outreach sequence for 300 target clinics (email + LinkedIn + phone) with pilot offer

# 2) Platform-Specific Playbooks

Website landing pages
- Objective: Convert clinic admins to demo requests and downloads (whitepaper/ROI calculator).
- Pages to create: Homepage hero (AI + workflows), Security & Compliance page, Solutions by role (Admin / Clinician / IT), Pilot program landing page, Case studies page, Pricing outline.
- Messaging hooks (examples): "Unify care delivery and admin operations — faster diagnostics with model-level transparency." "Piloted in clinics across 3 countries — see measurable admin time savings."
- CTAs: Request demo, Download whitepaper, Start pilot, Book security review.
- Conversion elements: short demo video (hero), social proof (150+ doctors), clinical advisory board badge, model-evaluation sample PDF, ROI calculator.
- SEO & content: Pages optimized for keywords: "telemedicine EHR Canada", "clinic EHR with AI diagnosis", "PIPEDA-compliant telemedicine." Publish 2–4 SEO blog posts/month about AI governance, telehealth workflows, and case study highlights.
- KPIs: Landing conversion rate ≥6% for demo; whitepaper downloads; bounce rate <60%; demo video playthrough rate.

LinkedIn & healthcare professional networks (detailed)
- Objective: Establish clinical credibility, drive demo requests, generate outbound leads.
- Tactics:
  - Company page: Weekly posts — 1 thought leadership article/month (CEO + clinician co-authored), 2–3 short posts (product features, security claims).
  - Sponsored content: Promote whitepaper + webinar to clinic admin and healthcare managers (seniority targeting).
  - Thought leadership: CEO + Clinical Advisor multi-post series: "Safe AI in clinic workflows" (3 posts + infographic).
  - Employee advocacy: Equip sales and leadership with post templates and a calendar.
  - Healthcare networks (Doximity-like, local medical association platforms): Sponsored educational posts and event listings.
- Ad targeting: Job titles (Clinic Manager, Administrator, Medical Director, IT Lead), industry: Healthcare, company size filters (1–50 clinicians and 50–200), geolocation: Canada + target international markets.
- KPIs: CTR >0.45% for sponsored posts, Cost per demo <$200 (goal), leads from LinkedIn → % demos.

Email campaigns
- Objective: Nurture MQLs to demo and pilot.
- Sequences:
  - Cold outbound (3-touch): Intro (value + social proof + pilot offer) → Case study share → Call to action to book a short 20-min demo.
  - Demo follow-up (4-touch): Thanks + short video recap → ROI calculator → Pilot proposal → Final reminder.
  - Pilot nurture (ongoing): Weekly touchpoints with pilot KPIs, success checklist, clinician tips, and technical onboarding items.
  - Drip for leads (educational): Weekly or biweekly distribution on AI governance, security, and clinician testimonials.
- Example subject lines: "Reduce admin time by 30% — pilot offer for clinics", "How our ML model reports improve clinical confidence", "Quick demo? Secure, integrated EHR + telemedicine".
- Compliance: All outbound must include opt-out and follow CASL/PIPEDA email rules for Canada.
- KPIs: Open rate >25% (cold), CTR >3% (nurture), meetings booked per sequence 2–6%.

Medical conferences & events
- Objective: Generate high-quality leads, speak to clinical audiences, secure pilot partners.
- Tactics:
  - Targeted conferences in Canada and regionals with clinical track. Apply to speak on "Implementing responsible AI in outpatient clinics" (panel + case study).
  - Booth playbook: Demo station (clinician + admin workflows), printed one-pagers (compliance brief), tablet signup for demos, scheduled on-site demo times.
  - Pre-event outreach: Email invitations to target list offering 15-minute live demos and pilot discounts.
  - Post-event follow-up: 72-hour sequence with recorded webinar + case study download + CTA to book pilot.
- Conference collateral: 5-min product demo video, clinician testimonial poster, compliance one-pager, sample model evaluation reports.
- KPIs: Qualified leads per event (target 20–50), demo bookings post-event, pilot starts resulting from event.

Targeted clinic outreach (outbound & SDR play)
- Objective: Secure pilot agreements and paid deployments.
- Play components:
  - Build ICP list: clinics with 2–10 providers, clinics using legacy EHRs, clinics in regions with telemedicine growth.
  - Outbound cadence (for clinic admins): LinkedIn connect → 3-email sequence over 10 days → call + voicemails → product demo.
  - Pilot offer framework: 8–12 week pilot with KPI targets (reduction in admin time, consult throughput, satisfaction). Offer discounted pilot with SOW and success criteria.
  - Objection handling:
    - "AI isn’t trustworthy": Show model-evaluation reports, clinician co-authored whitepaper, human-in-loop workflow and audit logs.
    - "Regulatory concerns": Show security page, PIPEDA/HIPAA alignment, and roadmap to SOC 2.
    - "Integration complexity": Offer sandbox demo and integration checklist; highlight fast browser-only deployment.
  - Playbook assets: Pitch deck for admins (cost/time savings), demo scripts for clinicians, technical integration checklist for IT leads.
- KPIs: Calls per SDR/day (target 30–40), demos per month per SDR (6–8), pilot conversion rate (20–40% from demos based on outreach quality).

Content formats & creative guidance
- Product demo: 5–7 minutes, role-based views; include AI model-report walkthrough and auditability features.
- Explainer videos: 60–90s for landing pages and ads focusing on clinician and admin benefits.
- Whitepapers: Clinical/technical whitepaper with method, dataset size, evaluation metrics for the ML models; co-author with clinician.
- Case studies: 1–2-page one-pagers + full case study PDF showing measurable savings and clinical outcomes.
- Webinars: 30–45 min with clinician panel, demo, and Q&A. Use webinars as primary lead-gen drivers.

Ad and copy best practices
- Headlines: Lead with pain point and benefit: "Unify telemedicine + records — speed diagnostics with transparent AI."
- Ad copy variants to A/B test:
  - Feature-led: "Integrated ML model library with evaluation reports — deployed in clinics now."
  - Outcome-led: "Cut admin time by X% and improve diagnostic turnaround — book a demo."
  - Trust-led: "PIPEDA/HIPAA-aligned. Clinical advisory board oversight."
- Landing page variants: long-form (for whitepaper landing) vs. short-form (for demo signup). Test social-proof placement.

Analytics & testing plan
- A/B test landing pages (video vs. static hero), CTA wording, and email subject lines.
- Track downstream impact of content on pilot conversions (which whitepaper/webinar led to pilot?).
- Weekly review of ad performance and monthly content performance review.

Regulatory & legal playbook (quick checklist)
- Mandatory legal review of all claims around diagnostic accuracy and model performance.
- Include disclaimers: ML outputs are decision-support, not definitive diagnoses; clinicians retain final judgment.
- Create a one-page compliance brief for prospect IT/legal teams (data flow, storage, encryption, incident response).

Sales + marketing handoff
- Standardized lead scoring for MQL → SQL with required fields (clinic size, decision timeline, budget, compliance concerns).
- Demo recording templates and meeting notes stored in CRM; pilot SOW templates pre-approved by legal.
