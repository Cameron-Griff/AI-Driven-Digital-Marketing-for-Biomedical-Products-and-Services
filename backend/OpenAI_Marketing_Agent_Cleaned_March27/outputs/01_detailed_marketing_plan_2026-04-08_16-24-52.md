# 1) Detailed Marketing Plan

Summary objective
- Primary goal: Drive sales of official roundnet kits and accessories while growing community engagement and event participation across North America (with Canadian localization/CAD pricing).
- Success metrics: Revenue from kits & accessories, conversion rate (site), average order value (AOV), repeat purchase rate, email list growth, social follower growth, event registrations/attendance, UGC volume.

Core positioning & messaging pillars
- Play: “Pick up. Set up. Play.” — fast setup, instant fun.
- Community: Events, local groups, and contests that make it easy to find players.
- Portability & durability: Lightweight, travel-ready, built to last.
- Official leader: Branded, tournament-ready gear — trust & authority.

Target audiences (priority)
- Friends groups / college students — social and viral play, low-price sensitivity but high social proof needs.
- Families — safety, durability, easy learning for kids.
- Casual outdoor enthusiasts — portability, convenience.
- Community organizers & competitive players — reliability, official status for events.

Primary channels
- E‑commerce site (product + bundles)
- Instagram, TikTok, YouTube (short + long)
- Email marketing (automations + promos)
- Event activations (pop-ups, tournaments, retail demos)
- Retail partners (POS, demo units)

Assumptions (explicit)
- Campaign length assumed: 8-week seasonal push (spring → summer). Adjust timeline for exact seasonality.
- Marketing budget assumed: mid-tier (enough for paid social + micro+macro influencer seeding + 4–6 in-city event activations). If budget differs, reallocate paid/influencer/event spend.
- Team/resourcing: in-house creative + 1 freelance video editor + community manager + partnerships manager.
- Email list size & historical metrics are unknown — assume a starting list of 25k-75k subscribers and 1–2% weekly organic social follower growth. If actual numbers differ, KPIs must be recalibrated.
- Events: assume ability to run 4 regional activations across major NA cities during the campaign window.
- Compliance: All promotions will follow regional sweepstakes/laws, minors in creative require releases/guardian consent, and privacy/cookie compliance must be enforced.

Tactics & activation mix
- Acquisition: Paid social (short-form video), influencer seeding (UGC-first), SEO+PPC for product keywords, PR for official leader messaging.
- Conversion: Product page optimization (video hero, bundles, testimonials, clear shipping/CAD pricing), time-limited bundle offers, targeted email flows (welcome, cart recovery, post-purchase).
- Retention & community: Local events & tournaments, user-generated content challenges, ambassador program, loyalty perks (discounts for event sign-ups/referrals).
- Retail enablement: Co-branded POP displays, demo units, retail staff training sheets, retail events and cross-promos.

Offer & pricing strategy
- Anchor product: Official roundnet kit (single set)
- Bundles: Party Bundle (2 nets + 2 extra balls), Travel Bundle (compact carry case + accessories), Family Pack (kid-size accessories + safety guides)
- Promotion cadence: Launch discount for first 72 hours, mid-campaign flash bundles, end-of-campaign UGC contest awarding free set + event VIP passes.
- CAD/localization: All product pages must display local currency automatically based on region; Canadian promos comply with local laws/taxes and shipping specifics.

Creative & content requirements
- Assets: Short social videos (15–60s), 6–12s Story/Reel cuts, product hero video (30–45s), lifestyle imagery, tutorial clips (how to set up; how to play), UGC library, event recap videos.
- Creative tone: Youthful, playful, irreverent, inclusive, energetic, community-first.
- Compliance: Signed model releases for minors, safety copy on product pages (outdoor safety), privacy policy & cookie banner must be present.

Measurement & analytics
- Tracking: GA4 + e‑commerce events, Facebook/Meta & TikTok pixels, UTM parameters, CRM tracking for email flows, event RSVPs tracked in CRM.
- Dashboard KPIs: Revenue by SKU, conversion rate by traffic source, cost per acquisition (CPA), ROAS by channel, email revenue attribution, event registration→purchase conversion.
- Reporting cadence: Weekly performance snapshot, in-depth mid-campaign review at week 4, final post-mortem.

Risks & mitigations
- Seasonality risk (bad weather): Promote portability and indoor play alternatives; schedule backups.
- Legal/regulatory risk for sweepstakes & minors: Legal sign-off for contest rules; require guardian consent & age gating if needed.
- Inventory/fulfillment constraints: Monitor stock, pre-announce limited-bundle caps, communicate shipping ETAs clearly.

Budget allocation (assumption-based)
- Paid social & ads: 45%
- Influencer creator & UGC seeding: 20%
- Events & activations: 20%
- Creative production & asset creation: 10%
- Retail partner support & POS: 5%
(Adjust based on actual budget.)
