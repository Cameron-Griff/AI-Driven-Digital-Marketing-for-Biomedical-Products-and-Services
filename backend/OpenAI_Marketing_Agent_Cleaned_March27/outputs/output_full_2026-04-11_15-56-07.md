# 1) Detailed Marketing Plan

Summary objective
- Acquire clinician customers and healthcare organizations across Canada and drive telemedicine + integrated billing adoption. Short-term launch horizon: 12 weeks to validate channels, close pilot clinics, and build momentum for scale.

Primary goals and KPIs (assumptions listed below)
- New clinician accounts (physicians): 50 in 12 weeks
- Clinic/organization pilots signed: 3–5
- Demo requests booked: 200
- Telemedicine session volume (platform adoption signal): 1,000 sessions/month baseline target by week 12 from onboarded users
- Billing integration live for pilot clinics: 3
- CAC (assumption): target CAD 1,500–3,000 per physician in early stage
- LTV and conversion assumptions noted below

Core positioning and messaging
- Value proposition (single-line): The only Canadian-focused integrated telemedicine + EHR with AI-assisted diagnostics, lab and billing integrations that shortens clinician workflows while keeping patient data in-country and secure.
- Key messaging pillars:
  - Clinical efficiency: reduce documentation and referral friction; faster clinical decisions via AI-assisted diagnostics.
  - Revenue capture & billing: unified billing and claims workflows to reduce lost revenue and reconciliation overhead.
  - Canadian-first compliance: PHIPA/PIPEDA-aligned data residency and clinician licensing workflows.
  - Trust & safety: clinician-controlled workflows, secure messaging, audit trails.
  - Practical adoption: low-friction onboarding, CME credits and hands-on training.

Primary target audiences and buyer journeys
- Primary: Physicians (family medicine, internal medicine, psychiatry), clinic owners/administrators, community clinics.  
  Journey: Awareness (LinkedIn thought pieces, digital ads) → Consideration (one-pager, case study, demo video) → Evaluation (live demo + pilot offer + technical datasheet + reference clinic) → Purchase/onboarding (contract, clinician logins, onboarding checklist, CME session).
- Secondary: Billing specialists, lab partners, medical educators (for partnerships/CME distribution).

Value propositions / offers by funnel stage
- Awareness: Thought leadership posts, short demo clips, security + Canadian data residency callouts.
- Consideration: Sales one-pager, clinician case study PDF, ROI snapshot, technical datasheet (integration/interop details), gated demo video (clinician login required).
- Decision: Time-limited pilot program offer (3-month pilot with discounted integration and dedicated onboarding), live webinar + Q&A, customer reference calls, security & legal pack for procurement.
- Retention/Expansion: CME credits, billing efficiency report, monthly usage health check, referral incentives.

Go-to-market channels (priority)
- 1: LinkedIn (organic + paid) — primary channel for physicians and clinic administrators.
- 2: Targeted email outreach to clinics and clinic administrators — personalized sequences.
- 3: Canadian health conferences and medical association events — vendor booths, speaking slots, sponsored CME.
- 4: Physician-focused digital ads (health-specific publications, programmatic with contextual targeting).
- 5: Partnerships with provincial medical associations, lab networks, and billing vendors for co-marketing and integration pilots.

Creative & content assets (formats required)
- Sales one-pager (provincial compliance callouts, ROI bullets, CTA for demo/pilot)
- Clinician case studies (video + PDF) — present clinical workflow time-savings and revenue lift
- Demo video (2–4 minutes) — secure and gated for clinicians; highlights AI diagnostics and billing workflow
- Technical datasheet (integration points, APIs, HL7/FHIR notes, data residency)
- Webinar deck + Q&A moderation plan
- Onboarding checklist for clinics (pre-integration steps, lab mapping, billing set-up)
- ROI/billing calculator (interactive or spreadsheet)
- Email templates & LinkedIn post templates

Compliance and security communications
- Explicitly emphasize PHIPA/PIPEDA alignment and Canadian data residency (including where data is stored and backups).
- Provide a downloadable Security & Compliance pack for procurement teams: encryption, audit logs, access control, incident response.
- Gating: clinician-only demo/video and pilot sign-up requires verified clinician login and license verification workflow (to build trust and limit non-clinical exposure).

Pricing & pilot structure (assumptions noted)
- Recommended pilot: 3 months, up to 10 clinicians, waived onboarding fee, discounted transaction fees for billing for pilot period; outcomes report at end.
- Pricing models to test: per-clinician subscription + per-encounter telemedicine fee; bundled subscription with billing transaction rate discounts.

Sales & enablement
- SDR outreach for demo booking; AE-run demos with clinical product specialist present.
- Pre-built procurement package (technical datasheet + security pack + sample contract).
- Onboarding success manager for each pilot to ensure billing & lab integrations are live within 30 days.

Measurement & reporting
- Weekly funnel dashboard: impressions, demo requests, demos booked, pilots started, pilots converted to paid, active clinician sessions, billing transactions.
- Monthly ROI reports from pilot clinics (time saved, increased billable captures, telemedicine adoption).

Assumptions (explicit)
- Pricing specifics, enterprise procurement cycles, and legal readiness for all provinces are not provided — plan assumes pilot pricing flexibility and that deeper regulatory certifications will be pursued in parallel.
- Budget for paid ads and events not specified; recommended initial test budget = CAD 40–60k over 12 weeks (split across LinkedIn ads, conference sponsorships, targeted display).
- Team capacity: assumes 1 demand gen marketer, 1 content creator, 1 SDR, 1 AE, 1 customer success/onboarding manager, and technical resources for integrations.
- Target provinces prioritized: Ontario, BC, Alberta, Quebec (English + French support required for Quebec outreach; translations needed for assets).
- Target clinical verticals prioritized: family medicine and psychiatry for telemedicine adoption speed; pilot billing partners likely in family medicine. Adjust if enterprise buyers differ.

Risks and mitigation
- Regulatory scrutiny: Mitigate by proactively publishing compliance materials and offering procurement-ready documentation.
- Clinician trust in AI: Provide clinical validation snippets, peer-reviewed references where available, and optional toggles to view AI rationale.
- Workflow disruption: Offer dedicated onboarding, integration mapping, and weekend/time-flexible training for clinicians.

Budget allocation (example)
- Content production & CRO: 15%
- Paid LinkedIn & programmatic ads: 35%
- Events & sponsorships (conferences, associations): 30%
- Email and SDR operations: 10%
- Tools and analytics: 10%

# 2) Platform-Specific Playbooks

LinkedIn (organic + paid)
- Objectives: Build credibility, generate demo requests, recruit pilot clinics.
- Targeting: Physicians by specialty (family med, psychiatry, internal med), clinic managers, medical directors, provincial association group members, and hospital outpatient leads. Geo-target by province; French-language ads for Quebec.
- Organic tactics:
  - Weekly thought leadership posts (clinical efficiency, Canadian data residency, AI explainability).
  - Short clinician testimonial clips (30–60s) and text case studies.
  - Product milestone posts (pilot launches, conference presence).
  - Engagement with provincial association posts and group discussions; sponsored InMail for event follow-ups.
- Paid tactics:
  - Sponsored content: demo video snippet → gated demo sign-up (clinician login required).
  - Lead Gen Forms for webinar registrations and pilot expressions of interest.
  - Retargeting: website visitors and video viewers to pilot landing page.
  - Creative variations: security-first vs productivity-first vs billing ROI creatives—A/B test messaging.
- CTAs: Book demo, Join pilot, Register for webinar.
- KPI targets: CTR 0.25–0.6% (clinician audiences higher CPM); demo CPL CAD 120–500 in pilot phase.

Targeted Email to Clinics
- Objectives: Move administrators and clinicians from consideration to demo/pilot sign-up.
- List building: purchased specialty clinic lists, association mailing partnerships, conference lists, referral opt-ins from LinkedIn.
- Sequence example (clinician/clinic admin):
  - Email 1: Pain-driven subject + one-pager attached; CTA: demo (day 0)
  - Email 2: Case study + ROI snapshot (day 4)
  - Email 3: Invite to webinar + pilot offer (day 10)
  - Email 4: Testimonial + last-chance pilot spots (day 18)
  - Follow-up cadence by SDR via phone for warm opens/clicks.
- Personalization: include province-specific compliance snippet, and reference to clinic size/specialty.
- Compliance: Ensure opt-out, CAN-SPAM/PIPEDA-friendly consent practices.
- KPI targets: Open rates 25–45%, click-to-demo 2–5%, demo-booking conversion from sequence 5–12%.

Canadian Health Conferences & Associations
- Objectives: Get speaking slots, demo leads, pilot sign-ups; build credibility via association partnerships.
- Tactics:
  - Sponsor a symposium or CME session focusing on telemedicine adoption and billing optimization.
  - Apply for speaking slots on telemedicine workflows and AI ethics for clinicians.
  - Booth: run live short demos, book on-site demos, collect qualified leads with clinician verification.
  - Follow-up: 48-72 hour post-event email with one-pager, case study, and pilot link.
- Materials: one-pager printouts, tablets with gated demo player, sign-up QR codes, branded onboarding checklists.
- KPI targets: 400–1,200 booth interactions per major conference; 3–10 high-quality pilot leads per event.

Physician-Focused Digital Ads (Programmatic & Specialty Publications)
- Objectives: Drive awareness and top-of-funnel demo interest for clinicians.
- Targeting: Contextual targeting on physician portals, specialty journals, medical news sites; geofencing near conferences or hospital campuses.
- Creative: short demo clips, infographic on billing capture, security badge creatives.
- Landing pages: clinician-only gating, tailored by specialty and province.
- KPI targets: CTR 0.15–0.4%, CPL higher due to niche audience; test direct demo sign-up vs webinar registration.

Webinar & Virtual Events
- Objectives: Educate clinicians on workflows, AI diagnostics, and billing ROI; capture demo/pilot signups.
- Format:
  - 45-minute webinar: 20m product demo, 15m clinical case study speaker, 10m live Q&A.
  - Offer CME credit where possible (work with medical associations).
- Promotion: LinkedIn, email, association newsletters, paid ads.
- Follow-up: Email with recording, datasheet, pilot sign-up CTA, schedule link for live demo.
- KPI targets: 200–300 registrants per webinar (paid + organic); 20–30% attendance; 10–20% of attendees request demo.

Clinical Case Studies & Testimonials
- Objective: Reduce trust barrier, show measured workflow and revenue impact.
- Structure: Problem → Workflow demo → Quantified benefits (time saved, billing capture increase) → Quote and contact.
- Distribution: landing page, sales assets, LinkedIn posts, webinar slides.
- Verification: Clinician consent, optional anonymization if required for regulatory/privacy.

Gated Demo Flow & Clinician Verification
- Gating rationale: maintain clinician-only space for deeper product demos and pilot discussions.
- Verification process: license verification field + suggested automated checking (or manual follow-up by SDR); collect clinic affiliation and preferred demo times.
- UX: fast path for verified clinicians to view the demo video; non-clinician redirects to informational page.

Technical & Integration Playbook (for procurement/IT audiences)
- Audience: Clinic IT, billing specialists, lab partners.
- Assets: technical datasheet, API docs summary, HL7/FHIR mapping notes, data residency documentation, integration SLA.
- Tactics: Technical webinars, white-glove pilot integration service, joint pre-sales calls with integration engineers.
- KPI: time-to-integration for pilot clinics < 45 days.

Billing Adoption Playbook
- Pain point focus: lost charges, reconciliation overhead, denied claims.
- Tactics:
  - ROI billing calculator showing revenue recapture scenarios.
  - Billing-specific webinar for clinic billers and managers.
  - Offer a billing fast-track pilot with dedicated billing specialist training.
  - Provide migration scripts and reconciliation templates.
- KPI: billing transactions per clinician, percent of claims routed through platform, time to first successful claim billing.

French-language & Quebec-specific Playbook
- Translate key assets (one-pager, demo captions, email sequences).
- Use French-speaking clinician testimonials and Quebec association partnerships.
- Targeted LinkedIn & email lists for Quebec physicians and clinics.

A/B testing recommendations
- Messages: Security vs efficiency vs revenue-first headlines.
- Landing page: gated demo vs immediate demo video vs webinar sign-up.
- Creative: clinician testimonial vs product-first demo clip.
- CTA language: “Book a pilot spot” vs “Schedule a demo” vs “See ROI examples.”

Customer success & retention playbook
- Onboarding checklist and 30/60/90 day success plan.
- Weekly check-ins for the first month, monthly business reviews thereafter.
- CME sessions and peer community invites to drive ongoing engagement.
- Upsell paths: advanced billing features, CME content licensing, lab integration premium services.

Sales enablement
- Pre-built demo scripts tailored by specialty.
- Clinician FAQ and objection-handling guide (AI trust, PHIPA compliance, billing reliability).
- Pricing sheet and pilot contract template for sales closes.
