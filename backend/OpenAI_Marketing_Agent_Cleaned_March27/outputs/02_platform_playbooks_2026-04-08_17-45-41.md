# 2) Platform-Specific Playbooks

Spikeball.com (ecommerce)
- Goal: convert visitors and educate on tier differences.
- Priority pages: Homepage hero (rotating: community events / Titan promo), Category landing (tier comparison), Product pages (video + specs + reviews + FAQ), Events hub (tournament signup & organizer resources).
- Content specs: hero video 16:9, 1920x1080, 30–60s; product thumbnails 2000x2000 JPEG; lifestyle images 3000x2000; WebP for performance.
- Copy best practice: 2–3 sentence hero, 5–7 bullet feature benefits, FAQ answering common objections (durability, setup, returns).
- UX tips: sticky CTA, comparison table between tiers, “Pick my set” micro-quiz, one-click add-to-cart for common bundles.
- Measurement: product page conversion, exit intent pop, add-to-cart rate by variant, region-specific cart abandonment.

Instagram (Feed, Stories, Reels)
- Goal: build social proof, inspire UGC, drive traffic to product pages and tournament signups.
- Creative:
  - Reels: 15–30s high-energy plays, transition edits, before/after setup (fast setup POV), Titan slow-mo shots. Hook in first 2s.
  - Feed: carousel product explainers, user spotlights, tournament highlights.
  - Stories: polls (which net?), UGC reposts, swipe-up to event registration.
- Specs: Reels 9:16, max 90s (but prefer <60s); cover 1080x1350; captions: lead with first 125 characters; hashtags: 3–8 targeted (#Roundnet #Spikeball #BackyardGames).
- Posting cadence: Reels 3/week, Feed 2/week, Stories daily during events.
- Paid: boost high-performing reels; prospecting with lookalike audiences of buyers.
- KPIs: Reel views, saves, click-through to site, event signups.

TikTok
- Goal: discovery and virality among casual/college players.
- Creative:
  - Trends-first approach: use current sound trends, gameplay clips, setup speed challenges, “pass the net” transitions.
  - Education micro-series: 3-step setup, beginner tips.
  - Challenges: branded hashtag challenge (e.g., #NetQuickChallenge) with prize packs.
- Specs: 9:16 vertical, 15–60s, native captions, strong first 2–3 seconds.
- Posting cadence: 4–6 times/week; prioritize experimentation.
- Paid: In-feed ads for prospecting; top-view for tournament launches.
- KPIs: view-through rate, hashtag submissions, cost-per-click, CTR to product pages.

Facebook
- Goal: community building (clubs, parents), event promotion.
- Tactics:
  - FB Groups: nurture local community groups, support tournament organizers with pinned resources.
  - Events: create FB events for tournaments with easy RSVP and links to buy event bundles.
  - Ads: target parents, PE teachers, and campus rec interests; use carousel ads for bundles.
- Creative: longer copy accepted; use testimonials and event recaps.
- Posting cadence: 3–4 posts/week; increase before events.
- KPIs: event RSVPs, group growth, CPC, registrations.

Email
- Goal: convert intent, nurture community, drive repeat purchases.
- Flow templates:
  - Welcome (3 emails): brand story + product guidance + social proof.
  - Abandon cart (3 touches): reminder, social proof, limited-time offer.
  - Post-purchase (3 touches): setup tips + ask for reviews + cross-sell accessories.
  - Event series: announcement, reminder, post-event recap.
- Specs: subject lines ≤50 chars, preheader ≤80 chars; mobile-first template, single primary CTA.
- Frequency: 1–2 weekly sends; segmentation by behavior.
- KPIs: open rate, CTR, conversion rate, revenue per send.

Community & Events channels
- Event organizer kit contents: logos, banner templates, registration form templates, judge rules, branded bracket graphics, sponsor decks, co-marketing checklist.
- Co-marketing: provide localized promo codes, feature organizers on site & socials, provide prize support.
- On-site event capture: dedicated landing pages with registration & store bundles.

PE/Club & B2B outreach
- Materials: downloadable lesson plans, bulk/order forms, certification webinar kit, testimonial case studies.
- Outreach cadence: targeted email + regional reps + campus rec partnerships each quarter.
- Incentives: bulk discounts, free demo net for large program pilots, training webinars.

Influencer & UGC playbook
- Selection: micro-influencers in college rec, PE, family/parent creators, outdoor lifestyle.
- Briefing: clear deliverables (1 unboxing, 2 gameplay clips, 1 instructional), messaging bullets, brand voice guidelines (fun, slightly edgy, inclusive).
- Compensation: product + performance bonus (affiliate sales).
- Rights: secure usage rights for shorts in paid ads and site.

Creative guidelines & voice
- Voice: playful, slightly irreverent, nostalgic — keep it inclusive and family-friendly.
- Visuals: high-energy, real-world gameplay, diverse participants, clear product close-ups, Titan premium should feel tactile and premium (metal glints, slow-mo).
- Accessibility: captions on videos, alt-text on images, clean contrast for readability.

Compliance & international considerations
- Privacy & cookies: consent management on site; don't assume universal pixel firing—segment by consent.
- Shipping & returns: display regional shipping estimates & duties early; avoid promising delivery times where customs vary.
- Ad rules: avoid health or safety claims; for minors in videos, ensure appropriate releases and local regulations.
