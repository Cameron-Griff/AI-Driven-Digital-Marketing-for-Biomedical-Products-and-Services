# 3) Week-by-Week Content Calendar

| Week | Theme | Website | Instagram | Facebook | Pinterest | Primary CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Brand introduction and positioning | Refresh homepage hero with clear value proposition and featured collections | Reel introducing the brand and founder-led curation | Intro post with brand story and service overview | Create boards for living room, bedroom, dining, and styling tips | Shop collections / Book consult |
| 2 | Living room styling | Publish or update a living room collection page with shoppable items | Carousel: “How to style a cohesive living room” | Share the same carousel with a short trust-building caption | Pin living room mood board and product collage | View living room collection |
| 3 | Design services education | Add a service explainer page or FAQ section | Reel: “What virtual design can solve” | Post about who the service is for and how it works | Pin service infographic or process graphic | Request a design inquiry |
| 4 | Founder picks | Feature a curated edit from the founder on homepage or collection page | Static post or carousel: founder’s top 5 picks | Share founder story and expertise | Pin individual product images to style-focused boards | Shop founder’s edit |
| 5 | Bedroom refresh | Add bedroom lookbook page or section | Reel: bedside styling or bedding layering tips | Post a room reveal with product links | Pin bedroom inspiration and product pairings | Shop the bedroom edit |
| 6 | Before/after credibility | Add a portfolio case study or featured project page | Carousel showing before/after transformation | Share project story and client testimonial | Pin before/after comparison and room result | Book a consult |
| 7 | Seasonal styling | Launch seasonal collection page | Reel: seasonal table, shelf, or entryway styling | Post seasonal décor inspiration and bundle highlights | Pin seasonal décor mood board | Shop seasonal collection |
| 8 | Conversion push | Add featured products and “complete the look” modules across key pages | Carousel: bestsellers and how to use them together | Share bestsellers and reminder to inquire before capacity fills | Pin bestsellers and room-by-room styling pins | Shop now / Inquire now |

## Weekly execution notes
- Reuse each week’s hero asset across all platforms for efficiency.
- Keep each post anchored to one clear action.
- Alternate between product-led and service-led content to balance revenue goals.
- Prioritize high-quality photography, room context, and concise captions.
- If capacity is limited, add urgency carefully: “limited consult spots” or “small-batch collection.”

## Suggested recurring content rhythm
- 1 website update or landing page refresh per week
- 2 to 3 Instagram feed posts per week
- 3 to 5 Instagram Stories per week
- 1 Facebook post per week
- 3 to 5 Pinterest pins per week
