# 3) Week-by-Week Content Calendar

Assumption: 12-week launch/activation plan focused on Ottawa/Canada clinician market; team: 1 marketing lead, 1 content/creative resource, 1 sales rep, 1 compliance reviewer.

| Week | Theme / Goal | Asset(s) | Channel(s) | CTA | Owner | KPI |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Launch: awareness + demo funnel setup | Website clinician landing page + demo booking widget; short hero demo clip (30s) | Website, LinkedIn organic post, LinkedIn Sponsored (awareness) | Book 15-min demo | Marketing | Demo bookings (target 10) |
| 2 | Credibility: clinician champion onboarding | Draft case study interview with 1 clinician; testimonial video shoot | Email outreach to candidate clinicians, LinkedIn teaser | Agree to be featured / join pilot | Marketing + Sales | Clinician champion commitment (1–2) |
| 3 | Lead magnet publish | Gated whitepaper (workflow ROI) + landing page | Website gated asset, targeted LinkedIn Lead Gen, Email to list | Download whitepaper / Book demo | Marketing | Whitepaper downloads (target 50) |
| 4 | Nurture: convert downloads to demos | Email nurture sequence (3 touches) with case study teaser | Email, LinkedIn retargeting | Book demo / Join pilot | Marketing | Email open/clicks, meetings booked |
| 5 | Demonstrate product value | Full product demo video (2–4 min) + 3 social cuts | Website resource page, LinkedIn organic & sponsored, Email | Watch demo / Book pilot | Marketing | Video watch-through rate, demo links clicked |
| 6 | Publish case study #1 | Published clinician case study + 60s testimonial clip | Website blog, LinkedIn post, Email to prospects, PR pitch | Download/read / Book pilot | Marketing + Clinician Champion | Case study reads, inbound demos |
| 7 | Webinar preparation | Webinar landing page: topic “Faster diagnostics with integrated labs + AI” | Email invites, LinkedIn event, partner invites (labs) | Register for webinar | Marketing + Sales | Webinar registrations (target 50) |
| 8 | Host webinar / CME push | Live webinar + recording; offer pilot spots and CME info | Webinar platform, Website, LinkedIn | Book pilot / Watch recording | Marketing + Clinician speaker | Webinar attendance and follow-up meetings |
| 9 | Conference prep & PR | Conference one-pager, booth creatives, press release for pilot program | Conference outreach, PR distribution, LinkedIn | Book on-site demos / Schedule meeting | Marketing + Sales | Meetings scheduled at conference |
| 10 | Publish ROI one-pager | Short ROI one-pager (clinic admin time saved) + infographics | Website resources, LinkedIn posts, targeted email to clinic admins | Download / Request pilot | Marketing | One-pager downloads, demo bookings |
| 11 | Outreach push & partner conversations | Targeted email sequence to lab partners and billing vendors; co-marketing proposal | Email, LinkedIn | Request partnership meeting | Partnerships + Sales | Partner meetings set (target 3) |
| 12 | Review & scale plan | Performance report, refine paid channels, plan next quarter | Internal review, stakeholder updates | Approve Q2 budget & focus | Marketing leadership | Conversion rates, CAC, pipeline growth |

Notes on execution
- Cadence: publish at least one LinkedIn organic post/week (post types rotate: thought leadership, demo clip, case study highlight).
- Email sequences: ensure CASL compliance and maintain opt-in lists.
- Demo booking: use a scheduling widget with required clinician fields to pre-qualify (specialty, clinic size).
- Post-demo: sales to send tailored pilot pack within 48h; marketing to request permission to convert pilot into case study.
- Monthly review: review results vs KPIs and adapt copy/targeting.
