## Weekly execution rhythm
- **Monday:** Publish educational content
- **Tuesday:** Share LinkedIn post and email to relevant segment
- **Wednesday:** Promote feature or workflow asset
- **Thursday:** Run demo or webinar follow-up content
- **Friday:** Retarget high-intent visitors and summarize key takeaway

## Content prioritization rules
- Lead with provider value, then patient convenience
- Use proof points where available, but do not overstate validation
- Keep privacy and security visible in every major asset
- Rotate between operational, clinical, and trust-building messages
- End each piece with a clear next step: demo, brief, webinar, or consultation
