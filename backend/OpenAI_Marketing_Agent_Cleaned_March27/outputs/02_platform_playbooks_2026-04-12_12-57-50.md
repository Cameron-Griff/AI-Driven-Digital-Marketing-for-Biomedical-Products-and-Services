# 2) Platform-Specific Playbooks

## Website playbook
### Homepage structure
- Hero: clear value proposition, one-line proof of benefit, strong CTA
- Trust strip: security, privacy, clinical workflow, Canada focus
- Feature overview: telemedicine, records, scheduling, messaging, billing, AI
- Use-case blocks by audience: doctors, clinics, specialists, patients
- Social proof: testimonials, metrics, or logos if available
- Final CTA: book a demo or see the platform in action

### Best practices
- Use calm, clinical visuals and avoid consumer-app styling
- Keep claims precise and medically cautious
- Add privacy and security notices near any data-entry or login action
- Provide clear “how it works” steps for each role

## Doctor dashboard playbook
### In-app marketing goals
- Drive feature discovery
- Reduce time-to-value
- Increase daily active use
- Encourage cross-feature adoption

### Tactics
- Onboarding checklist for first 7 days:
  - complete profile
  - add clinic information
  - connect scheduling
  - enable secure messaging
  - upload or import records
  - activate virtual visits
  - explore specialty AI tools
- Contextual tooltips tied to user behavior
- Empty-state prompts with direct actions
- Monthly feature highlights inside the dashboard
- “Recommended next step” banner based on usage stage

### Tone and UX
- Reassuring, concise, professional
- Minimal jargon
- Always include “for clinical decision support” language where AI appears

## Patient portal playbook
### Goal
Make care access feel easy, secure, and reliable.

### Tactics
- Simple booking and appointment reminders
- Clear messaging and visit preparation instructions
- Privacy-first explanations in plain English
- Post-visit follow-up prompts
- Mobile-friendly layouts and large tap targets

### Messaging priorities
- “See your doctor from home”
- “Secure messages and easy scheduling”
- “Your information is protected”
- “Fast access to care when you need it”

## Specialty module playbook
### Goal
Differentiate with relevance, not generic AI hype.

### Tactics
- Create specialty-specific landing pages and in-dashboard prompts
- Explain what the AI module helps with:
  - triage support
  - documentation assistance
  - workflow prioritization
  - case summary organization
- Include disclaimers and boundaries clearly
- Use specialty language that matches practitioner expectations

## Compliance and trust playbook
### Required content
- Privacy policy summary
- Data storage and access explanation
- Secure messaging and authentication overview
- Medical disclaimer for AI features
- Regional compliance notes where applicable

### Trust-building tactics
- Place compliance badges and notices near conversion points
- Create a short “How we protect your data” section
- Offer downloadable security overview for clinic buyers
- Avoid overstating diagnostic accuracy

## Lifecycle email playbook
### Segments
- New doctor sign-up
- Incomplete onboarding
- Demo requested, not booked
- Trial active but low usage
- Clinic admin evaluating implementation
- Patient onboarding and appointment reminders

### Content examples
- Welcome and setup guidance
- Feature education by role
- Reminder and rescheduling workflows
- AI feature introductions
- Activation nudges for secure messaging and scheduling

## Paid acquisition playbook
### Search
Target intent around:
- virtual care platform
- telemedicine software
- clinic scheduling system
- secure healthcare messaging
- digital patient portal
- healthcare workflow software

### Retargeting
- Show feature-specific ads based on visited pages
- Use clinic-oriented proof points
- Retarget with demo and security-focused CTAs

### LinkedIn
- Target clinic admins, operations leaders, and healthcare decision-makers
- Use educational offers rather than hard-sell messaging
- Promote webinars, white papers, and demo recordings
