# 3) Week-by-Week Content Calendar

| Week | Focus / Funnel Stage | Channel(s) | Content / Activity | Target Audience | Goal / KPI | Owner |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Awareness | Website, LinkedIn, Email | Publish homepage hero + 30s explainer video; LinkedIn post announcing platform benefits; lead magnet: "AI in Clinical Workflow" whitepaper landing page | Physicians, Practice Managers | Site visits, whitepaper downloads; CTR from LinkedIn | Product marketer |
| Week 2 | Awareness → Consideration | Targeted Email, LinkedIn (paid) | Email nurture #1 to conference leads & downloads; LinkedIn Sponsored Content promoting whitepaper + demo CTA | New leads, targeted physicians | Email open 25%+, demo CTA clicks | Growth marketer |
| Week 3 | Consideration | Webinar landing page, Email, LinkedIn | Announce CME webinar (date wk5); create registration form & promo assets; push via email & LinkedIn | Physicians | Webinar registrations (target 150) | Partnerships lead |
| Week 4 | Consideration | Website, Blog, Newsletter placements | Publish clinician case study (written + 90s clip): workflow/time saved + billing outcomes; syndicate to clinician newsletters | Physicians, Clinic Managers | Case study downloads, demo requests | Content creator |
| Week 5 | Conversion (Event) | Webinar (CME) + On-demand | Host CME webinar: clinical panel + 15-min product demo; CTA: schedule pilot/demo | Physicians (attendees) | Attendance rate 40%+, demo signups | Clinical lead & product marketer |
| Week 6 | Conversion | Email follow-up, Sales outreach | Post-webinar nurture: recording, pilot offer; Sales schedules demos for engaged leads; LinkedIn retargeting | Webinar attendees, engaged leads | Demo conversion rate (target 20% of engaged) | Sales / Growth |
| Week 7 | Conversion → Trial | Sales demos, Pilot onboarding assets | Run first wave of pilot onboarding (onboard 5 clinics); publish pilot success checklist + onboarding one-pager | Pilot clinics, clinic managers | Pilot activation (logins, first teleconsult) | Onboarding manager |
| Week 8 | Activation | In-product tips, Email, Case study video | Share quick-start tips email to pilots; record short pilot testimonial clips; publish billing capture one-pager | Pilot clinicians, billing staff | Activation rate within 14 days (target 70%) | Customer success |
| Week 9 | Expansion | LinkedIn, Partnerships outreach | Publish lab-partner case study/one-pager; outreach to lab partners for integration pilots | Labs, Clinic managers | Partner interest meetings, integration pilots | Partnerships lead |
| Week 10 | Retention / Proof | Website + Email + LinkedIn | Publish whitepaper: AI validation & governance (technical brief); promote via email to pilots & prospects | Clinicians, IT/security leads | Downloads, trust-building engagements | Clinical lead |
| Week 11 | Expansion → Scale | Paid LinkedIn, Newsletters | Launch targeted paid campaign offering pilot slots to region-specific clinics; sponsored newsletter placement | Physicians & practice owners in Ontario/target regions | Pilot signups (target 10-20) | Growth marketer |
| Week 12 | Review & Optimize | Internal review, CRM, Email | Analyze 12-week KPIs; send summary to stakeholders; iterate creative/cadence; plan next 12 weeks | Internal | Conversion KPIs, lessons, next-quarter plan | Product marketer / Ops |

Notes on measurement and iteration
- Weekly: track demo requests, pilot signups, activation rate, email metrics; review with sales bi-weekly.
- Monthly: MRR, teleconsult volumes, retention cohorts.
- Iterate creatives and targeting based on performance: if LinkedIn CPL high, pivot to clinician newsletters and partnerships.
