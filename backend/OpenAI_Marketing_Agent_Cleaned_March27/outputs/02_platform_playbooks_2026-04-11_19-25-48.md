# 2) Platform-Specific Playbooks

LinkedIn (organic + paid)
- Objective: Generate awareness among pharma office managers, clinical trial coordinators and clinic administrators; drive demo requests and webinar signups.
- Messaging hooks:
  - "Centralize your trials, inventory and virtual care in one dashboard — built for Canadian compliance."
  - For coordinators: "Reduce trial admin by X hours/week (pilot metric to confirm)."
- Content types: short demo clips (30–90s), carousel feature explainers, executive thought leadership posts, sponsored whitepaper download campaigns, Customer testimonial posts.
- Targeting (paid): Job titles (trial coordinators, clinical operations manager, clinic admin, pharmacy manager), industries (pharmaceuticals, clinical research, healthcare), geolocation (Canada + selected provinces), company size (clinics/pharma offices).
- Tactics:
  - Run A/B tests on CTAs: "Book a demo" vs "Join pilot".
  - Use LinkedIn Lead Gen Forms for webinar and whitepaper downloads; follow up via automated email + sales outreach within 24 hours.
  - Retarget video viewers with demo-request ads.
- KPIs: CTR, lead form submissions, demo booked rate, CPL.

Industry email campaigns (segmented cadence)
- Objective: Nurture leads into pilots and demos with targeted sequences per persona.
- Segments: pharma office managers, trial coordinators, clinic admins, lab managers.
- Cadence (example): 6-email sequence over 4 weeks:
  1. Intro + whitepaper download
  2. Short demo video + invite to webinar
  3. Case study (pilot clinic) + testimonial
  4. Compliance & data residency brief + FAQ
  5. Pilot offer + limited slots
  6. Final reminder + direct calendar link
- Subject-line testing: emphasize benefit (time saved, regulatory fit) vs curiosity (AI in trials).
- Compliance: Ensure CAN-SPAM/CASL rules, explicit unsubscribe, and data residency reassurance.
- KPIs: open rate, click-to-demo, reply rate, pilot signups.

Pharma trade publications & PR
- Objective: Build credibility and reach senior pharma operations stakeholders.
- Tactics:
  - Publish bylined articles (whitepaper excerpts) on AI diagnostics and trial management.
  - Place two case-study features once pilot outcomes available.
  - Secure interviews/quotes in major Canadian pharma/healthcare outlets.
  - Use data from whitepaper as press hooks ("X% time saved in trial enrollment").
- Creative: authoritativeness—include clinician quotes, data points, regulatory emphasis.
- KPIs: article placements, referral traffic, inbound demo requests.

Webinars & virtual events
- Objective: Educate and convert trial coordinators and clinic administrators via product demos + panel discussions.
- Formats:
  - Intro Webinar (45 min): Problem → Demo → Q&A. CTA: Book Pilot.
  - Deep-dive (60 min): Trial/inventory workflows + implementation checklist — include live Q&A with a pilot clinic or advisory clinician.
- Promotion: 4-week promo window via LinkedIn, email, partner channels; offer CE credits or certificates if possible (adds incentive).
- Follow-up: Send recording + tailored one-page pilot offer. Sales to call high-value registrants within 48 hours.
- KPIs: registration, attendance rate, demo requests from attendees.

Conferences & in-person events
- Objective: Close enterprise/pharma conversations, secure pilots with larger offices.
- Tactics:
  - Pre-event: Schedule 1:1 demos with prospects, send targeted invites to attendees.
  - Booth strategy: Live demo station, short product video loop, printed one-pagers, quick QR-code sign-up for pilot.
  - Speaking: Submit abstracts focused on "AI diagnostics in clinical trials" or "Integrated workflows for Canadian pharma".
  - Post-event: Follow-up sequence (email + phone within 72 hours).
- KPIs: qualified meetings, demos scheduled, pilot agreements.

Targeted clinic outreach (direct)
- Objective: Convert local clinics and pharma offices through high-touch demos and pilots.
- Tactics:
  - Localized outreach in Ottawa + key provinces (Ontario, Quebec): calls, personalized emails, and in-person demo offers.
  - Offer on-site or secure remote demo tailored to their workflows; include a one-page ROI calculator tailored to their organization size.
  - Provide bilingual materials and a dedicated onboarding concierge for pilot customers.
- Sales enablement: leave-behind one-pagers, checklist for integrations (labs, billing), SLA and pilot terms.
- KPIs: outreach-to-demo conversion, pilot acceptance rate, deal velocity.

Content formats & distribution mapping
- Demo videos: LinkedIn, website gated demo, trade pub embeds, conference loops.
- Whitepapers & case studies: gated landing pages (lead capture) promoted via LinkedIn and email.
- Feature one-pagers: sales collateral + downloadable on website (English & French).
- Social copy: short, benefits-first, with regulatory reassurance and clear CTA.
- Localization: all externally facing assets must have French variants for Canadian distribution.

Compliance checklist for marketing assets
- Include data residency and privacy statement on all gated assets.
- Avoid clinical claims until validated; use language like "AI‑assisted" and "clinician‑validated".
- Legal review prior to publishing claims about diagnostic accuracy or time-savings.
- Display roadmap/commitment to certifications (e.g., “Pursuing [cert]”) if formal badges not yet obtained.

Measurement & iteration
- Weekly metrics review: leads by source, demo bookings, pilot signups.
- Bi-weekly creative A/B tests for ads and email subject lines.
- Monthly go/no-go review to scale channels that hit CPL and conversion targets.
