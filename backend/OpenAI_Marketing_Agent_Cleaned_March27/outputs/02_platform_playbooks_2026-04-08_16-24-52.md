# 2) Platform-Specific Playbooks

E-commerce product pages (product + bundles)
- Key goals: Maximize conversion, increase AOV (bundles), reduce returns.
- Must-haves: Hero video (15–30s setup + play), multiple lifestyle images (desktop & mobile crops), detailed features (setup time, portability specs), trust badges, reviews with photos, clear CTA, shipping & return policy, CAD/local pricing detection, cross-sell “Complete your playset”.
- Conversion elements: Countdown for limited bundles, free-shipping thresholds, one-click bundle add, cart savings messaging.
- SEO + content: Product FAQ, how-to blog links (rules + drills), schema markup for product & reviews.
- Measurement: Product page views → add-to-cart → checkout conversion funnel, AOV, bounce rates.

Instagram (Reels, Feed, Stories, Guides)
- Content types: Reels (15–60s) with trending audio for demos, tricks, social hangouts; Stories for day-of event coverage + polls; Grid for lifestyle imagery & product highlights; Guides for how-to content and local club directories.
- Cadence: Reels 3x/week, Stories daily during events, grid posts 2x/week.
- Specs: Reels vertical 9:16 (1080x1920), cover optimized for feed, caption with CTA + 3–5 brand hashtags + 5 local/community hashtags.
- Tactics: UGC amplification (reshare tagged content), influencer takeovers, “Find a Game Near You” story highlight, shoppable tags for products.
- CTA: Shop product/bundle, join event, tag a crew.
- Measurement: Reels views, saves, website clicks, conversion rate from Instagram traffic.

TikTok (short-form viral + UGC)
- Content types: Trend-driven challenges (e.g., “60s setup challenge”), tutorial snippets (how to serve), playful competitor POVs, duet/stitch interactions with creators.
- Cadence: 4–6 posts/week (prioritize volume & fast iteration).
- Specs: Vertical 9:16; 15–60s (focus on first 3s hook).
- Tactics: Seed with micro-influencers (college teams, active outdoors creators), hashtag challenge (#RoundnetSetChallenge), UGC incentive (contest for best trick shot).
- CTA: Link in bio to bundles/sheet; event RSVP.
- Measurement: Views, follower growth, traffic to site (link in bio clicks), conversion from paid TikTok.

YouTube (Shorts + longer-form)
- Shorts: 15–60s recuts of reels/tiktoks for reach.
- Long-form: 3–8 min tutorials (how to play, coaching tips), event recaps, team interviews — great for community and SEO.
- Cadence: Shorts 2–4x/week; long-form 1x/2 weeks.
- Specs: Shorts vertical, long-form 16:9; thumbnails branded, timestamps for tutorials.
- CTA: Subscribe, product link in description, event registration.
- Measurement: Views & watch time, subscriber growth, referral traffic.

Email marketing
- Flows: Welcome series (3 emails: brand story + bestsellers + social proof/bundle offer), Abandoned cart (3 emails over 72 hours), Post-purchase (thank you + setup guide + referral ask + event invites), Re-engagement.
- Campaigns: Launch email (72-hour discount), Mid-campaign flash, Event RSVPs & recaps, UGC contest & winner announcement.
- Segmentation: New subscribers, past purchasers, cart abandoners, event attendees, local region (Canadian list separated for CAD messaging).
- Subject lines & tone: Playful, urgent when needed; emoji use acceptable (keep regional compliance in mind).
- Measurement: Open rate, click-through rate, revenue per recipient, unsubscribe rate.

Event activations (pop-ups, tournaments, demos)
- Objectives: Drive demo-to-purchase, capture leads, grow local clubs.
- Pre-event: Local ads, RSVP landing page (collect email & region), influencer invites, retail partner co-promotion.
- On-site: Branded booth, demo nets, game organizers, photo backdrop for UGC, QR codes to shop exclusive event bundles, merch giveaways.
- Post-event: Email recap (photos + highlights), UGC round-up, event discount for attendees.
- Legal & safety: Waivers for competitive play, parental consent for minors, insurance checks.
- Measurement: Registrations, demo-to-purchase conversion, email capture rate, social mentions.

Retail partner playbook
- Assets: Shelf signage, demo units, quick setup video on loop, staff cheat-sheet with key features & FAQs.
- Promotions: Weekend demo events, co-branded displays, buy-online-pickup-in-store (BOPIS), bundle exclusives for retail.
- Training: 1-pager + 2-min product video for retail staff, incentive for sales (spiffs) during launch weekends.
- Measurement: Sell-through rates, retail POS data, uplift in surrounding store categories.

Creator & UGC strategy
- Briefs: Fast setup demo, 2 plays (funny/relatable + impressive trick), 1 CTA to tag brand and use hashtag.
- Creator tiers: Micro (10–25k) for authentic local reach; Macro (100k+) for broad awareness and event attendance.
- Rights: Secure usage rights to repurpose UGC in ads/site for 6–12 months.
- Incentives: Free kit + promo codes for creators, performance bonus on sales.

Compliance & legal playbook
- Privacy: Cookie banners, opt-ins for marketing, region-specific data handling.
- Promotions: Written legal rules for contests; age restrictions/cautions; CA-specific requirements for CAD/language.
- Media: Model releases for anyone identifiable; guardian consent for minors.

Ad creative specs & constraints (high level)
- Instagram/TikTok: vertical 9:16, .mp4/.mov, <500MB, 15–60s recommended; short hooks 0–3s.
- YouTube: horizontal 16:9 for long form; Shorts vertical.
- Paid caps: Keep creative variations A/B tested; ensure text-to-image ratios comply with platform policies.
- Commerce policies: No misinformation, no unsafe claims, clear shipping/return policy; avoid prohibited content.
