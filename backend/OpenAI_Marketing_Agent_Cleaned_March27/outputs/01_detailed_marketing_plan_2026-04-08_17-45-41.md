# 1) Detailed Marketing Plan

Summary objective
- Drive online sales on Spikeball.com and increase community engagement (tournament signups, repeat purchases, ambassador/club participation). Focus on converting aware/consideration audiences and educating novices who need discovery.

Primary KPIs
- Sales: monthly online revenue, conversion rate (site-wide and product-level), AOV.
- Paid efficiency: ROAS and CAC by channel.
- Community: tournament registrations, club/PE program signups, ambassador activations.
- Retention: repeat purchase rate, 30/90-day retention, email repeat-purchase conversion.
- Content: engagement rate (likes/shares/comments), video view-through rate, UGC submissions.

Target segments & priorities
- Priority 1: Casual players & families — value durability, quick setup, social play.
- Priority 2: College clubs & competitive players — interested in Titan premium and tournament gear.
- Priority 3: PE teachers & youth organizations — buying for programs, need lesson plans and kits.
- Secondary: Church groups, community groups, tournament organizers.

Core messaging framework
- Primary tagline: "Durable. Fast set-up. Game on." (Playful, slightly irreverent voice)
- Pillars:
  - Social + inclusive: “Anyone can play — bring people together.”
  - Durability + reliability: “Built for backyard beatings and tournament pressure.”
  - Fast setup + portability: “Get set up in minutes, play for hours.”
  - Tier clarity: “Every player. Every budget. Titan for pros, Core for everyone.”
- Proof points: real-world durability testing, community testimonials, tournament endorsements, clear warranty.

Pricing, offers & packaging
- Maintain tiered SKUs: Intro/Core, Pro, Titan (premium). Highlight tradeoffs (weight, materials, warranty).
- Bundles to drive AOV: Team Pack (4 nets + carry), Family Pack (2 nets + balls), Repair/Accessory bundle.
- Promotions: timed tournament registration bundles (net + discounted entry), limited-edition community colorways to drive urgency.
- Subscription/consumables: Offer replacement nets/balls on repeat subscription with discounted re-order to increase LTV.

Conversion rate optimization (site & product pages)
- Product page structure: hero lifestyle image/video, 3-tier comparison table, durability callout (tests), setup time, tech specs, reviews, tournament-ready badge, shipping & warranty clarity.
- A/B tests: CTA copy (Buy vs Join a Game), hero video vs hero image, price anchoring with bundles, showing stock urgency by region.
- Checkout: reduce friction for international buyers (auto-currency display, shipping estimator early), one-page express checkout options, clear return policy and localized shipping times.

Community & events strategy
- Tournament playbook: seed monthly local tournaments with a standardized organizer kit (branding assets, registration templates, scoring app suggestions, prize templates). Offer organizer discounts and event co-marketing.
- Ambassador & club program: structured tiering (Community Organizers, Competitive Ambassadors), benefits (discounts, early product access, promo codes), quarterly activation requirements (host event, produce content).
- PE/Club outreach: curriculum kits, lesson plans, bulk discount program, video guides, certification webinars for teachers.

Content strategy
- Content pillars: How-to/setup & skills, Community stories & UGC, Product differentiation (Titan vs Core), Tournament highlights & leaderboards, PE/Club resources, Seasonal promos.
- Format mix: short-form video (15–60s), micro UGC clips, 30–90s product demos, carousel product explainers, email newsletters, event landing pages.
- UGC loop: incentivize with monthly themes and prize packs; highlight top clips on site & social with credit.

Paid acquisition & retargeting
- Prospecting: TikTok + Instagram Reels (trend-led creative featuring play moments), Meta prospecting with lookalikes from purchasers and engaged community members.
- Search/Shopping: Google Shopping for high-intent queries, and search ads for comparison keywords ("spikeball vs [competitor]", "best roundnet set").
- Retargeting: dynamic product ads for cart abandoners, video viewers converted to site visitors, site visitors by product viewed; sequence messaging (education -> social proof -> discount).
- Measurement: use server-side tracking and GA4; prioritize ROAS by creative set and audience.

Email & CRM
- Flows: welcome series with brand + product education, cart abandonment (staggered discounts), browse abandonment, post-purchase cross-sell (balls, nets, accessories), winback.
- Segmentation: by purchase tier, community role (player vs organizer vs PE), region, LTV.
- Frequency: 1–2 emails/week non-spammy; event or tournament pushes as needed.
- Loyalty: points for purchases, event hosting, UGC submissions; exchangeable for accessories or discounts.

Partnerships & PR
- Strategic partners: outdoor brands, campus rec departments, PE associations, youth organizations. Cross-promote tournaments and club kits.
- PR hooks: durability testing features, tournament circuits, Titan launch stories, community impact (inclusion, youth programs).

Measurement & reporting cadence
- Weekly: traffic, add-to-cart, cart abandon rate, conversion.
- Monthly: revenue by channel, ROAS, tournament registrations, ambassador activations.
- Quarterly: retention cohort analysis, LTV vs CAC, product mix & inventory health.
- Attribution: use mixed model (last-click for reporting, incrementality tests/P experiments for strategic budget shifts).

Assumptions (explicit)
- Campaign start: assume immediate start; calendar below assumes Week 1 today.
- Monthly paid budget: assumed mid-range (e.g., $50–100k/month), adjustable per region; if lower, shift focus to organic/community and email.
- Baseline metrics: assume existing conversion rate ~1.5–2% and AOV in line with mature ecommerce sporting goods; adjust tactics if actuals vary.
- Inventory: assume stable inventory for core SKUs and controlled inventory for Titan limited runs; if limited, prioritize Titan to high-value audiences.
- Translations/localization: assume English-first launch with translations for top markets (EU, AU, CA); full localization cost/time not included.
- Tech: assume Spikeball.com can support dynamic content, UGC embedding, and standard ecommerce CRO tests; if not, prioritize simpler A/B tests and email retargeting.

Risks & mitigation
- International checkout friction: mitigate with clear shipping costs early, localized messaging, prominent shipping times.
- Premium Titan price resistance: use warranty, professional endorsements, and trial/return guarantees.
- Community fatigue: keep tournaments diverse (casual vs competitive), stagger frequency, rotate incentives.

Budget & resource allocation (recommended)
- Paid acquisition: 50% (split: TikTok 25%, Meta 20%, Search/Shopping 30% of paid mix).
- Creative production & UGC seeding: 15%.
- Community programs & events (organizer grants, prizes): 15%.
- Email/CRM & retention: 10%.
- SEO/content & partnerships: 10%.

Ramp/Testing plan (first 12 weeks)
- Weeks 1–4: Launch creative tests (3 creative themes per channel), kick off ambassador recruitment, publish product comparison content, enable tournament organizer kits.
- Weeks 5–8: Scale top creatives, start regional tournament co-promotions, introduce Titan spotlight campaigns, launch subscription for nets/balls pilot.
- Weeks 9–12: Evaluate cohort retention, optimize bundles, expand translations for top-performing markets, run a major community tournament push.
