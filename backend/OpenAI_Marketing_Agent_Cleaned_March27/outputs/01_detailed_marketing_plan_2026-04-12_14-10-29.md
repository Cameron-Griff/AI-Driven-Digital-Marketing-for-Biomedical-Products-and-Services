# 1) Detailed Marketing Plan

## Objective
Drive adoption of an AI-powered digital triage tool that helps patients and care teams determine the appropriate care level quickly, safely, and with confidence—while reducing unnecessary emergency department visits.

## Assumptions
- The product is already functional and can be embedded into patient-facing and provider-facing workflows.
- The tool provides symptom-based guidance, not diagnoses, and includes escalation prompts for emergencies.
- The primary markets are English-speaking, global healthcare organizations operating under HIPAA and PIPEDA expectations.
- Success is measured by reduced avoidable ED utilization, improved triage accuracy/consistency, higher patient confidence, and better routing to care pathways.

## Positioning
### Core positioning statement
An explainable, safety-first digital triage assistant that helps people understand where to seek care—without diagnosing—so they can act faster, reduce uncertainty, and avoid unnecessary emergency visits.

### Key differentiators
- **Safety-first guidance:** Clear emergency escalation and red-flag detection messaging.
- **Explainability:** Recommendations are easy to understand and justify.
- **Operational impact:** Helps reduce ED crowding and improve patient flow.
- **Responsible design:** Privacy-compliant, medically cautious, and non-diagnostic.
- **Workflow flexibility:** Useful for patients, clinics, telehealth, and healthcare administrators.

## Audience segmentation
### 1. Patients and caregivers
**Needs:** quick reassurance, urgency guidance, fear reduction, accessibility  
**Message:** “Get clear guidance on what level of care to seek, and know when to seek emergency help.”

### 2. Clinic and provider staff
**Needs:** triage support, fewer low-acuity bottlenecks, consistent routing  
**Message:** “Standardize care navigation and reduce avoidable strain on front-desk and nursing workflows.”

### 3. Telehealth systems
**Needs:** pre-visit routing, better appointment prioritization, efficient escalation  
**Message:** “Improve virtual intake with safe, explainable triage before the visit begins.”

### 4. Healthcare administrators
**Needs:** ED diversion, operational efficiency, patient satisfaction, compliance  
**Message:** “Reduce unnecessary emergency utilization while preserving safety and compliance.”

## Messaging framework
### Primary message pillars
1. **Safety first**
   - Designed to escalate urgent symptoms immediately
   - Not a diagnosis tool
   - Supports responsible care-seeking

2. **Clarity under uncertainty**
   - Helps users understand what level of care may be appropriate
   - Reduces anxiety around symptom severity

3. **Operational efficiency**
   - Helps route lower-acuity cases away from emergency services
   - Improves flow across clinics and telehealth channels

4. **Trust and compliance**
   - Privacy-conscious messaging
   - Suitable for HIPAA and PIPEDA-aware deployments
   - Clear disclaimers and medical responsibility language

## Funnel strategy
### Awareness
Goal: educate on the problem of uncertainty and care navigation  
Tactics:
- Educational content on “when to seek urgent vs routine care”
- Short explainer videos and interactive symptom navigation demos
- Thought leadership on ED overcrowding and triage burden

### Consideration
Goal: demonstrate utility and trustworthiness  
Tactics:
- Live demo of the symptom-check triage experience
- Case-based content showing safe routing scenarios
- FAQ pages covering privacy, limitations, escalation, and compliance

### Conversion
Goal: drive product trials, pilots, or integrations  
Tactics:
- Landing pages by persona: patient, clinic, telehealth, administrator
- Demo request CTA with compliance and safety reassurance
- Pilot offer for healthcare systems with outcome tracking

### Retention and advocacy
Goal: sustain usage and generate referrals  
Tactics:
- Usage analytics dashboards for clients
- Monthly insights reports on routing patterns
- Testimonials from clinicians and operational leaders

## Content pillars
- **Care navigation education**
- **Symptom urgency awareness**
- **Emergency red-flag guidance**
- **Healthcare access and flow**
- **Privacy, trust, and compliance**
- **Workflow integration and operational ROI**

## Trust-building requirements
Every public-facing asset should include:
- Non-diagnostic disclaimer
- Emergency escalation language
- Privacy-compliant messaging
- Medical responsibility language
- Clear “seek immediate help” instructions for severe symptoms

## KPI framework
### Top-of-funnel
- Tool engagement rate
- Time spent in triage flow
- Content CTR
- Demo completion rate

### Mid-funnel
- Demo requests
- Pilot sign-ups
- Healthcare partner inquiries
- Repeat usage rates

### Bottom-funnel / business impact
- Reduction in unnecessary ED referrals
- Percentage routed to appropriate non-emergency care
- Patient satisfaction / confidence scores
- Provider adoption and retention
- Cost avoidance estimates

## Recommended campaign themes
1. **“Know where to go, faster”**
2. **“Safe guidance when symptoms are unclear”**
3. **“Reduce ED crowding without compromising safety”**
4. **“Explainable triage for modern care access”**
5. **“Help patients get the right care level the first time”**
