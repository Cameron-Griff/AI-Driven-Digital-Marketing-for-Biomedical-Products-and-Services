# 3) Week-by-Week Content Calendar

| Week | Website Content | Social Media Content | Showroom / Local Channel Content | Primary CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Refresh homepage hero and CTA; publish concise service overview | Intro post: who you are and what you solve; 1 carousel on “why curated furnishing matters” | Update signage/booking card copy; train staff on consultation pitch | Book a Consultation |
| 2 | Add FAQ section covering process and service scope | Reel: showroom or styling walkthrough; story Q&A on design pain points | Invite local clients to visit by appointment | Visit the Showroom |
| 3 | Publish portfolio/gallery entry or project spotlight | Carousel: before/after or vignette transformation; testimonial graphic | Follow up with past leads and referral contacts | Start Your Project |
| 4 | Create lead-capture page: “How to create a cohesive home” checklist | Post checklist teaser; pin inspiration boards on Pinterest | Offer printed checklist at showroom or consultation | Download the Checklist |
| 5 | Update about page to emphasize expertise, trust, and dealer access | Educational post: “3 mistakes that make rooms feel disconnected” | Reach out to trade partners with a collaboration note | Refer a Client |
| 6 | Add service page section on the consultation process | Reel: selecting furnishings with intention; story poll on style preferences | Host a small appointment-only styling event | Book a Consultation |
| 7 | Publish a blog or article on furnishing a cohesive living room | Carousel: “How we simplify sourcing for clients” | Share blog link via email and QR code in showroom | Read the Guide |
| 8 | Optimize contact form and booking flow | Client quote post or project highlight; behind-the-scenes sourcing content | Send follow-up offer to warm leads from past 7 weeks | Schedule a Call |

### Weekly content guidance
- Reuse the same visual assets across website, social, and showroom materials for consistency.
- Keep each post focused on one client problem and one clear benefit.
- Use a polished but simple CTA in every week’s content.
- If French-market support is needed, duplicate only high-value pages and promotional posts first: homepage, services, booking, and top-performing social captions.
