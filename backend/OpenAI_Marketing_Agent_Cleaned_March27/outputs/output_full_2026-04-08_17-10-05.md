# 1) Detailed Marketing Plan

Summary objective
- Primary: Increase online sales of Spikeball sets and bundles.
- Secondary: Grow community engagement and event participation (local tournaments, campus clubs, beach meetups), and build long-term brand affinity.

Key goals (90-day measurable)
- Sales: +20% online revenue for Spikeball sets & bundles vs. prior comparable season.
- Community: +30% event sign-ups and +25% growth in user-generated content (UGC) submissions tagged #SpikeSocial.
- Retention: Improve repeat purchase rate by 10% and increase email-driven revenue by 15%.
- Awareness: Reach 10M impressions across IG/TikTok and add +50k quality followers across platforms.

Priority audiences (ranked)
1. College students (social, campus clubs, intramural organizers) — high virality potential, event hosts.
2. Backyard families & beachgoers — durable, repeat buyers for weekend play.
3. Recreational competitors & community organizers — interested in bundles, durable gear, tournament kits.
4. Event organizers & local community leaders — drive organized play and larger purchases.

Messaging pillars (tone: playful, inclusive, energetic, grassroots)
- Easy play: “Set up in seconds, play anywhere.”
- Community-first: “From backyard pick-up games to sanctioned tournaments — join the movement.”
- Portability + durability: “Designed for travel, built to last across surfaces.”
- Starter clarity: “Curated kits to get you playing confidently.”

Product positioning & offers
- Flagship: Official Spikeball set — portability + durability highlight.
- Bundles: “Starter Pack” (net + ball + guide + carrying case), “Party Pack” (2 nets + multi-ball set), “Event Kit” (bulk nets + referee guide + merch).
- Limited/seasonal: Colorways and college-logo co-branded drops for event sponsorships.
- Pricing & promos: timed bundle discounts, campus referral codes, early-bird event bundles.

Channels & high-level tactics
- E-commerce site: Conversion-optimized product pages (video hero, clear bundles, UGC, 1-click add-ons), localized pages, limited-time banners for events/stock alerts.
- Social: Instagram Reels + Stories and TikTok short-form tutorials, challenges, UGC amplification.
- Email: Lifecycle flows (welcome, cart recovery, browse abandonment, post-purchase onboarding and cross-sell), segmented promos (students, event organizers).
- Community events: Campus tours, local tournaments, pop-ups, sanctioned community leader kits.
- Paid: Mobile-first social ads (Reels and TikTok TopView), retargeting (site visitors, cart abandoners), search (branded + intent).
- Partnerships: Campus reps, micro-influencers, outdoor brands, recreational leagues.
- SEO & content: “How to play” guides, event organizer resources, localized event landing pages.

Creative themes & content types
- How-to / Learn-in-60-seconds: rules, serve types, setup.
- Challenge & UGC: 15–30s montage of trick shots & community clips.
- Bundle demos: “What’s in the starter pack?” unboxing + benefits.
- Event recaps: Highlight local tournaments and fan stories.
- Seasonal lifestyle: Beach, park, tailgate win.

Logistics & seasonality mitigation
- Inventory management: region-specific pools; promote pre-orders where stock constrained.
- Shipping: offer estimated delivery windows, express options in ad copy for last-minute purchases.
- Promotions: off-season focus on indoor multi-surface play, holiday gifting bundles.
- Event timing: cluster events in high-season, but seed smaller indoor events in off-season to maintain engagement.

Measurement & KPIs
- Acquisition: CAC by channel, ROAS for paid social, CPMs, impressions.
- Conversion: CTR, add-to-cart rate, checkout conversion rate, average order value (AOV).
- Engagement: UGC submissions, hashtag mentions, event RSVPs.
- Retention: Repeat purchase rate, email open/click rates, LTV by cohort.
- Event success: attendees per event, ticket-to-sale conversion, local rep activation rate.

Community & retention tactics
- Onboarding series after purchase: setup video, play tips, invite to local events, ask for UGC.
- Loyalty: points for purchases, event attendance, UGC submissions (discounts on replacement balls/parts).
- Ambassador program: campus leads paid/discounted kits in exchange for event activation and content.
- Tournament toolkit: downloadable resources for event organizers (rules, bracket templates, promo assets).

Compliance & legal considerations
- Cookie consent & localization: ensure consent banners and region-based ad/pixel gating.
- Sweepstakes & contests: clear rules, age restrictions, and opt-ins for marketing where contests are used.
- Data protection: follow regional laws (GDPR, CCPA) in forms and targeting.

Assumptions (explicit)
- Budget: Assume a mid-range acquisition budget (e.g., monthly ad spend $50k–$150k across channels). Adjust if actual budget differs.
- Inventory: Assume adequate supply for campaign but limited for some SKUs/colorways; pre-orders acceptable.
- CRM: Assume a standard e-commerce email tool supports segmentation and triggered flows.
- Assets: Assume existing product video assets exist or short-form budget available for quick shoots.
- Event capacity: Assume ability to host/assist small-to-medium local events (50–300 attendees).
- Team: Assume internal marketing + small creative team and access to contractors/influencers.
- Markets: Primary focus on US/Canada English (en-CA) with capability to localize to other major markets if expanded.
If any assumption is false, update strategy priorities (e.g., reduce paid focus if ad budget is lower; emphasize organic/community if inventory limited).

# 2) Platform-Specific Playbooks

E-commerce product pages
- Hero experience: 3–5s autoplay mobile-friendly video showing setup and play; clear bundle selector with comparisons.
- Social proof: prominent UGC carousel, average ratings, and short testimonials (family, college club, tournament).
- Buy path: single-page checkout, bundle presets with add-ons (spare balls, carrying strap), “Buy for event” bulk CTA.
- Localization & consent: language selector, region shipping notice, cookie consent gating before personalized recommendations.
- CRO tests: A/B test hero creative (lifestyle vs. product demo), bundle messaging, urgency banners (limited stock/seasonal).
- KPIs: product page conversion rate, AOV, bundle attach rate.

Instagram (organic + paid)
- Content formats: Reels (primary), Carousels (bundle breakdowns), Stories (polls, UGC reshared), Guides (how-to & local events).
- Playbook:
  - Reels: 15–30s tutorials, challenges, event highlights. Post 3–5x/week.
  - Stories: daily during campaigns; use question stickers for UGC prompts, countdowns to events.
  - Feed: High-quality lifestyle shots, bundle promos, testimonials.
  - Lives: Monthly Q&A or live pickup game during campus activations.
- Influencer strategy: micro-influencers (10–200k) for campus reach; 1–2 macro pushes for national promos.
- Hashtags & UGC: primary #SpikeSocial, secondary #SpikeballChallenge, tag and reshare.
- Paid specs: mobile-first 9:16 reels, 4:5 vertical ads; UGC creative performs best.
- KPIs: reach, saves, shares, reel plays, link clicks.

TikTok
- Content formats: short, authentic, trend-aligned challenges and tutorials (10–30s).
- Playbook:
  - Launch a branded challenge (e.g., #SpikeBounce) that invites trick shots/creative plays with a prize for winners.
  - Seed with influencers and campus ambassadors for viral spread.
  - Post 4–7x/week during campaign peak; engage in duet/stitch with creators.
  - Use native sounds or create a branded sound to improve trend adoption.
- Paid: In-feed ads, TopView push on launch day, Spark ads to amplify creator content.
- KPIs: hashtag views, engagement rate, challenge participation, referral traffic to product pages.

Email marketing
- Lifecycle flows:
  - Welcome series (3 emails): brand story + starter bundle intro + 10% student discount with verification CTA.
  - Cart abandonment (3-step): friendly reminder + social proof + urgency (low stock).
  - Post-purchase (4-step): setup tips + invite to local event + request for review/UGC with incentive.
  - Re-engagement: exclusive event invites, community spotlights.
- Campaign cadence: 1–2 promotional emails/week during campaign; segmentation for students, past purchasers, event RSVPs.
- Personalization: recommended bundles based on browsing, geo-targeted event invites.
- KPIs: open rate, click-to-purchase rate, revenue per email.

Community events & on-ground activation
- Formats: campus demos, beach pop-ups, local tournaments, evening rooftop socials.
- Organizer toolkit: provide branded nets, flyers, social graphics, registration landing page, timing/format templates.
- Activation plan:
  - Seed 10–20 campus ambassadors per region to host weekly pickup games.
  - Run 1 signature city tournament per major market per month during season.
  - Sponsor local rec leagues and provide event kits (discount codes to attendees).
- Promotion: build event landing pages with RSVP, early-bird bundle offers, and post-event recap UGC.
- Measurement: RSVP rate, conversion to purchase, post-event social mentions.

Paid social & search
- Audiences: lookalikes from buyers, interest in outdoor sports/beach activities, college age demographics, event attendees.
- Funnel structure:
  - Top: awareness — short lifestyle reels and TikTok challenge.
  - Mid: consideration — UGC reviews, how-to content, bundle pages.
  - Bottom: retargeting — cart abandoners, viewed product pages, event RSVPs.
- Creative best practices: vertical, first 2 seconds show product in action, captions for sound-off, UGC-forward.
- Bidding & measurement: optimize for purchases (ROAS target) and event sign-ups as secondary conversion.

Influencers & ambassadors
- Tiering:
  - Micro (10k–100k): campus credibility, UGC creation, ambassador program.
  - Mid (100k–1M): trend seeding, challenge launches.
  - Local community leaders: event organization and recruiting.
- Brief: emphasize grassroots story, giveaways, discount code tracking, UGC rights for ads.
- Compensation: product + performance bonus on tracked sales and event sign-ups.

Customer support & post-purchase
- Rapid responses for shipping/parts; clear replacement parts shop and FAQs.
- Post-purchase engagement: community invite in order confirmation and instructional content to reduce returns.
- Warranty & durability messaging: highlight replacement ball program and spare parts.

SEO & content
- Pillars: “How to play Spikeball”, “Best Spikeball sets for X”, “Host a Spikeball tournament” landing pages.
- Local SEO: event landing pages with schema and localized keywords.
- Measurement: organic traffic to product & event pages, search rankings for “Spikeball set” + regional terms.

Compliance & ops
- Cookie consent gating for personalization, and sweeps/contest legal pages per region.
- Event waivers and age-specific rules for tournaments.

Notes & execution details
- Format optimization: all ads and videos are mobile-first vertical (9:16) or 4:5 for IG feed; concise hooks (0–2s) followed by play/action.
- Localization: duplicate key assets for en-CA and other priority markets; local event pages show region-specific shipping/dates.
- Measurement cadence: weekly dashboard for impressions, clicks, purchases, RSVPs; deeper ROAS and LTV review every 2 weeks.
- UGC pipeline: permission request flow in post-purchase emails—collect rights to repurpose for ads (offer small discount).
- Contingency: if inventory constraints appear in Week 3–5, push pre-order messaging and prioritize bundle SKUs for events.
