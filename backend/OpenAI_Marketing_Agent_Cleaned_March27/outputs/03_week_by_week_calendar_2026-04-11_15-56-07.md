# 3) Week-by-Week Content Calendar

Assumptions for the calendar
- Start: Week 1 is campaign kickoff and asset production start.
- Timeline: 12-week initial launch phase.
- Team: Demand gen marketer owns execution, content creator develops assets, SDRs for outreach, AE runs demos, CSM handles onboarding.
- Budget: Example budgets allocated per channel (not shown in table).

| Week | Focus / Goal | Content & Deliverables | Channels | Owner | KPI / Target |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Launch prep & asset production | Sales one-pager (EN + FR), demo script, technical datasheet draft, ROI calculator template, landing page wireframe | Internal, prep for channels | Demand Gen + Content + Product | Ready-to-publish assets (100% complete) |
| 2 | Website & LinkedIn foundation | Landing page with gated demo form + clinician verification; LinkedIn company update; teaser posts | Website, LinkedIn | Content + Dev | Landing page live; LinkedIn teaser + 2 posts |
| 3 | Email sequence + SDR outreach | Email sequences (EN+FR) for clinics & admins; SDR call scripts; enablement training | Email, Phone, LinkedIn | SDR + Sales | Start outreach to 500 clinic contacts; 20 demo bookings target |

| 5 | Paid LinkedIn launch | Launch sponsored content & Lead Gen Forms; A/B test creatives (security vs ROI) | LinkedIn Ads | Demand Gen | 2000 impressions/day; CPL target CAD 250 |

| 12 | Close pilots & onboarding | Convert pilots, begin onboarding; publish pilot outcome teasers + testimonial capture | Email, LinkedIn, Onboarding | CSM + Sales | 3 pilots onboarded; first billing transactions live; produce testimonial draft |

Notes per week and follow-ups
- Ongoing: weekly LinkedIn organic cadence (2 posts/week), paid creative refresh every 2 weeks, SDR follow-ups weekly for warm leads.
- Post-pilot: produce a short case study and 60s testimonial clip by week 14 to feed back into the funnel.
- Localization: translate core assets into French by Week 3 for Quebec-targeted activities starting Week 5.
- Measurement cadence: weekly funnel review meetings and a full performance review at Week 6 and Week 12 to iterate messaging, budget allocation, and target adjustments.
