# 1) Detailed Marketing Plan

Objective
- Primary: Acquire clinician adopters and clinic pilots in the Ottawa/Canada market and build scalable evidence (case studies, ROI data) to accelerate expansion into other English-speaking markets.
- Secondary: Raise brand awareness among lab partners, billing vendors and referral networks to enable integrations and partner-led growth.

Target audience and personas
- Primary: Practicing physicians and clinic administrators (family medicine, internal medicine, urgent care, community clinics).
- Secondary: Patients (as referral/retention messaging), lab partners, billing teams, CME providers.

Core positioning and messaging pillars
- Positioning statement: eHospital centralizes telemedicine, EHR, AI diagnostics and lab integration into a privacy-first orchestration platform that reduces clinician admin time and speeds diagnostics.
- Pillars:
  1. Efficiency: Reduce duplication and admin overhead across telemedicine, labs and billing.
  2. Speed & accuracy: Faster diagnostic turnarounds via AI-assisted workflows + integrated labs.
  3. Trust & compliance: PHIPA/PIPEDA-compatible, secure data residency and clinician-controlled consent.
  4. Clinical enablement: CME, referral network and measurable workflow improvements.

Value propositions (tailored)
- For doctors: Less paperwork, faster diagnostics, referrals that close, and CME credits embedded in workflows.
- For clinic admins: Simplified billing and reconciliations; lower patient call volume.
- For partners (labs/billing): Seamless integration points and revenue/efficiency gains.

Top-line goals & KPIs (90-day / 6-month)
- 90-day: 10 new clinician pilot sign-ups; 3 published case studies/testimonials; 1 conference presence; +30% website leads MoM.
- 6-month: 50 clinician users (active), 3-5 lab integrations live, 2 published ROI whitepapers.
- Metrics: MQLs/SQLs, pilot-to-paid conversion rate, CAC by channel, website conversion rate, demo-to-pilot conversion rate, NPS among clinician users.

Go-to-market tactics (prioritized)
1. Acquire clinician champions via targeted outreach (LinkedIn + curated email sequences) and invite to pilot programs with incentives (discounted pilot, co-authored case study, CME credit).
2. Publish 1-2 high-quality clinician case studies & 1 ROI whitepaper within 6–8 weeks; use as gated assets for lead capture.
3. Website optimization: concise product pages for clinician flows, security & compliance microsite, demo booking CTA, and gated content.
4. LinkedIn: organic thought leadership + sponsored InMail/Lead Gen Forms targeted to Canadian clinicians and clinic admins.
5. Conference play: secure speaking/demo slot at 1 regional medical conference (Ottawa area), host a CME-accredited workshop or panel if possible.
6. Trade publications & PR: pitch 2–3 articles focused on AI-orchestration benefits and privacy-compliant telemedicine.
7. Email nurture: segmented sequences for clinicians, admins and partners with different CTAs (book demo, join pilot, request integration).
8. Referral & partnerships: prioritize local lab partners, billing vendors and medical associations for co-marketing.

Content mix & formats
- High priority: clinician case studies, product demo videos (2–4 min clinician workflow), landing pages, whitepaper (ROI & privacy), clinician testimonial snippets, short explainer animations.
- Lower priority: long-form blogs, patient-facing brochures (only after clinician adoption grows).

Compliance & privacy
- All assets to include data residency and PHIPA/PIPEDA statements. Clinical claims must be evidence-backed; legal/compliance review required before publication. Consent forms required for clinician testimonials and patient stories.

Sales & onboarding alignment
- Create a pilot pack: SLA, success metrics, training schedule, onboarding checklist, and promotional co-brandable assets for clinician champions.
- Sales enablement: one-pagers, demo scripts, objection-handling guide (privacy, workflow change), ROI calculator.

Budget & resource assumptions (see explicit assumptions below)
- Prioritize low-to-mid budget digital channels + 1 regional conference. Invest early in 1 polished demo video, 2 case studies, and a gated whitepaper.

Measurement & reporting cadence
- Weekly marketing dashboard for top funnel metrics; bi-weekly alignment with sales; monthly executive summary with KPIs and next-quarter recommendations.

Assumptions (explicit)
- Marketing automation & CRM are available (e.g., HubSpot, Salesloft) and integrated with analytics.
- Budget: modest but sufficient for sponsored LinkedIn campaigns, 1 conference booth/speaker, professional video production (~small agency rates). If budget differs, tactics should be re-prioritized.
- Access to at least 1–2 clinician champions willing to be profiled for a case study within 6 weeks.
- Legal/compliance review turnaround is 5–10 business days for clinical/PHIPA/PIPEDA copy and testimonial consent.
- Sales cycle median assumed at 3–6 months for clinics; onboarding resources exist for pilot support.
- Native mobile app availability is unknown; assume web-first platform and optimize content/language accordingly.
- Pricing model specifics (subscription vs per‑visit) were not provided; messaging will focus on efficiency/ROI rather than price. Update messaging once pricing is confirmed.

Risks & mitigations
- Risk: clinician skepticism / long evaluation cycles. Mitigate with short, low-risk pilots and clinician references.
- Risk: regulatory scrutiny. Mitigate by featuring data residency and compliance up front, and obtaining endorsements from clinicians/legal advisors.
- Risk: small current user base. Mitigate by aggressively producing and amplifying early success stories and metrics.

Roadmap next 90 days (summary)
- Weeks 0–4: Produce demo video, secure clinician champion(s), draft case study template, build gated whitepaper.
- Weeks 4–8: Launch website updates + gated assets, run initial LinkedIn sponsored campaign, begin clinician email nurture.
- Weeks 8–12: Host webinar/CME session, attend regional conference, publish 2 case studies, refine paid channels based on early CAC.

# 2) Platform-Specific Playbooks

Corporate website (web-first)
- Objective: convert clinician visitors to demo requests/pilot sign-ups; establish compliance credibility.
- Key pages: Homepage (value prop), Clinician landing page (workflows & ROI), Security & Compliance page (PHIPA/PIPEDA/data residency), Resources (case studies, whitepaper, demo video), Contact/Demo CTA.
- Messaging hooks: “Integrated telemedicine + EHR + AI diagnostics for faster, secure clinical decisions”; “Reduce admin time by X (estimate)”.
- CTAs: Book a demo, Request pilot, Download whitepaper (gated), Watch demo (lightgate).
- SEO: target keywords: telemedicine EHR Canada, AI diagnostics for clinicians, PHIPA-compliant telemedicine, clinic workflow automation.
- CRO: single-column demo form, clinician field (specialty), clinic size, scheduling widget (Calendly).
- Metrics: demo requests, gated downloads, time on page, bounce rate, conversion rate.
- Compliance: privacy policy and consent checkbox before downloading case study/testimonial.

LinkedIn (organic + paid)
- Objective: generate clinician leads, build thought leadership, recruit pilot participants.
- Targeting: Canadian physicians (by specialty), clinic administrators, practice managers; target Ottawa & surrounding regions for initial pushes.
- Organic content cadence: 3 posts/week — 1 thought leadership (AI+orchestration), 1 case study snippet, 1 product demo clip or testimonial.
- Paid play: Sponsored Content + Lead Gen Forms, InMail to clinic admins/physicians for pilot invites.
- Ad creative: short video (30–60s demo), carousel with case study highlights, single-image with strong CTA.
- Messaging examples: “Reduce follow-up admin by X—see how Dr. [Name] cut lab follow-up time in half.” / “PHIPA-compliant telemedicine that connects directly to labs & billing.”
- Metrics: CPL (cost per lead), form fill rate, click-through rate, demo-to-pilot conversion.
- Compliance: avoid clinical outcome promises without evidence; include data residency note in ad copy where possible.

Targeted emails (segmented sequences)
- Objective: nurture leads into pilots/demos; re-engage trial users.
- Segments: cold clinician prospects, inbound website leads, trial users, clinic admins, lab partners.
- Sequence examples:
  - Cold outreach: 5-touch over 3 weeks — Intro (value + short case study), Pain/ROI proof point, Invite to 15-min demo, Social proof/testimonial, Final offer to join pilot (limited slots).
  - Inbound nurture: immediate thank-you + gated asset, 1-week follow-up with demo invite, 3-week check-in with case study, monthly newsletter.
  - Trial nurture: product tips, onboarding checklist, invite to clinician community webinar, pilot success story.
- Personalization: specialty, clinic size, pain point (billing vs diagnostics).
- Metrics: open rate, reply rate, meetings booked, unsubscribe rate.
- Compliance: ensure CASL/PIPEDA compliance for email marketing in Canada; maintain opt-in/consent records.

Medical conferences & events
- Objective: secure pilots, build local brand credibility, network partners.
- Tactics:
  - Pre-event: targeted email/LinkedIn invites to book booth demos; advertise a CME-accredited workshop or demo session.
  - Booth: live demo station, clinician testimonial video, printed one-pager (compliance summary), sign-up tablet for demos/pilots, clinician lounge or demo appointment calendar.
  - Session: present clinical workflow case study or panel with clinician champion; promote pilot incentive.
  - Post-event: follow-up email within 48 hours, publish event recap + snippets on LinkedIn, target attendees with paid retargeting.
- Metrics: leads collected, demos booked from event, pilot conversions, PR mentions.
- Compliance: ensure any CME claims are valid and approved; get testimonial consent for on-site recordings.

Clinician trade publications & PR
- Objective: build credibility and reach clinicians via trusted editorial channels.
- Content types: bylined articles (clinical workflow improvements), evidence-backed whitepapers, case study summaries, press releases for pilot launches/integrations.
- Pitch angles: AI-orchestration in primary care, privacy-first telemedicine, real-world pilot results (time to diagnosis, admin reduction).
- Outreach: target Canadian clinician journals and associations; offer an exclusive case study or authored piece from a clinician champion.
- Metrics: article placements, referral traffic, inbound demo requests.
- Compliance: clinical claims must be evidence-backed and approved by legal/compliance.

Video & demo play
- Objective: reduce demo friction, show clinician workflow clearly.
- Assets: 2–4 minute product demo, 30–60s social cuts, clinician testimonial clips (15–30s), explainer animation for compliance/orchestration benefits.
- Distribution: website hero, LinkedIn posts/ads, email nurture, conference booth plays.
- Metrics: watch-through rate, demo link clicks, demo-to-pilot conversion.

Partner & referral playbook
- Objective: accelerate integrations and referral-based adoption via labs, billing vendors and medical associations.
- Tactics: co-branded whitepapers, technical integration guides, revenue share or referral incentives for labs, joint webinars.
- Metrics: partner referrals, integrations completed, co-marketing leads.

Measurement framework
- Core dashboard: sessions, leads, MQLs/SQLs, demo requests, pilot starts, CAC by channel, conversion timeline.
- Reporting cadence: weekly top-of-funnel, biweekly pipeline review with sales, monthly exec summary.
