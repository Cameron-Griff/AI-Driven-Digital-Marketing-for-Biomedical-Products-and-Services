- 8 hero short-form videos (15–30s), 4 long-form product demo clips (30–60s), UGC consent forms, event sign-up/ticketing pages, ambassador toolkit PDF, photography for PDP and hero images.

Measurement cadence
- Daily: paid spend & ad creative metrics.
- Weekly: traffic, add-to-cart, event RSVPs.
- Monthly: revenue, repeat purchase rate, tournament registrations progress.

Adjustments & next steps
- After Week 8: analyze CAC by channel, scale top-performing ads, expand creator seeding to more regions, deploy membership program pilot, and open Q4 limited-run colorway drop to drive urgency.
