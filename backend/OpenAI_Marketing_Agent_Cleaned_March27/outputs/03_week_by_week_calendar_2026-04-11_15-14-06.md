# 3) Week-by-Week Content Calendar

Assumptions for calendar: 8-week launch/scale window; marketing team and modest paid budget available; conference attendance scheduled in Week 6. Weeks numbered W1–W8.

| Week | Theme / Focus | Assets & Activities | Channels | Owner | KPI |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| W1 | Launch core assets | Publish refreshed product page + hero explainer video; upload one‑pager; enable demo scheduler; create email nurture templates; set up LinkedIn campaign structure & tracking | Website, Email, LinkedIn | Product Marketer / Content | Page live; demo scheduler functional |
| W2 | Lead gen push (Admins) | Send Admin nurture email 1 (pilot invite); LinkedIn Sponsored Content #1; Organic post with early-adopter stat (150+ doctors) | Email, LinkedIn | Demand Gen | Email open ≥25%; 50 demo signups target |
| W3 | Clinician champions | Send Physicians email 1 (short demo invite + 2-min clinician testimonial video); Publish demo walkthrough (6–8 min) on site; Start retargeting visitors with video clips | Email, Website, LinkedIn Ads | Sales Engineer / Content | Demo bookings from physicians ≥40 |
| W4 | Trust & compliance week | Publish Security & Privacy one‑pager and FAQ update; Email to admins: compliance-focused; LinkedIn thought piece on Canadian privacy in telehealth | Website, Email, LinkedIn | Legal + Product Marketing | Downloaded security one‑pager ≥30; reduced compliance objections |
| W5 | Case study & ROI | Release first anonymized case study (from pilot clinics); Send case-study email to nurture lists; LinkedIn testimonial post; update product page with case metrics | Website, Email, LinkedIn | Customer Success / Content | Case study downloads ≥60; demo conversion lift +10% |
| W6 | Conference activation | Pre-event email to registrants + LinkedIn event posts; booth live demos; collect leads; run live micro-demo sessions onsite; record short interviews with clinicians on-site | Conference, Email, LinkedIn | Events Lead / Sales | Leads from event ≥80; demo demos scheduled post-event |
| W7 | Partner outreach sprint | Send partner outreach sequence (3-touch): intro → benefits/case study → invite to partner webinar; host partner webinar showcasing integrations | Email, Webinar, LinkedIn | Partnerships | Partner pilot commitments ≥3; webinar attendees ≥40 |
| W8 | Scale & optimize | Analyze funnel: iterate email copy, landing page CTAs, and LinkedIn creatives; prioritize high-performing segments; push final pilot sign-up email (scarcity) | Website, Email, LinkedIn | All teams | Total demo requests (90 days) ≥150; conversion metrics updated |

Notes and execution tips
- Weekly content cadence: at least 1 email and 2 LinkedIn posts per week; refresh creative every 2 weeks based on performance.
- Use retargeting: visitors who view demo walkthrough get tailored email within 48 hours.
- Capture metrics in CRM and run weekly funnel reviews; prepare one case study during pilot windows (W3–W5).
- Conference follow-up: within 48 hours post-event send personalized email + recorded demo to all leads.
- Scarcity tactic: close pilot enrollment with limited pilot slots in W8 to drive urgency.

- Admin nurture schedule: Intro (W2) → Case study (W5) → Compliance deep dive (W4) → Pilot reminder (W8).
- Physician schedule: Intro demo (W3) → Testimonial (W3) → Clinical workflow demo (W3–W4) → Final CTA (W6 post-conference).
