# 1) Detailed Marketing Plan

Overview
- Objective: Increase patient access and engagement while streamlining clinician workflows and accelerating diagnostics turnaround for a Canadian patient‑centric digital health platform (telemedicine + EHR + AI diagnostics + lab integration).
- Primary outcomes to drive: new patient activations (authenticated users booking care), active patient engagement (repeat visits, labs ordered), clinician onboarding/usage (appointments per clinician, chart completion), and diagnostics TAT improvement (tracked via lab integration).

Key strategic pillars
1. Trust & Clinical Credibility — lead messaging with security, privacy, clinical endorsements and transparent AI explainability.
2. Accessibility & Convenience — emphasize fast appointment access, consolidated records and integrated labs (single workflow).
3. Clinician Enablement — show clinician productivity gains and clinical safety; reduce perceived adoption friction.
4. Measurable Proof — use case studies, pilot metrics (TAT, clinician time savings), and testimonials to move prospects from consideration to conversion.
5. Targeted Multi‑channel Activation — mix owned (website, app), earned (clinician partnerships, conferences), and paid/targeted outreach (LinkedIn ads, email nurtures).

Core messaging (tone: modern, professional, empathetic, security‑focused)
- Patient headline: "Virtual care that keeps your records, labs and clinician in one secure place — fast, private, and truly personal."
- Clinician headline: "Cut documentation time, speed diagnostics, and focus on care — AI‑assisted workflows built for clinical safety."
- Security sub‑messaging: PHIPA/PIPEDA compliance, secure storage, explicit consent flows, regular audits.
- AI sub‑messaging: Augmentation not replacement; explainability, clinician oversight, validated diagnostic performance.

Target audiences & tailored objectives
- Patients (primary): Drive authenticated signups, first consult bookings, lab orders. Objections: privacy, AI trust, billing clarity.
- Clinicians (secondary): Recruit clinician champions, drive active usage and referrals. Objections: workflow disruption, liability, EHR duplication.
- Clinic admins & lab partners: Integrations, revenue share, operational improvements.

Assumptions (explicit)
- Budget: Not specified → assume a modest pilot marketing budget of CAD 50k–100k over 3 months for content production, targeted ads and conference presence.
- Full feature set & integrations work in pilot scope; no major product freezes expected.
- Regulatory compliance: Canadian standards (PIPEDA/PHIPA) assumed satisfied; any non‑Canadian expansion is secondary and will require local review.
- Team capacity: Core marketing team with 1 PM, 1 content producer, 1 growth marketer, 1 clinical liaison; external agency support for video and design.
- Analytics: GA4 (or equivalent), event tracking and a CRM are available for conversion tracking. If not available, one must be implemented before campaign start.

Tactics & deliverables
- Website & Landing pages: Patient-facing landing with hero value props, security badges, demo video, FAQ (privacy/AI), and clear CTAs (Book a free consult, Request demo). Clinician landing for case studies, ROI calculator, and sign up for pilot.
- Video: 60–90s explainer; 2–3× 30–45s social cuts; clinician testimonial video.
- Case studies & data sheets: Pilot metrics (e.g., average diagnostic TAT reduction, clinician time saved) — anonymized and consented.
- Email flows: Welcome series, nurture series (patients + clinicians), re‑engagement drip for inactive users.
- LinkedIn: Thought leadership posts, targeted Sponsored Content to clinicians and clinic admins, and recruiting ads for clinician pilot.
- App store optimization: Clear benefits, screenshots demonstrating workflow and security, short promo video.
- Clinician outreach: Webinars, live demo sessions, peer champion referrals, ROI calculator and CME‑style content if feasible.
- Conferences: Booth with demo stations, QR codes to book live consults, clinician panel participation and speakerships.
- Partnerships: Lab networks, primary care clinics, patient advocacy groups for co‑marketing.

Measurement & KPIs
- Acquisition: Website visits, landing conversion rate (visitor → authenticated signup), paid CTR/CPA.
- Activation: Authenticated patient activation rate (signup → first appointment), time to first appointment.
- Engagement: Repeat visits per patient, average episodes per patient, lab orders per patient.
- Clinician adoption: % of pilot clinicians actively using platform weekly, appointments per clinician, documentation time saved (self‑reported + system logs).
- Diagnostics TAT: Average time lab ordered → result available in platform.
- Safety & retention: NPS/CSAT, churn of clinicians and patients.
- ROI: Cost per activation, lifetime value (LTV) estimates once data allow.

Channel budget allocation (assumed CAD 50k–100k over 3 months)
- Content production (video, case studies, landing pages): 25–35%
- Paid social (LinkedIn targeted to clinicians/admins) & search: 25–35%
- Conferences and events: 20–25% (booth, travel, collateral)
- Email & CRM tooling, analytics: 5–10%
- Contingency & agency fees: 5–10%

Timeline & phased approach (12‑week pilot marketing sprint)
- Weeks 1–3: Core assets (landing pages, explainer video, case study drafts, analytics setup), clinician onboarding collateral.
- Weeks 4–8: Launch patient acquisition (organic + paid), clinician webinars, app store listings live, begin email nurtures.
- Weeks 9–12: Scale paid channels by top performers, conference activations, publish case study results and early metrics, iterate.

Risks & mitigation
- Privacy concerns: Use clear consent flows, third‑party audits, visible privacy statements and security badges.
- AI skepticism: Publish model validation summaries, clinician oversight workflows, and limit claims to augmentation.
- Clinician adoption: Offer onboarding support, reduce double‑entry via EHR integrations, provide measurable clinician time savings.
- Regulatory expansion risk: Avoid country-specific claims beyond pilot countries; prepare localization plan.

Quick experiments to prioritize (A/B test candidates)
- Landing page hero messaging: security‑first vs convenience‑first.
- CTA wording: Book consult vs Start free trial vs Request demo.
- Email subject lines: clinical credibility lead vs convenience lead.
- LinkedIn audiences: practicing physicians vs clinic admins for clinician conversion.

Governance & approvals
- All patient materials and testimonials must have documented consent.
- Clinical claims reviewed by medical lead/legal for compliance.
