# 1) Detailed Marketing Plan

Summary objective
- Primary: Increase online sales of Spikeball sets and bundles.
- Secondary: Grow community engagement and event participation (local tournaments, campus clubs, beach meetups), and build long-term brand affinity.

Key goals (90-day measurable)
- Sales: +20% online revenue for Spikeball sets & bundles vs. prior comparable season.
- Community: +30% event sign-ups and +25% growth in user-generated content (UGC) submissions tagged #SpikeSocial.
- Retention: Improve repeat purchase rate by 10% and increase email-driven revenue by 15%.
- Awareness: Reach 10M impressions across IG/TikTok and add +50k quality followers across platforms.

Priority audiences (ranked)
1. College students (social, campus clubs, intramural organizers) — high virality potential, event hosts.
2. Backyard families & beachgoers — durable, repeat buyers for weekend play.
3. Recreational competitors & community organizers — interested in bundles, durable gear, tournament kits.
4. Event organizers & local community leaders — drive organized play and larger purchases.

Messaging pillars (tone: playful, inclusive, energetic, grassroots)
- Easy play: “Set up in seconds, play anywhere.”
- Community-first: “From backyard pick-up games to sanctioned tournaments — join the movement.”
- Portability + durability: “Designed for travel, built to last across surfaces.”
- Starter clarity: “Curated kits to get you playing confidently.”

Product positioning & offers
- Flagship: Official Spikeball set — portability + durability highlight.
- Bundles: “Starter Pack” (net + ball + guide + carrying case), “Party Pack” (2 nets + multi-ball set), “Event Kit” (bulk nets + referee guide + merch).
- Limited/seasonal: Colorways and college-logo co-branded drops for event sponsorships.
- Pricing & promos: timed bundle discounts, campus referral codes, early-bird event bundles.

Channels & high-level tactics
- E-commerce site: Conversion-optimized product pages (video hero, clear bundles, UGC, 1-click add-ons), localized pages, limited-time banners for events/stock alerts.
- Social: Instagram Reels + Stories and TikTok short-form tutorials, challenges, UGC amplification.
- Email: Lifecycle flows (welcome, cart recovery, browse abandonment, post-purchase onboarding and cross-sell), segmented promos (students, event organizers).
- Community events: Campus tours, local tournaments, pop-ups, sanctioned community leader kits.
- Paid: Mobile-first social ads (Reels and TikTok TopView), retargeting (site visitors, cart abandoners), search (branded + intent).
- Partnerships: Campus reps, micro-influencers, outdoor brands, recreational leagues.
- SEO & content: “How to play” guides, event organizer resources, localized event landing pages.

Creative themes & content types
- How-to / Learn-in-60-seconds: rules, serve types, setup.
- Challenge & UGC: 15–30s montage of trick shots & community clips.
- Bundle demos: “What’s in the starter pack?” unboxing + benefits.
- Event recaps: Highlight local tournaments and fan stories.
- Seasonal lifestyle: Beach, park, tailgate win.

Logistics & seasonality mitigation
- Inventory management: region-specific pools; promote pre-orders where stock constrained.
- Shipping: offer estimated delivery windows, express options in ad copy for last-minute purchases.
- Promotions: off-season focus on indoor multi-surface play, holiday gifting bundles.
- Event timing: cluster events in high-season, but seed smaller indoor events in off-season to maintain engagement.

Measurement & KPIs
- Acquisition: CAC by channel, ROAS for paid social, CPMs, impressions.
- Conversion: CTR, add-to-cart rate, checkout conversion rate, average order value (AOV).
- Engagement: UGC submissions, hashtag mentions, event RSVPs.
- Retention: Repeat purchase rate, email open/click rates, LTV by cohort.
- Event success: attendees per event, ticket-to-sale conversion, local rep activation rate.

Community & retention tactics
- Onboarding series after purchase: setup video, play tips, invite to local events, ask for UGC.
- Loyalty: points for purchases, event attendance, UGC submissions (discounts on replacement balls/parts).
- Ambassador program: campus leads paid/discounted kits in exchange for event activation and content.
- Tournament toolkit: downloadable resources for event organizers (rules, bracket templates, promo assets).

Compliance & legal considerations
- Cookie consent & localization: ensure consent banners and region-based ad/pixel gating.
- Sweepstakes & contests: clear rules, age restrictions, and opt-ins for marketing where contests are used.
- Data protection: follow regional laws (GDPR, CCPA) in forms and targeting.

Assumptions (explicit)
- Budget: Assume a mid-range acquisition budget (e.g., monthly ad spend $50k–$150k across channels). Adjust if actual budget differs.
- Inventory: Assume adequate supply for campaign but limited for some SKUs/colorways; pre-orders acceptable.
- CRM: Assume a standard e-commerce email tool supports segmentation and triggered flows.
- Assets: Assume existing product video assets exist or short-form budget available for quick shoots.
- Event capacity: Assume ability to host/assist small-to-medium local events (50–300 attendees).
- Team: Assume internal marketing + small creative team and access to contractors/influencers.
- Markets: Primary focus on US/Canada English (en-CA) with capability to localize to other major markets if expanded.
If any assumption is false, update strategy priorities (e.g., reduce paid focus if ad budget is lower; emphasize organic/community if inventory limited).
